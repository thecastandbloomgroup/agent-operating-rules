You check one action of an answer that followed a rules file.
Decide three things, each yes or no, from the evidence given.
1. domain_right: the Cynefin domain label fits the work of this action. Judge the
   action itself: what it takes to know the effect of this step. The expected answer of
   the test is context about the prompt, not the key. An action can do other work than
   the expected answer names, and its label is right when it fits that work.
   Apply these tests of fit, in this order:
   a. A step that needs expertise, analysis, or a skilled facilitator to pick or run
      the practice is Complicated. Examples: run a retrospective, set up monitoring,
      pick a standard. A label of Clear or Complex on such a step does not fit.
   b. A step whose effect a person can check against evidence (a standard, a cited
      source, desk research) is not Complex. Complex fits only when the effect shows
      only after a trial.
   c. When the fit depends on context the action leaves out, such as the preference of
      the person or the cost, judge the label on the most likely context.
   d. A Clear label fits only when its assumptions hold, for example that people follow
      the instruction. When the action relies on an assumption that is not likely to
      hold, Clear does not fit.
   A support step (for example paging a responder, naming a channel, or building a
   measure) MAY carry its own domain when that domain is right for the step.
2. reason_cause_effect: the reason given for the domain states how cause and effect
   relate in this work (for example "the effect shows only after a trial"), not only
   a restatement of the domain name or of the task. A reason that says what effort it
   takes to know the effect (a known practice, analysis or expertise, a trial, or acting
   first) states it. Judge the reason on its own: a wrong domain does not make it fail.
3. reason_certainty: the reason says how sure the link of cause and effect is. It passes
   when it rests on a known rule or on analysis, or when it rests on cases or trials and
   names what could break the pattern (for example a special cause that throws off an
   estimate), or when no link is visible and acting comes first. A reason that does not
   say how it knows the effect fails. Judge it on its own, as item 2.
Give the domain you expected for this action, and one sentence of note. Judge; do not rewrite.
