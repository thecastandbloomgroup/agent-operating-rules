## Headline

Start a short crisis process now: name one coordinator, preserve records, verify the disputed claim, and approve one response.

- Separate the journalist’s allegation from facts your team can prove.
- Give Legal, PR, and the board distinct roles and one decision channel.
- Stop further use of the disputed claim until you check it.

## Background

A journalist alleges that yesterday’s CEO statement was false. Legal, PR, and the board are contacting you without a shared plan. [1]

The information provided does not establish whether the statement was false or whether the CEO knew it was false. [1]

## Relevance

Your first task is to control decisions, not defend the CEO or accept the allegation. [judgment]  
Therefore, use a short, documented process before anyone gives a substantive public response. [judgment]

## Information

**Keep these questions separate:**
- What exactly did the CEO say?
- What evidence supports or contradicts that statement?
- What did the CEO know at the time?
- Who relied on the statement, and what needs correction?

These questions require separate evidence; an inaccurate statement does not alone establish intent. [judgment]

**Assumption:** You can convene the relevant leaders. **Why not measured:** The material does not state it. **Validate by:** Confirm authority with the board chair. **If wrong:** Ask the chair to appoint the coordinator.

**Alternative considered:** Issue an immediate denial or apology. It loses because the supplied information supports neither response yet. [judgment]

The time limits below are proposed operating targets, not legal deadlines. [judgment]

> “We are reviewing the statement and the records behind it. We will provide an update by [time].”

Use that wording only after the review starts and someone owns the promised update.

If the statement is wrong, correct the specific error and explain the practical remedy. Do not speculate about intent.

If records support the statement, provide the relevant evidence and address the journalist’s specific claim.
