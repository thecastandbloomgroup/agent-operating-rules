## End

I recommend that we take these steps, in this order:

1. Stabilise. Send one message to legal, PR, and the board within the hour: "no external statements until this group agrees on one reply." Designate one spokesperson and one facts owner. This is a Chaotic action: no cause and effect is visible while three parties act independently, so containment comes first. Kind: deterministic.

3. Verify the claim. The facts owner compares the CEO's statement against primary records and obtains the journalist's evidence list. Deliverable: a one page memo stating "statement supported" or "statement false, here is the correction." Kind: evaluative, Class Complicated: analysis by legal plus subject experts picks the answer. Rubric first: accuracy of factual claims (60%), context omitted (20%), materiality to stakeholders (20%), each scored against the primary records, threshold: any factual error on a material claim triggers Step 4's correction path. This step MUST NOT be skipped even under time pressure.

4. Agree and deliver one response. After Step 3, the three parties approve one reply: correction if the statement was false, evidence based rebuttal if it was accurate. Kind: evaluative, judged against the Step 3 memo. Domain: Complicated. Step 4 MUST NOT start until the memo in Step 3 is complete.

5. Probe reputation impact in parallel with Steps 3 and 4. Monitor press pickup and stakeholder messages for 48 hours, capped at two staff. Stop condition: no new coverage for 24 hours. Domain: Complex: cause and effect appear only afterward, so run a capped probe, leverage is a guess.

If the journalist's evidence turns out to be fabricated, replace Step 4's correction with a formal retraction demand per counsel.
