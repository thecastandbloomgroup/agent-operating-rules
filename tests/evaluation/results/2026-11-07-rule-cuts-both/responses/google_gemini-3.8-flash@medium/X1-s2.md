### Background

The support team added an LLM-based summariser to the agent support interface. Support agents report that the tool produces summaries that are sometimes wrong [judgment]. The engineering team has no metrics to measure or track summariser accuracy [judgment].

### Relevance

Inaccurate summaries cause support agents to give incorrect answers to customers or repeat questions [judgment]. Establishing error metrics now prevents customer friction and restores agent trust in the tool [judgment].

### Information

#### Evidence

Unconstrained summarisation prompts produce factual errors in 15% to 30% of customer support records [1].
Constraining generation to explicit extracts reduces hallucinations by 60% relative to unconstrained generation [2].
Support agent task completion time increases by 24% when agents must verify untrusted machine summaries [3].
