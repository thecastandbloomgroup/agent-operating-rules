## Headline

Freeze external messaging within two hours, run one privileged fact review, and answer legal, PR, and the board with a single written packet. Supporting points:

1. The alignment problem is internal, so one spokesperson and one channel stop the drift today.
2. No public verdict on the statement should go out before the evidence review is done, because a wrong public verdict is hard to retract. [judgment]
3. The review verdict chooses between two paths: a corrective statement or a documented correction demand to the outlet.

## Background

A journalist published that the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are messaging you at once, and nobody is aligned. You need a sequence that stabilizes today and sets up one decision tomorrow.

## Relevance

The first 24 hours set the facts on record, and staff on three teams act on whatever arrives first. A shared packet prevents three different company positions by Friday. [judgment]

## Information

**Evidence and analysis**

- Response path selection depends on the verdict of an evidence review, so that review has to come before any external narrative. [judgment]
- A holding line that states only "we are reviewing" is safe to issue because it commits to no verdict. [judgment]
- Parallel internal messaging produces competing drafts that must be reconciled later, which costs more time than one packet now. [judgment]

**Assumption log**

A1. The journalist's claim is not yet verified internally. Why not measured: the request does not state that anyone compared the statement to the underlying evidence. Validate by: a privileged side-by-side review of the statement text, the source data, and the article. If wrong: the path in Step 4 changes from a corrective statement to a correction demand.
A2. The CEO statement exists in writing, with a transcript or text available. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: the GC or a delegate pulls the transcript and release within one day. If wrong: reconstruct from recordings first, and add one day to the schedule.
A3. No regulatory disclosure duty is triggered by this event. Why not measured: no tool can reach it, and the company type is not stated. Validate by: ask outside counsel at the start of Step 1. If wrong: disclosure timing and wording override the PR sequence in Sections End Steps 1 to 3.

**Alternative considered**

Answer legal, PR, and the board separately and immediately. It loses: three unaligned answers create three company positions to retract, and one packet is one increment instead of three. [judgment]

## End

I recommend that we do the following, in this order.

1. **Stabilize the channels (Chaotic).** Within 2 hours, name one spokesperson, issue a 3-sentence holding statement, and set the routing rule that all queries go to that person. This removes the blocker of parallel public and internal messages. Test (deterministic), written first: Given any inbound press query, When an employee other than the spokesperson answers, Then the answer is the holding line plus a routing note, and the log shows zero rogue replies.
2. **Fact review (Confused).** Within 24 hours, the GC or a delegate runs a privileged side-by-side review of the statement, the source data, and the article. Its output classifies the next steps, so it comes before the response decision. Test (evaluative), rubric first: one row per material claim in the statement; criteria are evidence for, evidence against, and verdict, weighted by materiality; pass threshold is two reviewers reaching the same verdict; the evidence each criterion needs is a dated document or recording. [15]
3. **One packet (Clear).** Within 36 hours, send a 2-page brief with the verdict and the two response options to legal, PR, and the board. This removes the blocker of three teams acting on private views. Test (deterministic): Given the packet is sent, When a stakeholder asks for status, Then the reply cites the packet section and not a private message.
4. **Publish the chosen response (Complicated).** Within 72 hours, release either the corrective statement or the documented correction demand to the outlet. Pick the practice by analysis of the review verdict, per the packet. This adds the capability to speak with one verified voice. This step MUST NOT start until the test in Step 2 passes and the packet in Step 3 is sent. Test (evaluative), rubric first: accuracy against the review, legal sign-off recorded, and one named speaker; pass threshold is all three criteria met; evidence is the signed draft and the review document. [15] [18]

If the journalist holds further unpublished claims, add a second-wave plan before Step 4. If the CEO's statement text cannot be reconstructed by the Step 2 deadline, extend the schedule by one day and keep the holding line in place. [judgment]
