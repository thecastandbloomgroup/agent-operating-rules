## Headline

Find out which tests and jobs fail at random before you change any code.

- The report gives no logs, so the cause is unknown today.
- Past studies show most flaky failures come from a few known causes, so a sorted list of failures will find the cause.
- A fix is safe only after you can measure the failure rate before and after it.

## Background

A flaky build passes and fails on the same code. The request names the symptom and gives no logs, tool names, or failure rate. The next step is to measure the problem and then fix it.

## Relevance

Each random failure costs a rerun and lowers trust in the build. People then ignore real failures, so defects reach production.

## Information

Evidence:

- In a study of fixes to flaky tests, 45% came from async waits. Another 20% came from concurrency. Another 12% came from test order. That leaves 23% for other causes.

- Non-deterministic tests are best handled by isolating them and then removing the non-determinism.
