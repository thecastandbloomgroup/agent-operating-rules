# Headline

Pause public statements, put one person in charge, check the facts, then release one response.

- **Control:** In the next hour, one call names one person who decides and pauses all public statements.
- **Facts:** Counsel checks the CEO's statement against the article and internal records before anyone replies in public.
- **Response:** The fact check and the legal duties together decide if the company corrects, contests, or does both.

# Background

A journalist reported that a statement by the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) yesterday was false. Legal, public relations (PR), and the board each send you different directions. You need the next steps.

# Relevance

Each hour without one voice raises the risk of a second false or conflicting statement [judgment]. Any public reply also becomes part of the legal record [judgment].

# Information

**Evidence**

1. There are no shared facts and no single person who decides. In this state, the first task is to restore order, and then to sense and respond.
[1]

2. The right response depends on how responsible the company is. Situational Crisis Communication Theory (SCCT) links a preventable crisis to rebuild responses, that is, apology and corrective action.
[2]

3. Under U.S. law, legal privilege covers what employees tell company counsel when counsel gives legal advice. Legal privilege is protection from forced disclosure. Therefore counsel should lead the fact check.
[3]

4. If the company is listed in the U.S., an untrue statement of material fact in connection with securities trades is unlawful.
[4]

5. Regulation Fair Disclosure (Regulation FD) applies when a company discloses material nonpublic facts to selected investors or analysts. The company must then publish those facts. If the disclosure was intentional, the company must publish at the same time. If it was not intentional, the deadline is 24 hours or the next trading day's opening on the New York Stock Exchange. The later of the two applies.
[5]

6. Steps 1 to 3 in the End section stay the same for every result of the fact check. My confidence here is high. The basis is that no step depends on whether the statement was true [judgment].

**Response options by fact-check result**

| Fact-check result | Response | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| False | The CEO corrects the statement in public and states what changes | [2] |
| True | Send records to the publication and ask it to correct the article | [judgment] |
| Partly false | Correct the false part and show records for the rest | [judgment] |
| Not resolved by the deadline | Publish a new holding line with a new update time | [judgment] |

**Assumption log**

A1. The company is listed on a U.S. stock exchange. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Ask counsel, or check for a stock ticker. If wrong: Remove the securities items from Step 3.

A2. You have the authority to call the CEO, the General Counsel, the PR lead, and the board chair together. Why not measured: The material does not state your role. Validate by: Ask the board chair to confirm in one message. If wrong: The board chair runs Step 1, and you prepare the agenda.

A3. Nobody yet knows if the CEO's statement was false. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Step 2. If wrong: Use the known result in Step 4, and still complete Steps 1 and 3.

A4. Nobody yet suspects that the CEO knew the statement was false. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Ask counsel and the board chair during the Step 1 call. If wrong: Independent directors and outside counsel run Steps 2 to 4.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is to rebut the article in public today, before the fact check. It loses because a rebuttal that proves wrong adds a second false statement [judgment]. In addition, SCCT matches denial only to rumors and unfounded challenges, not to preventable crises.
[2]

Full silence also loses. The holding line shows that the company is acting, and it commits the company to no position [judgment].

# End

I recommend that we take these five steps in this order.

**Step 1. Stabilise in the next hour.**

- **Increment:**
  - Hold one call with the CEO, the General Counsel, the PR lead, and the board chair.
  - Name one person who approves every external statement.
  - Name one channel for all internal messages on this topic.
  - Pause all public statements, including the CEO's social media accounts.
  - Allow only a holding line, that is, a short statement that promises an update. Example: "We have seen the report. We are reviewing the facts and will update by 18:00 today."
  - Ask counsel to send staff a notice to keep all related records.
- **Removes:** three groups that give conflicting directions.
- **Kind:** deterministic. Write this test before the call:

```text
Given the Step 1 call has ended
When any executive asks who approves an external statement
Then the written log names one person
And no public statement except the holding line has been released
And counsel has sent the notice to keep records
```

- **Cynefin domain (how cause and effect relate in the work):** Chaotic. Act first to restore order.
[1]

**Step 2. Check the facts today.**

Step 2 MUST NOT start until the Step 1 test passes.

- **Increment:**
  - Counsel leads the check, so legal privilege covers the work.
  [3]
  - Two reviewers list each claim in the CEO's statement and each claim in the article.
  - They match each claim to internal records.
  - They mark each claim true, false, or unverified.
- **Removes:** the unknown truth of the statement. While it is unknown, nobody can choose a response.
- **Kind:** evaluative. Write this rubric before the check. Score each criterion as the share of claims that meet it.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Each mark has a record behind it | 50% | A document, a data extract, or a named witness |
| Both reviewers agree on each mark | 30% | Both names on the claim sheet |
| Counsel approves the summary | 20% | A signed summary |

The check passes at a total of 80% or more, with the first criterion at 100%.

- **Cynefin domain:** Confused. This step collects what the team needs to classify the rest of the work.

**Step 3. Check legal duties today, in parallel with Step 2.**

Step 3 MAY start at the same time as Step 2. Counsel MUST NOT issue a final answer until the Step 2 rubric passes.

- **Increment:** Counsel lists each rule that applies. For each rule, counsel states if it requires a correction, and by when. If A1 holds, the list includes these items:
  - The rule against untrue statements of material fact in securities trades.
  [4]
  - Regulation FD, which controls any contact with investors or analysts.
  [5]
  - A pause on share trades by insiders until the facts are known [judgment].
  - Contract, regulator, and defamation duties, which apply in every case [judgment].
- **Removes:** unknown legal exposure. The legal exposure sets the form and the deadline of the response.
- **Kind:** deterministic. Write this test first:

```text
Given the list of rules that apply
When counsel reviews each rule
Then each rule has a yes or no answer, a deadline, and a cited source
And no executive has spoken alone with an investor or analyst
```

- **Cynefin domain:** Complicated. Legal analysis picks the practice.

**Step 4. Choose one response.**

Step 4 MUST NOT start until the tests in Steps 2 and 3 pass.

- **Increment:** Score the options in the table in the Information section. Use Kepner-Tregoe decision analysis. In this method, an option must meet every "must" before you score it on weighted "wants".
[6]
- **Adds:** one agreed company position.
- **Kind:** evaluative. Write this rubric first:
  - **Must 1:** every factual sentence matches a Step 2 mark of "true". Evidence: the claim sheet.
  - **Must 2:** the response meets every duty from Step 3. Evidence: the counsel's list.
  - **Want, 40%:** the response restores the trust of the board and investors. Evidence: the board chair's score from 1 to 10.
  - **Want, 30%:** the response can be published before the earliest Step 3 deadline. Evidence: the deadline list.
  - **Want, 30%:** the response limits legal exposure. Evidence: the counsel's score from 1 to 10.
  - **Pass threshold:** the option meets both musts and has the highest weighted score. The board chair and counsel approve it in writing.
- **Cynefin domain:** Complicated. The rubric is the analysis that picks the practice.

**Step 5. Release the response.**

Step 5 MUST NOT start until the Step 4 rubric passes.

- **Increment:**
  - Send the approved text to the board and to staff.
  - Next, publish the text and send it to the journalist.
  - All spokespeople use the same text.
- **Removes:** the gap between the company position and the public record.
- **Kind:** deterministic. Write this test first:

```text
Given the approved text
When the release time arrives
Then the board and staff receive the text no later than the public
And every spokesperson uses the approved text without changes
And the journalist receives the text and the records that counsel allows
```

- **Cynefin domain:** Clear. A known practice exists: one text and one release order.

# Follow-up

**Conditions that change the recommendation**

- If A1 is false, remove the securities items from Step 3. Keep the other items.
- If A4 is false, independent directors (board members who are not employees) and outside counsel run Steps 2 to 4. In that case, the CEO does not speak in public for the company.
- If the publication sets a deadline before Step 2 ends, send it the holding line only.
