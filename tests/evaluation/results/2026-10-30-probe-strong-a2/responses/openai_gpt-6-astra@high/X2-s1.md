1. **Find the break.**  
   **Kind:** Deterministic, meaning a fixed acceptance check.

   **Test first:** Given three recent retrospectives, when each proposal is traced, then each has a state and supporting evidence or “unknown.”

   Spend 20 minutes assigning the states listed under Information. If notes are missing, reconstruct one recent example with two willing participants.

   Identify the first failed step in that example. Classify its cause as known, requiring further analysis, or needing a trial.

   **Output:** A short action history that separates observed failures from assumed causes.

2. **Create one change card.**  
   **Kind:** Deterministic.  
   **Domain:** Clear, meaning the procedure has known steps.

   Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 passes.

   **Test first:** Given one recorded failure, when the card is complete, then another person can verify every required field.

   Spend 15 minutes completing one card:

   - **Problem:** What happens, and what does it cost?
   - **Change:** What will people do differently?
   - **Owner:** Who ensures the trial happens?
   - **Authority:** Who can approve the change?
   - **Time:** What work will stop or wait to create capacity?
   - **Measure:** What outcome will show improvement?
   - **Baseline:** What happens before the change?
   - **Target:** What result counts as success?
   - **Review date:** When will the team inspect the result?

   Avoid vague actions such as “communicate better.” Instead, name an observable behavior, such as “name a receiver before each handoff.”

   **Output:** One executable commitment, rather than several unfunded intentions. [judgment]

3. **Run a bounded trial and review its effect.**  
   **Kind:** Stochastic, meaning results can vary.  
   **Domain:** Complex, meaning effects become clear through a trial.

   Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 2 passes.

   **Acceptance test first:**
   - **Metric:** The card’s outcome measure.
   - **Baseline:** Measure five comparable cases before the change.
   - **Sample:** Five eligible cases under the change.
   - **Pass threshold:** Meet the card’s target without extra overtime or defects.

   For example, measure handoff delay and target a 20% reduction against the measured baseline. This example illustrates the test; it does not describe your team. [judgment]

   Cap the trial at one team-hour of extra effort over two weeks. Stop if it causes harm or exhausts that budget. Do not accept the change if the sample remains incomplete.

   Review the result before collecting new retrospective topics. Keep the change only if it passes the test.

   **Output:** Evidence about whether the change helps, not merely whether someone completed an action. The expected benefit remains a guess until the trial ends. [judgment]
