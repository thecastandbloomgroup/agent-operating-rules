## Headline

Name one decision owner, freeze outbound statements, verify the facts against records, and then pick one response path.

- **Control:** one owner and one channel end the conflicting guidance from Legal, PR, and the board.
- **Facts:** nobody should reply until the CEO statement is checked against primary records.
- **Protection:** a document hold protects the records while the facts are open.
- **Decision:** a scored comparison picks the response, and the board approves it once.

## Background

A journalist published a claim that the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board each send you separate guidance. No one owns the decision.

## Relevance

Each uncoordinated reply can become a second statement that is also wrong. The next few hours decide whether the company has one message or three.

## Information

**Evidence**
- Three groups give conflicting guidance, so no single owner exists (per the query).
- No one has yet compared the article with company records [judgment].
- Confidence that the statement is false or true: low. No primary records have been reviewed.
- The time limits below (1 hour, 4 hours, 24 hours) are working targets [judgment].

**Assumption log**

`Assumption: you hold authority to convene Legal, PR, and the board chair. Why not measured: the material does not state your role. Validate by: check the escalation chart. If wrong: Step 1 moves to the CEO or board chair.`

`Assumption: the company is not listed and no regulator is involved. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask Legal for a yes or no. If wrong: add a disclosure review to Step 2, with a deadline set by counsel.`

`Assumption: the CEO statement exists in writing or on record. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: PR retrieves the transcript or recording. If wrong: Step 2 starts with witness accounts, and confidence stays low longer.`

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is an immediate denial or apology. It loses because either one becomes a second false statement if the facts differ [judgment]. Silence also loses: it leaves the article uncontested and the board unaligned [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these four steps, in this order.

1. **Name one owner and freeze statements.**
   - Increment: hold a 30-minute call with the CEO, general counsel, head of PR, and board chair. Name the decision owner. Send one internal hold message.
   - Blocker removed: three conflicting instruction streams.
   - Kind: deterministic.
   - Domain: Chaotic [1]. Act first to stabilise, then sense.
   - Test (Gherkin [2]):
     - Given Legal, PR, and the board each send guidance
     - When the owner issues the hold message
     - Then all guidance routes through the owner and no new public statement goes out

2. **Verify the statement against primary records.**
   - Increment: Legal and one reviewer outside the CEO's reporting line check each factual claim. Target: 4 hours. Mark each claim true, false, or unverified.
   - Blocker removed: nobody knows if the statement is false.
   - Kind: evaluative.
   - Domain: Confused. The domain of the response is unclear until the facts are known, so this step gathers what is needed to classify.
   - Rubric: pass at 85 of 100 or more.
     - Coverage of every factual claim, weight 40. Evidence: a claim-by-claim list.
     - Primary records, not recollection, weight 35. Evidence: a document or recording for each claim.
     - Reviewer independence, weight 25. Evidence: the reviewer's reporting line.

3. **Preserve records.**
   - Increment: Legal sends a preservation notice to everyone who drafted or approved the statement.
   - Blocker removed: loss or change of evidence.
   - Kind: deterministic.
   - Domain: Clear. Deduction from a known best practice.
   - Test:
     - Given a person helped draft or approve the statement
     - When Legal sends the preservation notice
     - Then that person confirms receipt in writing
   - Start Step 3 right after Step 1. Step 3 MUST finish before Step 2 reviewers edit any file.

4. **Choose one response path.**
   - Increment: write a one-page memo that scores three paths: stand by, clarify, and retract and correct. Take it to the board once.
   - Blocker removed: no shared basis for the decision.
   - Kind: evaluative.
   - Domain: Complicated. Analysis with known expertise picks the practice, using a Pugh matrix [3].
   - Rubric: the baseline is "stand by". The chosen path MUST score above baseline and MUST NOT score worse on accuracy.
     - Accuracy against Step 2 results, weight 30
     - Legal exposure, weight 25
     - Stakeholder trust, weight 20
     - Speed, weight 15
     - Reversibility, weight 10
   - Evidence for each criterion: the Step 2 claim list and Legal's written view.
   - Step 4 MUST NOT start until the rubric in Step 2 passes.

Open questions: none.

Conditions that change the recommendation:
- If Step 2 shows the statement is true, replace Step 4 with a rebuttal plan that publishes the records.
- If the company is listed, add a disclosure review to Step 2 before any public reply.
