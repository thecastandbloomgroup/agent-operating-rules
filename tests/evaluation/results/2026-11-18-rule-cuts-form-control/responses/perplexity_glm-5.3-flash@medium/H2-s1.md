## Headline

Stabilize first, then verify the facts, then speak once with one voice. Do not answer legal, PR, or the board individually today.

1. Freeze all external statements for the next 12 hours and route every media contact to one owner.
2. Build a fact base: the CEO's exact words yesterday, the article's exact claims, and the underlying documents.
3. Have legal and PR review the same fact base, not their own versions of events.
4. Then publish one external statement, either a correction or a confirmation, through the single owner.

## Background

The CEO issued a public statement yesterday. A journalist published that it was false. Legal, PR, and the board message you in parallel, and no one is aligned.

## Relevance

Uncoordinated responses create a second story: the conflict among your own functions. The first hours set the correction cost, because each new statement is a fresh legal record [judgment].

## Information

The material does not give the CEO's words, the article, or the documents behind them. So the decision rests on the structure of the crisis, not on its details.

Assumption log:

- Assumption: no regulator, market announcement, or safety issue is involved. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: legal scans the statement and the article for regulated topics in step 2. If wrong: escalate to counsel before step 1 ends, and the freeze period may extend.
- Assumption: you hold authority to direct legal, PR, and board communication now. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: confirm the CEO or chair names you as single owner. If wrong: step 1 runs with the owner that the CEO or chair names instead.
- Assumption: the journalist's claim is at least partly accurate. Why not measured: no tool can reach the counterparty's evidence. Validate by: the fact comparison in step 2. If wrong: a confirmation instead of a correction becomes right.

The workflow follows the Chaotic and Confused pattern: stabilise before analysis, then gather facts before picking a story [1].

Alternative considered: respond within hours with an immediate denial. It loses because an unverified denial that later breaks doubles the correction cost and creates a second false statement on record [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we run these steps in order:

1. **Freeze and route (today, within 2 hours).** All recipients of media contact reply "the company will respond later" and forward questions to one named owner. Increment: one email plus one phone tree. Blocker removed: parallel contradictory messages. Kind: deterministic, test written first. Cynefin: Chaotic, because no cause and effect are visible and stabilisation comes first [1].

2. **Build the fact base (today, before the freeze ends).** Pull the transcript or recording of yesterday's statement, the published article, and the primary documents behind both. Classify each of the article's claims as supported, contradicted, or unverifiable against the documents. Increment: one working session. Blocker removed: the unknown truth value of the CEO's statement. Kind: deterministic, test written first. Cynefin: Confused, because the first action must gather what is needed to classify the dispute [1].
   ```gherkin
   Given the transcript, the article, and the primary documents
   When each article claim is checked line by line
   Then every claim has one of the three labels plus a document and section reference
   ```
3. **Legal exposure analysis (same day, after step 2).** Counsel scores the liability paths that the fact base opens, using a rubric: disclosure duties (weight 3), prior public statements (weight 2), correction timing rules (weight 1); pass threshold: every duty item above score 4 out of 5 has a documented action [2]. Increment: one written memo. Blocker removed: legal acting on its own facts. Cynefin: Complicated, because the analysis is knowable with expertise. Step 3 MUST NOT start until the test in step 2 passes. Kind: evaluative, rubric written before scoring.
4. **Draft one external statement (step 3 in hand).** One paragraph that matches the fact base, with the correction or the confirmation. Increment: one draft, approved by the owner. Blocker removed: the ability to speak with one voice. Kind: deterministic, test written first. Step 4 MUST NOT start until step 3's memo is signed off.
   ```gherkin
   Given the approved draft
   When every factual claim in it is checked against the fact base
   Then no claim contradicts a fact and no claim lacks a document reference
   ```
5. **Run a capped probe before full release (next morning).** Share the statement with the journalist under embargo and one board member, and measure the factual objections raised. Stop condition: any factual objection triggers a rewrite. Budget: one release attempt plus one rewrite, capped at two. Cynefin: Complex, because the public reaction shows only afterward, and the response is a safe-to-fail probe; its leverage is a guess [1]. Kind: stochastic, acceptance test first: metric is unsupported claims per statement, baseline is theudit of yesterday's statement from step 2, pass threshold is zero unsupported claims at the full text sample.
