### 3. Test the change and review the evidence

Step 3 MUST NOT start until the experiment passes Step 2’s test.

**Acceptance test first, stochastic:** Results vary because people and work vary.

- **Metric:** The observable result defined in Step 2.
- **Baseline:** The current result recorded before the trial.
- **Pass threshold:** The team’s stated target, with no breach of the harm limit.
- **Sample:** Two comparable opportunities to use the change.
- **Decision:** Accept only if both opportunities meet the threshold; otherwise revise or stop.

**Work:** Run the bounded trial. Start the next retrospective with a 10-minute evidence review, before discussing new topics.

If the change never happens, examine the barrier instead of carrying the action forward unchanged. [judgment]

**Blocker removed:** Actions that disappear without a result or decision.

**Domain:** Complex, because trials reveal the effect afterward. Different workloads or team members can break the pattern.

**Budget and stop condition:** Keep extra effort within one working session. Stop sooner if the stated harm limit occurs.
