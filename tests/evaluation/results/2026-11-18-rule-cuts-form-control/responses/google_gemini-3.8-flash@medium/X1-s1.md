### Headline

Deploy a golden evaluation set and structured agent feedback before you change prompts or models.

### Background

You deployed a Large Language Model (LLM) to summarise customer support tickets.
Support agents report that summary outputs are sometimes wrong.
The engineering team currently tracks no quality metrics for these summaries.

### Relevance

You need actionable quality metrics now to stop unmeasured regressions.
Without a test baseline, prompt modifications risk further customer support errors.
