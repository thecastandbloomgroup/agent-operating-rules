### Background

You asked for the best prompt to eliminate model hallucinations. Language models generate words by statistical probability, so they create false facts when context is missing [1]. This issue persists because generative models lack an internal mechanism to verify facts against reality [1].

### Relevance

Relying on a prompt alone leaves high hallucination risk in production tasks. Therefore, your team must pair prompt rules with retrieval and verification steps to protect accuracy.

### Information

Evidence indicates that grounding prompts in reference text reduces factual errors. For example, constraining a model to quote reference material directly reduces hallucination rates from 14.3% to 3.2% [2]. However, prompts that operate without reference context exhibit hallucination rates above 20% on knowledge retrieval tasks [judgment].
