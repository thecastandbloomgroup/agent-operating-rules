# Background

A release on 2026-10-21 changed the connection pool, cache TTL, and ORM eager loading. p95 latency rose from 120 ms to 900 ms with 503 errors. Logs, a diff, and traces were supplied.

# Relevance

Error rate in Window B is 0.40%, 20 times the baseline of 0.02% [3]. Each day unfixed costs failed customer requests.

# Information

The pool was cut from 50 to 8 connections and the timeout from 30 s to 5 s [2]. Peak active connections before the release were 31, therefore a cap of 8 cannot serve the known peak [3]. The logs confirm 8 of 8 connections in use with 9 to 14 waiters, and "pool exhausted" warnings rose from none on 2026-10-20 to 1,800 to 2,400 per hour on 2026-10-22 [1]. Pool acquire p95 rose from 3 ms to 690 ms, which accounts for most of the total p95 rise from 120 ms to 900 ms [3].

The cache TTL fell from 300 s to 60 s. Miss rate rose from 12% to 31%, so more requests reach the database [3]. However, `http.request` p50 of 210 ms roughly equals p50 acquire of 140 ms plus p50 query of about 30 ms, so connection queueing drives even the median degrade.

The `selectinload` change adds one extra query per request, but orders p95 moved only from 52 ms to 58 ms, and the lines query runs at 9 to 26 ms [3]. It probably prevents a worse N+1 pattern, so keep it.
