## Headline

Stabilise communications now, verify the facts before any public reply, and start legal preservation today.

- The largest near-term risk is an unverified rebuttal, so all external comment pauses under one spokesperson.
- The truth status of the statement is unknown, so verification gates every later step.
- Preservation duties already apply because litigation is reasonably anticipated [2].
- The final message goes through a small-audience test first, because public reaction is not predictable.

## Background

A journalist has published that the CEO's statement yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are messaging separately, and no position is agreed. Next steps are requested.

## Relevance

The first hours set legal exposure and reputational cost. Early, uncoordinated replies create new facts and extend the crisis [judgment] (see A2).

## Information

First, the evidence. Crisis research supports verifying facts and matching the response to established responsibility before replying [1]. The duty to preserve documents starts when litigation is reasonably anticipated, which this publication triggers [2]. If the company is listed, a false CEO statement can raise disclosure questions, and Regulation Fair Disclosure (Reg FD) governs selective disclosure to investors [3].

Second, the uncertainty. Whether the statement is false: about as likely as not (33 to 66%) until verification completes [judgment].

Third, the assumption log.

`A1. You can direct Legal, PR, and board communications. Why not measured: the material does not state your role. Validate by: confirm mandate with the CEO or chair on the first call. If wrong: the owner of Step 1 becomes the general counsel.`

`A2. The company is listed on an exchange. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: one question to the general counsel. If wrong: remove Reg FD review from Step 4.`

`A3. The CEO statement is on record in writing or on video. Why not measured: a journalist quoted it. Validate by: pull the original text in Step 2. If wrong: verification relies on witnesses, which weakens Step 3.`

Fourth, the alternative considered. An immediate public rebuttal is the strongest alternative. It loses because a rebuttal against a true claim doubles both credibility and legal damage [judgment], while guidance supports correcting false information only after facts are checked [1].

## End

I recommend that we:

1. **Stabilise within 60 minutes.** Open one incident channel, pause all external comment, and name one spokesperson and one decision owner. This removes the unaligned inbound messaging. Test (deterministic):
Given the incident channel is open and the spokesperson is named,
When an external inquiry arrives,
Then only the spokesperson replies, and every reply is logged.
Domain: Chaotic, because uncoordinated replies create new facts before causes are known, so stabilisation comes first.

2. **List the disputed facts within 2 hours.** Extract each factual assertion from the CEO statement and from the article. This removes the unknown scope of the dispute. Test (deterministic):
Given both source texts,
When the assertion list is complete,
Then every assertion carries a verifier name and a source label: record, witness, or unverified.
Domain: Confused, because this output classifies the problem and decides the remaining path.

3. **Score the truth of the statement, evaluative task.** Rubric: documentary records 40%, direct witnesses 30%, independent expert review 20%, the journalist's published evidence 10%. Bands: 70 to 100 means verified accurate, 31 to 69 means unresolved, 0 to 30 means verified false. Evidence per criterion: original documents, recorded interviews, an expert memo, the article attachments. This removes the blocked response choice. Step 3 starts after Step 2 passes. Domain: Complicated, because records and experts can establish cause and effect.

4. **Contain legal exposure in the same working session.** Send a litigation hold to named custodians, and add securities counsel review if A2 holds. This removes spoliation and disclosure risk. Test (deterministic):
Given counsel approves the custodian list,
When the hold notice sends,
Then 100 percent of custodians acknowledge within 24 hours [2].
Domain: Complicated, because counsel analysis selects the practice. This step waits on the assertion list from Step 2.

5. **Publish a holding line now, and probe the full reply in parallel.** The holding line acknowledges the report, states verification is in progress, and gives the next update time; this is established practice, that is, instructing information first [1]. Domain: Clear, because a known best practice applies. Then test the full reply, stochastic task: show the draft to n=30 employees and investors, measure trust against the pre-crisis baseline, pass if within 5 points of baseline, stop and redraft below that, budget capped at 48 hours and one external panel. Domain: Complex, because public reaction is visible only after release, so this runs as a capped probe, parallel to Step 4.

Open questions, each with its use:

2. Do you hold a mandate from the CEO or chair? The answer decides whether Step 1 ownership moves to the general counsel.

Conditions that change this plan: if Step 3 returns verified false, add a public correction and a board governance review of how the statement was cleared. If A1 is false, Step 1 ownership moves as stated.
