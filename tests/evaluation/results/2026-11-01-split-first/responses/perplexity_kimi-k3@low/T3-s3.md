## Headline

Treat this as two separate initiatives: a security change with clear practices, and a conversion change that needs definition before work starts. First, JWT migration has known trade-offs against sessions, so confirm it is required. Next, conversion improvement is unmeasurable today. Finally, three questions below decide scope.

## Background

The request combines migrating an auth service from sessions to JWT and improving sign-up conversion. Auth migration affects security and every client. Conversion work affects product funnels and metrics.

## Relevance

Mixing a security cutover with a funnel experiment in one effort couples two risk profiles. Separating them lets each ship at its own pace and be measured on its own.
