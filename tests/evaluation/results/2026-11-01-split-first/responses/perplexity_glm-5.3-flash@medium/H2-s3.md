# Headline

Freeze all outbound statements within two hours, verify the journalist's claim with a fact memo today, then issue one corrected message through a single spokesperson. Supporting points:

1. Stabilize first: one controlled channel stops mixed messages from legal, PR, and the board.
2. Verify second: a fact memo decides whether the path is correction, clarification, or rebuttal.
3. Respond third: one approved statement from one spokesperson reaches every audience.
4. Monitor in parallel: a capped probe tracks whether the story grows or dies.

# Background

The CEO gave a public statement yesterday. A journalist published that the statement is false. Legal, PR, and the board each send messages, and no group is aligned. The request is the next steps for the person managing the response.

# Relevance

The story is live today, Sunday, November 1. Each hour of mixed messages adds new coverage and new proof of disarray [judgment].

# Information

The only material is the query. The disputed statement, the article, and the audience size are not in the session. Therefore the analysis classifies the situation by structure, not by facts. The query shows three stakeholder groups that act without a shared fact base or a decision owner. Each group has one source here, so each theme has a source count of one [no source]. Confidence in the process below is high because the sequence does not depend on the disputed facts. Confidence in the factual dispute itself is low, because no document is available for review [judgment].

**Assumptions**

A1. The statement contains at least one verifiably false factual claim. Why not measured: the material does not include the statement or the article. Validate by: read the article beside the transcript of the statement. If wrong: replace Step 4 with a rebuttal path.

A2. No litigation or regulator notice exists yet. Why not measured: no tool can reach internal legal records in this session. Validate by: ask legal in the first call whether a notice exists. If wrong: no public word releases before legal sign-off at the level of counsel.

A3. One communications lead exists and can act today. Why not measured: the material does not name one. Validate by: name the person in the first call. If wrong: the CEO's chief of staff holds the channel until the board appoints a lead.

**Alternative considered**

Respond in public immediately, with a denial or a correction. It loses because a public reply built on unverified facts can create a second false statement. A second false statement doubles the damage and gives the story a second life [judgment]. Therefore the fact memo comes before any public word.

# End

I recommend that we run the five steps below. Steps 1 to 4 run in sequence. Step 5 runs in parallel, after Step 2.

**Step 1. Freeze outbound statements (2 hours).**
Send a written freeze order to legal, PR, and the board. Name one comms lead as the single approver. This is a Chaotic domain action, because messages now leave through uncontrolled channels, so stabilization comes before analysis [18]. Test, deterministic, in Gherkin form [14]:

```gherkin
Given the freeze order reached all three groups
When a team member drafts an outbound statement
Then the draft routes to the comms lead and publishes only after approval
```

Step 2 must not start before the freeze order goes out.

**Step 2. Produce a fact memo (rest of today).**
Collect the CEO statement, the article, and every record behind them. Write a one-page memo that lists each disputed fact with its evidence. This is a Confused domain action, because the facts decide which response path applies [18]. Test, evaluative, rubric:

- Every disputed fact traces to a document. Weight 0.5.
- The memo lists all facts in the article plus the statement. Weight 0.3.
- Each listed fact cites one primary record, such as the transcript or a data extract. Weight 0.2.
- Pass threshold: 80 percent weighted score. Evidence: the transcript, the article, and internal records.

**Step 3. Approve one holding statement (same evening).**
Legal, PR, and the board review the memo and approve one statement for one spokesperson. A single voice is the established practice for a live dispute [judgment]. This is a Clear domain action, because the best practice is known and applies directly [18]. Test, deterministic, checklist:

- Given the memo passes its Step 2 threshold
- When each of the three groups signs the statement
- Then the approval count equals 3 and no unsigned version circulates

Step 4 must not start until this test passes.

**Step 4. Choose the path and respond within 24 hours.**
Score three paths, correction, clarification, and rebuttal, against the memo. Release through the single spokesperson. This is a Complicated domain action, because analysis picks among more than one good practice [18]. Test, evaluative, Kepner-Tregoe decision analysis [15]:

- Factual accuracy of the chosen path. Weight 0.4. Evidence: the fact memo.
- Legal exposure reduced. Weight 0.3. Evidence: written input from legal.
- Audience trust retained. Weight 0.2. Evidence: prior coverage themes.
- Speed to release. Weight 0.1. Evidence: hours from memo to release.
- Pass threshold: 70 of 100 weighted.

**Step 5. Monitor the story (starts after Step 2, runs 7 days).**
Track follow-up coverage daily with a stop condition. This is a Complex domain action, because the story's path is visible only afterward, so it runs as a safe-to-fail probe and its leverage estimate is a guess [18]. Test, stochastic:

- Metric: number of new articles that repeat the false claim, per day.
- Baseline: 1 article, the original publication.
- Pass threshold: 0 new articles on 5 of 7 days.
- Sample size: 7 days.
- Stop condition: pause the probe and escalate to the board if coverage grows to 3 or more new articles in one day. Probe budget: 1 hour of review per day, 7 days total.

**Sequencing**

1. Step 1 first, regardless of leverage.
2. Step 2 next, because its output decides the rest.
3. Step 3 waits on Step 2. Step 4 waits on the Step 3 approval.
4. Step 5 runs in parallel with Steps 3 and 4, capped by its budget.

**Condition that changes this recommendation**

If A2 is false, insert one step between Step 3 and Step 4: counsel reviews and rewrites the statement before release, and the 24-hour window extends to 48 hours.

# Follow-up
