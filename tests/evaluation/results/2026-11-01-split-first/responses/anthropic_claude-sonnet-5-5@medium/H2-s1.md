## Headline

Name one decision owner now, verify the facts, then choose one public response and send it to everyone at once.

- Conflicting messages from Legal, PR, and the board are the first problem, so stop them before anything else.
- The facts decide the response, so check the article against primary records before any public statement.
- One approved message reduces the risk of a second contradiction.

## Background

A journalist published that the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board send you separate and conflicting advice. The material does not say whether the claim is true.

## Relevance

Each hour without alignment raises the chance that two groups say different things in public. A second contradiction would harm trust more than the first article.

## Information

**Evidence**

- Crisis research says the response should match how much responsibility the public assigns to the organization [1]. That is why the facts must come first.
- A holding statement is a short public note that says you know about the report and are checking it. Issue it within 1 to 4 hours [judgment].
- The claim in the article is likely (66 to 100%) to be partly true or partly false, not fully one or the other. Confidence is low, because the article and the records are unseen [inference].
- This section uses the Cynefin framework, which sorts work by how cause and effect relate [2].

**Assumption log**

A1. The CEO's statement is not yet checked against records. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: Legal compares the transcript with the source records. If wrong: skip Step 2 and go to Step 3.

A2. The company has no legal duty to disclose to a regulator or exchange. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: Legal confirms in one message. If wrong: add a disclosure step before Step 3.

A3. You can ask the CEO or chair to name a decision owner. Why not measured: the material does not state your role. Validate by: send the request and note the reply. If wrong: ask the chair to send it.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is an immediate public denial or an immediate retraction. It loses because both bet on an unchecked fact. A wrong bet creates a second false statement [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these four actions in order:

1. **Name one decision owner and freeze outside statements.**
   - Increment: one written note to Legal, PR, and the board, sent within 30 minutes.
   - Blocker removed: three groups give conflicting advice.
   - Kind: deterministic. Domain: Chaotic, because signals conflict and no cause and effect is visible. This action comes first for that reason.
   - Test:
     - Given Legal, PR, and the board each send advice
     - When the note names one owner and one rule: no outside statement without owner approval
     - Then each group confirms by reply and sends all drafts to the owner

2. **Check the article against primary records.**
   - Increment: a claim-by-claim table, finished in 90 minutes.
   - Blocker removed: nobody knows what is true.
   - Kind: evaluative. Domain: Complicated, because records and expertise can answer it.
   - Test (rubric, written first):

     | Criterion | Weight | Evidence needed |
     |---|---|---|
     | Each claim labeled true, false, or unclear | 50 | Transcript and source record |
     | What the CEO knew when speaking | 30 | Dated emails or briefings |
     | Errors in the article | 20 | Record that contradicts the article |

     Pass threshold: 80 of 100 points, with a record cited for every label.
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 2 passes.

3. **Choose one public response and one holding statement.**
   - Increment: a Pugh matrix with three options (correct, defend, hold and investigate), scored on accuracy, legal exposure, trust, and speed. The baseline is "hold and investigate". Then one approved statement of up to 100 words.
   - Blocker removed: PR and Legal disagree on tone and content.
   - Kind: evaluative. Domain: Complicated, because the Step 2 analysis picks the practice.
   - Test: the chosen option MUST score higher than the baseline on at least two criteria and lower on none. Legal MUST confirm that no claim in the statement conflicts with Step 2.
   - Step 4 MUST NOT start until the owner approves the statement.

4. **Brief the board and staff with the same text.**
   - Increment: a one-page brief with the facts, the decision, and the next update time.
   - Blocker removed: the board hears a different story than the press.
   - Kind: deterministic. Domain: Clear, because a standard board briefing practice exists.
   - Test:
     - Given the owner approved the statement
     - When the brief goes to the board, Legal, and PR at the same time
     - Then the brief repeats the approved statement word for word and names the next update time

If A2 is false, insert a disclosure review between Step 2 and Step 3.
