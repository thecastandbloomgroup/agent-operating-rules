## Headline

Choose Northwind Analytics, but only after two checks: confirm data growth and get a written cap on overage fees.

- Cost: Northwind is the only vendor-backed option that fits the 400,000 budget, at about 399,000 after growth.
- Risk: Bluepeak exceeds the budget with no cap, and Cedar moves unpriced operating work to a one-engineer team.
- Performance: Northwind's 2.1 s median is slower than Bluepeak's 1.3 s but faster than Cedar's 3.4 s.

## Background

Three vendors proposed a data warehouse. The budget is 400,000 USD over three years. Current data is 14 terabytes (TB), and the team expects growth of about 3 TB per year.

## Relevance

Northwind fits the budget by a margin of about 1,000 USD. A small error in the growth figure changes the decision.

## Information

Evidence (all vendor figures are as stated in [1]):

| Option | Stated cost (USD) | Adjusted cost (USD) | Versus budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northwind | 372,000 | 399,000 | 1,000 under |
| Bluepeak | 455,000 | 455,000 or more | 55,000 over |
| Cedar | 286,000 | 286,000 plus staff time | 114,000 under, before staff |

- Northwind data size: 14 TB now, 17 TB after year 1, 20 TB after year 2, 23 TB after year 3. The licence covers 20 TB. Overage is 3 TB at 9,000 per TB, which is 27,000. [1]
- Budget break point: overage above 28,000 breaks the budget. That is 3.11 TB over, so growth above 3.04 TB per year fails. [inference]
- Bluepeak: the vendor's own estimate is 455,000 at current query volume, and pricing has no cap. High confidence, one primary source. [1]
- Cedar: hardware covers 30 TB, which is enough for 23 TB. It has no availability guarantee and one database engineer today. [1]
- Cedar headroom of 114,000 equals 38,000 per year for staff time. Whether that is enough is unknown. [judgment]
- Availability: 99.9% allows about 8.8 hours of downtime per year. 99.95% allows about 4.4 hours. [inference]
- Benchmarks used a 10 TB sample, not our 14 TB. Medians are 2.1 s, 1.3 s, and 3.4 s. [1]
- Confidence that Northwind stays within budget: about as likely as not (33 to 66%). The basis is a stated growth of 3 TB per year against a break point of 3.04.

Assumption log:

A1. Overage is billed only on year 3 size. Why not measured: the proposal does not state billing timing. Validate by: ask Northwind in writing. If wrong: cost rises above 399,000 and the budget fails.

A2. Growth is a steady 3 TB per year. Why not measured: the material gives only the team's estimate. Validate by: read 36 months of storage history. If wrong: growth above 3.04 TB per year breaks the budget.

A3. A 2.1 s median and 99.9% availability meet our needs. Why not measured: the material states no requirement. Validate by: ask the main users for their limits. If wrong: no proposal fits both need and budget.

Alternative considered: Cedar. It costs 113,000 less before staff time. It loses because staff cost is unpriced, it has no availability guarantee, and the team has one engineer. [judgment]
