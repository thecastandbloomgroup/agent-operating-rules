# Headline

Stabilise first: verify the facts, freeze all public statements, and get Legal, PR, and the board on one call within two hours.

- Nobody knows yet whether the CEO's statement was false, and that fact controls every later step.
- Three groups are messaging one person, which means there is no single decision owner or message discipline.
- An uncoordinated reply from any group creates legal and reputational damage that outlasts the news cycle.
- A short, structured sequence (stabilise, verify, decide, respond) works for both outcomes: the claim is true, or the claim is false.

# Background

A journalist published a claim that the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are contacting you separately, and there is no alignment. You have asked for the next steps.

# Relevance

The first 4 to 24 hours set the public narrative [judgment]. Acting before verification risks a false denial, which is worse than the original claim. Not acting at all allows speculation to fill the gap.

# Information

There are two possible facts: the statement was false, or it was accurate and the article is wrong. The response differs greatly by outcome, so verification is the first dependency [judgment]. The current state fits the Chaotic Cynefin domain: no clear cause and effect, many actors, no order [1]. In a Chaotic domain, the first job is to stabilise before anything else [1].

Assumption: You have authority to convene Legal, PR, and the board, or access to someone who does. Why not measured: the material does not state your role. Validate by: ask one sponsor (board chair or general counsel) to mandate the call. If wrong: route all steps through that sponsor instead.

Assumption: The CEO is reachable today. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: one call to the CEO's office. If wrong: the board chair takes interim authority for the response.

Alternative considered: issue an immediate rebuttal to control the story. This loses because a rebuttal without verified facts risks a second, larger crisis if the journalist's evidence holds [judgment].

# End

I recommend that we do the following, in order.

1. Call for a statement freeze now. Message Legal, PR, and the board that no one speaks to press or posts publicly until Step 4. This removes the blocker of uncoordinated responses, which is the largest current risk. Cynefin domain: Chaotic, because no cause and effect is stable yet, and acting first restores order. Test: each of the three groups confirms in writing that it will hold.
   - Given a journalist has published a falsity claim,
     When Legal, PR, and the board each confirm the freeze in writing,
     Then no statement goes out before Step 4. (Deterministic.)

2. Convene one call within two hours. Include the general counsel, the head of PR, one board member, and the CEO or their delegate. Appoint one decision owner, normally the CEO with board oversight. This removes the blocker of fragmented authority. Cynefin domain: Chaotic to Confused, because the group must first classify the facts. Test: the call ends with a named decision owner and a verification plan. (Deterministic; step 2 MUST NOT slip past four hours.)
   - Given the freeze holds,
     When the call ends,
     Then one decision owner, one verification owner, and a deadline exist.

3. Verify the statement against primary records. Pull the document, data, or witness the CEO's statement rests on, and the evidence in the article. Legal leads this to protect privilege. Target: within 24 hours. Cynefin domain: Complicated, because analysis of known records reveals the answer. Test: Legal issues a one-page finding: true, false, or ambiguous, with citations. (Evaluative: criteria are factual accuracy, completeness, privilege; decision owner signs off.)
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start public messaging. Step 4 MUST NOT start until Legal's finding exists.

4. Decide the response on a conditional plan. If the statement was accurate: publish a correction request with evidence, and prepare legal review of the article. If it was false: correct the record quickly, attribute cause, and state the fix, because corrected errors with ownership do less long-term damage than defended errors [judgment]. Cynefin domain: Clear once the finding exists, because the playbook differs by branch. Test: the drafted response passes Legal sign-off and board approval before release. (Deterministic.)

# Follow-up

- If the CEO is implicated personally (not just the company statement), the board, not the CEO, owns Step 4. This changes the recommendation.
- If the market is trading on the claim, securities counsel reviews any statement before release; this can override the 24-hour target.
