### Define “better”

Use the ticket record available **when the summary was created** as the source of truth. Otherwise, later messages can make an accurate summary appear wrong. [judgment]

Classify each defect once, by its primary cause:

| Defect | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| False statement | The summary contradicts the record or adds an unsupported claim. | It says a refund occurred when an agent only proposed one. |
| Missing fact | The summary leaves out a fact needed for the next action. | It omits an unresolved cancellation request. |
| Unclear wording | The facts appear, but their wording obscures their meaning. | It confuses the customer with the account owner. |

Separately, mark severity. A **material error** changes the next support action or misstates a commitment, payment, identity, or unresolved issue. [judgment]

Track these measures:

- **Material-error rate:** summaries with at least one material error divided by reviewed summaries.
- **Required-fact coverage:** required facts present divided by required facts identified in the ticket.
- **Agent correction time:** seconds needed to make a summary usable.
- **Run cost and response time:** currency per summary and seconds per summary.

The first two measures test accuracy. The others test whether the change helps agents at an acceptable operating cost. [judgment]
