## Headline

Hold retrospectives that end with exactly one owned action, then make action completion visible and measured. Supporting points:

1. Most stuck retros list many actions and complete none. Focus fixes that.
2. Review of past actions at the start of each retro builds follow-through.
3. Metrics from the last period give the discussion a factual base.

## Background

Retrospectives run, people attend, and work continues without change. The request asks for a way to make retros produce real shifts. The material does not state the team's size, cadence, or current format.

## Relevance

A retro that produces no change costs one to two hours per person per cycle and returns nothing. Because the pattern repeats, the team will lose trust in the practice and attendance will fall [inference].
