## Background

A journalist published a report today that says the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board each send you advice, and the advice conflicts. You ask for the next steps.

## Relevance

Each hour without a shared position gives the report more time to spread unanswered. In addition, a second inconsistent message from the company would add a new problem to the first.
