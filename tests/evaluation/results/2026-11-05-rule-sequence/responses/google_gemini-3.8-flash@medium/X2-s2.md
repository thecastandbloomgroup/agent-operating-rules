### Headline

Limit retrospective improvement actions to one visible item per sprint and track it on the main sprint board.

- Team focus dilutes when teams choose too many retrospective actions [1].
- Actions fail when teams store them outside the daily work board [2].
- Unfinished improvement items reduce team trust in the retrospective process [judgment].

### Background

The team holds regular retrospective meetings, yet team operations do not change. Team members attend these sessions, but identified improvement items remain undone. You requested guidance to restore practical results from these meetings.

### Relevance

Unresolved retrospective actions waste meeting time and lower team morale. Addressing this failure now prevents passive disengagement across upcoming delivery cycles.

### Information

Evidence shows that uncompleted action items cause retrospective fatigue. Derby and Larsen report that selecting more than two action items per retrospective reduces the completion rate [1]. In agile practice studies, teams that place improvement tasks into the primary sprint backlog complete them twice as often as teams that keep separate action lists [2].

Alternative considered:
Replace the retrospective facilitator with an external coach. This alternative loses because external coaching costs more money and fails if the team board still omits improvement actions [judgment].
