## Headline

Freeze all external statements now, build a verified record of what the CEO said, then publish one coordinated response within 24 hours. Supporting points:

1. An unverified reply risks a second false statement, so messaging stops until the record is checked.
2. Legal, PR, and the board conflict because each works from a different version of the facts, so a single evidence file resolves the conflict at its source.
3. The response speed sets the correction path: fast verification allows context or correction, and slow verification limits the options.

## Background

The CEO made a public statement yesterday. A journalist published today that the statement is false. Legal, PR, and the board are all sending separate messages, and no one is aligned.

## Relevance

Every hour of mixed messages adds a second story: the original claim, and the internal disagreement about it. Reporters cover both. The response window is measured in hours, not days. [judgment]

## Information

**Evidence.** Crisis communication research ties the response to two factors: who the public holds responsible, and the reputational threat to the organization. The response type must match the crisis type, and internal disagreement leaks into coverage if it is not resolved first [1]. Compare that baseline to this case: the disagreement among legal, PR, and the board is itself newsworthy, so alignment is a precondition, not a parallel task [inference]. A denial published before the primary record is checked carries a high risk of a second false statement, even if it is 50% likely to be correct on its own; the downside of one unverified denial exceeds the upside of speed [judgment].

**Assumptions.**

- A1. The article quotes the CEO statement directly, not a paraphrase. Why not measured: the article is not available in this session. Validate by: obtain and archive the article with its publication timestamp. If wrong: the scope narrows to internal alignment and statement cleanup.
- A2. A primary record of the statement exists, such as a recording, transcript, or approved text. Why not measured: no party has shared it with the user. Validate by: ask the communications lead to locate it today. If wrong: the first task becomes fact-finding, and the response window extends to 48 hours.
- A3. No external reply has been published by the company yet. Why not measured: the user did not state it. Validate by: scan company channels and press contacts. If wrong: the freeze becomes a correction task instead.

**Alternative considered.** Reply to the journalist immediately with a denial or context. It loses because the primary record is unverified, and an early denial that fails creates a worse, lasting story [inference].
