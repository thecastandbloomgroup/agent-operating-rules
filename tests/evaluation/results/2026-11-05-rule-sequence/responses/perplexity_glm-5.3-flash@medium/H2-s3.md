# Headline

Stabilize first: name one incident owner, freeze all external messages for 6 hours, and verify the reported falsehood before any correction or rebuttal.

1. Control: the conflict among legal, PR, and the board is a process gap, so one owner with a freeze closes it.
2. Facts: the article and the record of the statement determine the response, so verification precedes any wording.
3. Risk: a legal exposure assessment decides the timing and the wording of any correction.
4. Message: one approved short statement goes to all three groups at the same time.

# Background

The CEO made a public statement yesterday. A journalist has published an article that says the statement is false. Legal, PR, and the board each send conflicting guidance, so no single decision owner exists.

# Relevance

The first hours decide the size of the damage. An unverified correction creates a second false statement, which is the larger risk in this situation.

# Information

A one-page fact sheet becomes the shared basis for every later decision. Without it, each group argues from its own reading of the event [judgment].

Assumption log:

- A1. A verbatim record of the statement exists (transcript, recording, or written text). Why not measured: the request does not state it. Validate by: ask the CEO office for the record within one hour. If wrong: Step 2 becomes reconstruction from witnesses, which takes one more half-day.
- A2. The statement carries legal or regulatory exposure. Why not measured: the subject of the statement is unknown. Validate by: Step 3, a legal review inside 3 hours. If wrong: the scope shrinks from legal-first to PR-first and Step 4 becomes the main work.
- A3. The conflict among the three groups comes from role boundaries, not from disagreement about the fact. Why not measured: no message content reached the analysis. Validate by: compare the three threads before Step 4. If wrong: Step 4 runs as a decision meeting, not a briefing.

Alternative considered: publish a full correction now, before verification, to show speed. It loses because an unverified correction that later proves wrong creates a second false statement and a larger legal exposure [judgment]. High confidence, because both failure paths follow from the same record.

# End

I recommend that we take these actions in this order.

1. **Appoint one incident owner and issue a message freeze for 6 hours.** Increment: the owner is named in writing and the freeze notice reaches legal, PR, and the board within one hour. Blocker removed: three conflicting message streams. Cynefin: Chaotic [2], because no cause and effect are visible yet, so the action stabilizes first (acting before analysis). Test (deterministic [1]):
   ```
   Given the owner is named in writing
   When any group sends an external message
   Then the message cites only pre-approved text or is stopped
   ```
2. **Build a one-page fact sheet.** Increment: the sheet records the verbatim statement, the article headline, the claimed falsehood, and the true position. Blocker removed: no shared factual basis exists. Cynefin: Complicated [2], because the verifier applies a known review procedure (deduction). Test (evaluative). Rubric, scored before sending: accuracy of the quote against the record, weight 50; existence of a record that contradicts the article, weight 30; credibility of the contradicting source, weight 20; pass threshold: 70 of 100, each criterion supported by a document.
3. **Run a legal exposure review.** Increment: a one-page assessment that states whether the statement creates a disclosure duty, a liability, or no duty. Blocker removed: the response has no timing rule. Cynefin: Complicated [2], deduction from statute and contract. Test (evaluative). Kepner-Tregoe style rubric [3]: criteria are legal exposure, disclosure duty, reputational risk, each weighted 33; threshold: pass when exposure is rated with named law or contract clauses; evidence: the fact sheet from Step 2. Step 3 MUST NOT start before the fact sheet in Step 2 is scored at 70 or above.
4. **Issue one holding statement to legal, PR, and the board at the same time.** Increment: a text of 60 words or less says the company is checking the facts and will respond by a stated time. Blocker removed: the three groups still lack one message. Cynefin: Clear [2], because a standard practice exists and one step applies it. Step 4 MUST NOT start before Step 3 returns its assessment. Test (deterministic [1]):
   ```
   Given the holding statement is approved by the owner and legal
   When the statement is sent
   Then all three groups receive it within the same hour
   ```
5. **Probe: monitor press and market reaction for 48 hours.** Increment: an owner-tracked log of new coverage, two checkpoints per day. Blocker removed: no information on whether the story grows or fades. Cynefin: Complex [2], because cause and effect appear only afterward, so a safe-to-fail probe applies. Budget and stop condition: 48 hours and 10000 units of attention cap; stop and switch to full response if coverage volume doubles from the day-one baseline [judgment]. Leverage is a guess here.
