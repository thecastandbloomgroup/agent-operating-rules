## End

I recommend that we use these three steps. The time limits and thresholds below are proposed trial limits, not research benchmarks. [judgment]

### 1. Inspect the last three retrospectives

**Kind:** Deterministic.  
**Domain:** Confused, because the cause remains unknown; gather evidence before choosing an intervention.

**Test first:**

Given the last three retrospectives, using records or participant recall,  
When the team traces each proposed change,  
Then each has a status: no decision, not completed, or completed with an observed result.

**Work:** Spend 15 minutes creating that list. Mark missing evidence rather than inventing it.

This removes the diagnosis blocker. Finish with a shared list, not a discussion about individual effort.

### 2. Create one change agreement

**Dependency:** Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 produces the list.

**Kind:** Deterministic.  
**Domain:** Clear, because deduction from the agreement’s checklist determines whether the commitment is ready.

**Test first:**

Given one recurring problem from Step 1,  
When the team writes a change agreement,  
Then it names one change, one owner, reserved time, a check date, and an observable success measure.

**Work:** Spend 15 minutes completing this card:

> **Problem:** What happens, and when?  
> **Change:** What will differ during the next cycle?  
> **Owner:** Who coordinates it?  
> **Capacity:** What work will make room for it?  
> **Check:** What evidence will show whether it helped?

This removes ambiguity and adds capacity. If authority is missing, replace the change with one specific sponsor request.

### 3. Run a limited trial

**Dependency:** Step 3 MUST NOT start until the agreement passes Step 2’s test.

**Kind:** Stochastic, because team behavior varies.  
**Domain:** Complex, because induction from trials reveals results; workload changes or outside dependencies can break the pattern.

**Acceptance test first:**

- **Metric:** Changes completed and checked for benefit before the next retrospective.
- **Baseline:** The completion-and-check rate found in Step 1.
- **Threshold:** Two of two changes completed and checked.
- **Sample:** Two consecutive retrospective cycles.

**Work:** Run one change per cycle, with a maximum budget of one team-hour per change.

Start each retrospective with the previous result: keep, adapt, or stop. Stop a change early if it causes harm or exceeds its budget.

The trial remains untested until both cycles finish. Completion tests follow-through; observed benefit determines whether to keep each change.
