### Style notes

- Initials come before the surname (B. Minto, not Minto, B.).
- Book titles are italicized in formal IEEE typesetting; in plain text, italics may be omitted.
- Edition statements use the ordinal abbreviation: 3rd ed., 2nd ed.
- U.S. cities take the state code and USA; non-U.S. cities take the country abbreviation.
- Place, publisher, and year are joined with colons and commas as shown.
