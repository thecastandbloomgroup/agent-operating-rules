## Background

## Relevance

A wrong figure in a quarterly report spreads to decisions and to later reports. A short, ordered check limits that spread.

## Information

First, the causes. The list below splits the possible causes into three parts that do not overlap and that cover every case.

1. Input: the source data is wrong, late, duplicated, or cut at the wrong date.
2. Calculation: a formula, filter, currency rate, or unit changes the data wrongly.
3. Presentation: the label, period, rounding, or chart shows a correct number wrongly.

Next, the evidence. Input and calculation errors are the common causes of a mismatch in period reports [judgment]. No count of cases supports this, so confidence is low. The order of steps follows from this: check the source first, then the logic, then the display.
