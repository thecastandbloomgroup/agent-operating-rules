## Headline

**Choose Northwind Analytics, and fix its thin budget margin in the contract before you sign.**

- Bluepeak costs 455,000, which is 55,000 over the 400,000 budget, and its pricing has no cap.
- Cedar is cheapest at 286,000, but it has no uptime guarantee and depends on one database engineer.
- Northwind costs 372,000 to 399,000 depending on how it counts storage, so it fits the budget with little room.

## Background

Three vendors proposed a data warehouse. The budget is 400,000 USD over three years. All figures are the vendors' own statements [1].

## Relevance

Contract terms bind for three years. A wrong choice either exceeds the budget or leaves the data platform without an uptime guarantee.

## Information

**Evidence**

- Bluepeak's estimate is 455,000 at current query volume, with no price cap. This is 13.75% above budget [1].
- Northwind's licence covers 20 TB. Current data is 14 TB, with growth of about 3 TB per year [1].
- Northwind overage costs 9,000 per extra TB per year [1].
- By year 3, data reaches 20 TB (counted at the start of the year) or 23 TB (counted at the end). The overage is therefore 0 to 27,000, so the total is 372,000 to 399,000.
- The worst case leaves 1,000 of headroom. Growth above about 3.03 TB per year breaks the budget in that case.
- Cedar's hardware holds 30 TB, which covers the 23 TB expected in year 3 [1].
- Cedar has no SLA and one database engineer today [1].
- Cedar leaves 114,000 of headroom. That is 38,000 per year to pay for staff time, which no vendor priced [1]. A full-time engineer likely costs more than that [judgment].
- Benchmarks used a 10 TB sample, not our 14 TB. Median times: Bluepeak 1.3 s, Northwind 2.1 s, Cedar 3.4 s [1].
- No vendor states the price after year 3 [1].

**Scoring.** Bluepeak fails the must-have budget limit, so it is excluded. The table scores the others from 1 to 5 [judgment]. Weights sum to 100. The pass threshold is 300 out of 500.

|---|---|---|---|

Northwind passes the threshold. Cedar does not.

**Assumption log**

A1. Northwind counts overage at the year-end data size. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask Northwind in writing. If wrong: cost drops to 372,000 and headroom grows.

A2. A median query time of 3.0 s or less meets our need. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: ask the main report owners. If wrong: Northwind may fail and Bluepeak or Cedar gain weight.

A3. The 400,000 budget covers vendor costs only. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask finance. If wrong: Cedar's staff cost would count against the budget and weaken it further.

A4. Growth of 3 TB per year holds. Why not measured: it is our team's own estimate [1]. Validate by: check the last 12 months of storage logs. If wrong: Northwind overage rises by 9,000 per TB per year.

**Alternative considered.** Cedar has the lowest cost and the most headroom. It loses because it has no SLA and relies on one engineer [judgment]. Its true cost also stays unknown until someone prices the staff time.
