## Headline

I recommend one decision owner, one verified fact base, and one holding statement before any rebuttal.

- Misalignment is the blocker, so one person must own decisions today.
- The claim of falsehood is unverified, so facts come before any public position.
- Legal, PR, and the board each hold a valid concern, so one brief must serve all three.
- Silence costs more each hour, so a short holding statement should go out while checks run.

## Background

A journalist published that the CEO's statement of yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board each message you with different advice. The material does not say whether the claim is true.

## Relevance

The first day of a story sets its frame [judgment]. Conflicting advice delays the reply and risks a second false or contradictory statement.

## Information

**Evidence**

- Legal usually wants fewer words, to limit liability [judgment].
- PR usually wants speed, to limit reputation harm [judgment].
- The board usually wants control and no surprises [judgment].
- These three goals conflict only on timing and wording, not on facts [inference]. A shared fact base therefore removes most of the conflict.
- Cynefin separates work by how cause and effect relate [1]. The labels in the End section use it.

**Assumption log**

A1. The story is not yet proven true or false. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: compare the CEO's exact words with the source records. If wrong: if the claim is plainly true, Step 5 becomes a correction, not a holding statement.

A2. No law or regulator requires a disclosure within hours. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask Legal for a yes or no. If wrong: add the disclosure to Step 2 and move it first.

A3. The CEO's statement was public and recorded. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: get the transcript or the post. If wrong: Step 3 needs witness notes, which takes longer.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is an immediate firm denial. It loses because A1 is unverified. A denial later shown false adds a second false statement [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these five steps, in this order:

1. **Name one decision owner and one channel.**
   - Increment: a 30-minute call with Legal, PR, and the board chair. It ends with a written note of who decides what.
   - Blocker removed: no one is aligned.
   - Kind: deterministic. Test:
     - Given Legal, PR, and the board have joined the call
     - When the call ends
     - Then one named owner and one channel exist in a written note
   - Cynefin: Clear. Incident command is one known practice, applied by deduction from a known rule.

2. **Issue a document hold.**
   - Increment: Legal sends a notice to keep all records on the statement. This takes under one hour.
   - Blocker removed: loss of evidence, which would harm every later option.
   - Kind: deterministic. Test:
     - Given the notice is sent
     - When each recipient is checked
     - Then every recipient has confirmed receipt in writing
   - Cynefin: Clear. A standard legal practice exists, applied by deduction.
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 1 names the owner.

3. **Check the facts.**
   - Increment: one page that sets each sentence of the CEO's statement against its source record, with a verdict per sentence.
   - Blocker removed: A1, the unknown truth of the claim.
   - Kind: evaluative. Rubric, pass at 80 points:
     - Source is primary (40 points).
     - Two people checked it (30 points).
     - Wording is matched exactly (30 points).
   - Cynefin: Complicated. An expert can answer from the records, so the method is deduction from data.

4. **Brief the board once, in writing.**
   - Increment: a one-page note with the verified facts, the open items, and the next decision time.
   - Blocker removed: separate and conflicting board messages.
   - Kind: evaluative. Rubric, pass at 70 points:
     - Facts match the Step 3 page (50 points).
     - Open items are labeled (30 points).
     - Next decision time is stated (20 points).
   - Cynefin: Complicated. Analysis from the Step 3 data picks the content.
   - Step 4 MUST NOT start until the Step 3 rubric passes.

5. **Run a holding statement as a capped probe.**
   - Increment: two sentences that say the company is checking the facts and will report by a stated time. Legal and PR both approve the wording.
   - Blocker removed: silence while the story spreads.
   - Kind: stochastic. Test:
     - Metric: share of 50 sampled public replies that call the company evasive.
     - Baseline: the share in the first 50 replies before release.
     - Threshold: no rise above baseline.
     - Sample size: 50 replies, 4 hours after release.
   - Stop condition: withdraw and revise if the share rises. Budget: one release and one revision.
   - Cynefin: Complex. The public reaction shows only after release. Induction from trials applies. A new fact from Step 3 could break the pattern.
   - Run this step in parallel with Steps 3 and 4. A full rebuttal or correction MUST NOT go out until the Step 3 rubric passes.
