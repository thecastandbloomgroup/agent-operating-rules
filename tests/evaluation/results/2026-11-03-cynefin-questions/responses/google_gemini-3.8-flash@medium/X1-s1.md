### Information

Factual inconsistency, also called hallucination, affects LLM summarization systems frequently.
Research shows that baseline LLMs produce factual errors in 15% to 30% of summarization tasks without domain tuning [1].
Furthermore, human evaluation remains the gold standard benchmark for summarization quality [2].
