In a corporate communications crisis, contradictory statements increase legal risk and reputation damage [1]. Therefore, organizations require a single point of release [1]. 

A standard response workflow separates fact-finding from public communication [2]. That is, the company audits the factual record before issuing a correction or defense [2].
