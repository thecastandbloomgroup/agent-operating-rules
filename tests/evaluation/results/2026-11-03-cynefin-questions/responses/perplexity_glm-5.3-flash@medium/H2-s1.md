## Headline

Stabilize the messaging first with one owner and one holding line, then build a fact dossier from primary records, then classify the discrepancy in one decision meeting.

1. Containment comes before analysis: several uncoordinated replies create new quotes for follow-up articles [judgment].
2. Every decision needs a shared factual base: the statement text, the article text, and primary records such as recordings and transcripts.
3. The disagreement between legal, PR, and the board comes from three different theories of the case; a fact classification meeting settles which theory applies.
4. A capped media-response probe then shows whether the holding line holds.

## Background

A journalist published a claim yesterday that the CEO's statement was false. Legal, PR, and the board each send messages to the same contact without a shared owner. The response starts while the story is live and facts are unverified.

## Relevance

The first hours after a public accusation decide whether the story grows or stalls [judgment]. An uncoordinated response supplies fresh quotes to journalists and removes control from the release process. Alignment today, not this week, is the blocker.

## Information

**Evidence.** The Chaotic domain rule says: act first to stabilise, because no cause and effect are visible now [18]. The Complicated rule says: bring in experts for analysis when cause and effect are knowable but not yet known [18]. Evaluative decisions need a written rubric before the scoring happens [15]. Confidence: medium; the evidence for the method is strong, but the facts of this case are unverified.

**Assumption log.**

`A1. The journalist's claim points to one specific, checkable statement. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: request the article text today. If wrong: the step 3 rubric changes to a defamation review of the article.`

`A2. No pre-approved crisis protocol with one named spokesperson exists. Why not measured: the query says nobody is aligned, which implies none is active. Validate by: one question to the general counsel before the meeting. If wrong: replace step 1 with activation of that protocol.`

`A3. The CEO's statement was public and a transcript or recording exists. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: pull the recording, transcript, or video in step 2. If wrong: the fact classification in step 3 slows by one session.`

`A4. Legal has not issued a privilege instruction that changes who owns communication. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: confirm with the general counsel in step 1. If wrong: the step 1 owner becomes the legal department, and review spans shrink.`

**Alternative considered.** The strongest alternative is to reject the article at once and go on the attack. It loses because a denial before fact verification can produce a second false statement and doubles the damage [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we run four steps today, in this order.

**Step 1. Containment.** *(Deterministic. Chaotic.)*
Increment: within 2 hours, name one communications owner, issue one approved holding line ("The statement and the article are under review"), and direct every responding team member to use it. Blocker removed: uncoordinated messages reaching journalists and the board. Test, written before the work:

```gherkin
Given the approved holding line and the named owner list
When any team member sends a message to the journalist, other media, staff, or the board
Then the message matches the holding line or routes to the owner for review
```

Cynefin: Chaotic, because no cause and effect are visible while legal, PR, and the board all act at once, so the action is to stabilise first, judged by act-first inference [18].

**Step 2. Evidence pack.** *(Deterministic. Clear.)*
Increment: within 4 hours, compile one document with the statement text, the article text, and one primary record per disputed fact (recording, transcript, video, or document), with a logged gap for each fact that lacks a record. Blocker removed: decisions without a shared factual base. This step waits on step 1 only for the owner to exist. Test, written before the work:

```gherkin
Given the CEO statement text and the article text
When the evidence pack is assembled
Then every disputed fact has one primary record or a logged gap in the pack
```

Cynefin: Clear, because anyone would apply the known rule: gather sources before you judge, deployment by deduction from that rule [18].

**Step 3. Fact classification meeting.** *(Evaluative. Complicated.)*
Increment: one meeting, same day, with legal, PR, and one board representative. Score every disputed fact on this rubric before the meeting starts: source independence (weight 0.4), existence of a primary record (weight 0.4), review by the responsible expert (weight 0.2); a fact counts as misstated only when the score meets the threshold of two independent sources plus a record, otherwise the article stands. Output: one of three labels for the case as a whole: error in the statement, error in the article, or dispute over judgment. Blocker removed: three factions working from three theories of the case. Cynefin: Complicated, because an expert can give the answer from enough data, so the action names the analysis that picks the practice, deployed by deduction from analysis [18].

**Step 4. Media-response probe.** *(Stochastic. Complex.)*
Increment: after steps 2 and 3 pass, release only the agreed line for 24 hours and no further statements, capped at a budget of one release and one owner. Metric: number of follow-up articles that quote new internal sources, beyond the baseline count of 0 quoted internal sources at publication time. Sample size: every article in the 24-hour window. Pass threshold: 1 or fewer follow-up articles with a new internal quote. Stop condition: 3 or more such articles trigger a stop and a re-convene with new evidence. Cynefin: Complex, because the result shows only after the trial, so it is a safe-to-fail probe with a measure, a stop condition, and a budget; inferred by induction, and the pattern breaks if an internal leak starts, which the window measure will show [18]. Leverage is a guess here.

Do not start step 4 until the meeting in step 3 produces a case label. Condition that changes this plan: if step 3 labels the case "error in the statement", replace step 4 with a corrective release under one owner; if it labels "error in the article", replace step 4 with a documented refutation request to the publication.
