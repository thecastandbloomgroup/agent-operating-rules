## Background

A journalist published that a statement from the CEO yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board each send separate messages to the user. The request asks for aligned next steps under time pressure.

## Relevance

The first hours of a crisis set the public record. A fast and accurate response lowers the damage, and a slow or missing one raises it [1].

The situation matches the Chaotic case: harm to the public record happens now, so the first action stabilises before analysis [2].
