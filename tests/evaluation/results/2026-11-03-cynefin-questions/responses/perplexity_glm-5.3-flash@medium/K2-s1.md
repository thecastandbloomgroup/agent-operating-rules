## Background

Three vendor proposals for a data warehouse arrived on 2026-10-10, with stated prices, benchmarks, SLAs, and migration plans [1]. The task is to select one vendor within a 400,000 budget over three years. The current data volume is 14 TB, and our team states growth of about 3 TB per year [1].

## Relevance

The choice locks three years of cost and two to four months of migration effort. Two of the three proposals fail a listed requirement, so the analysis narrows fast and a wrong pick is expensive to reverse.
