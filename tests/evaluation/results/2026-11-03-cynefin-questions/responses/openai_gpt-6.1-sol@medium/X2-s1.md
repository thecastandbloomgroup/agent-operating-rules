## Headline

Replace open-ended retrospectives with a short cycle: identify one obstacle, test one change, and review the result.

- First, check whether ideas fail at the decision, action, or review stage. [judgment]
- Then, test one team-controlled change before replacing the meeting format. [judgment]
- If the obstacle sits outside the team’s control, seek a decision from someone with authority. [judgment]

## Background

People attend your retrospectives, but you report no resulting change. [1] The missing detail is where progress stops after people raise an issue.

## Relevance

Another discussion format does not directly address the gap between discussion and action. [judgment] Therefore, the next retrospective should produce a small test, not a longer list of concerns. [judgment]

## Information

### Find where progress stops

Use three distinct checks. These form a diagnostic guide, not a confirmed diagnosis. [judgment]

| Stage | Evidence to check | Response if this stage fails |
|---|---|---|
| Decision | Did the team choose one specific change? | Replace broad goals with one observable action. |
| Action | Did someone accept responsibility and receive time? | Assign an owner and reserve capacity. |
| Review | Did the team compare results with an agreed measure? | Start the next retrospective with that comparison. |

The Scrum Guide says retrospectives should identify helpful changes and address the most useful improvements promptly. [2] That supports a focus on action, though it does not establish your team’s cause.

**Confidence in the diagnosis: low, because no meeting notes or action records are available.**

### Assumption log

A1. The team controls at least one small work change. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Identify one change requiring no outside approval. If wrong: Seek an external decision instead of assigning another team action.

### Alternative considered

Replace the facilitator or meeting format. This loses as the first step because attendance already occurs, while the reported gap concerns change. [judgment] Revisit this option if people cannot speak safely or the meeting never reaches a decision.

## End

I recommend that we take these three steps.

### 1. Trace the last three retrospectives, or all available if fewer

**Kind:** Deterministic.  
**Test, before work:**

```gherkin
Given the available retrospective records
When the team traces each agreed action
Then each action has a decision, owner, status, and result, or an explicit missing field
```

**Work:** Spend 20 minutes creating that record. If notes do not exist, reconstruct it together and mark uncertain entries.

**Capability added:** A shared record of where progress stops, rather than competing explanations. [judgment]

**Domain:** Complicated. Deduction from the record comparison identifies which stage needs attention.

### 2. Choose one response using the record

**Kind:** Evaluative. Use this weighted decision rubric before choosing:

- Directly addresses the observed failure: **50%**, supported by Step 1.
- Within the responsible person’s authority: **30%**, confirmed by that person.
- Feasible within one work session: **20%**, supported by a time estimate.

Score each criterion from 0 to 2. Pass at **75%**, with no zero for authority.

For the evidence currently available, these provisional scores apply:

| Response | Addresses failure | Authority | Feasible | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Test one owned change | 2 | 2 | 2 | 100% |
| Replace meeting format | 1 | 2 | 2 | 75% |
| Cancel retrospectives | 0 | 2 | 2 | 50% |

These scores are judgments, not measured team results. [judgment] Authority scores depend on A1.

**Work:** After Step 1 passes, spend 15 minutes scoring responses against the actual record. Select one response.

Write its action card:

> By [date], [owner] will change [specific behavior].  
> This addresses [observed obstacle].  
> Compare [measure] before and after.  
> Reserve [time] to complete the action.

**Blocker removed:** An action without a clear owner, scope, or measure. [judgment]

**Domain:** Complicated. Deduction from the weighted comparison selects the response.

### 3. Run a capped trial across three retrospective cycles

**Kind:** Stochastic, because team behavior varies.

**Acceptance test, before work:**

- **Metric:** Cycles ending with a completed action and a recorded result.
- **Baseline:** Your report of no resulting change; verify the record in Step 1.
- **Threshold:** At least two successful cycles out of three.
- **Sample:** Three consecutive cycles.

These are proposed trial limits, not validated benchmarks. [judgment]

**Work:** Start only after Step 2 passes. Review the previous action before discussing new concerns.

Limit each action to one work session. Reserve no more than 30 minutes per retrospective for this trial.

**Stop condition:** After two consecutive missed actions, stop adding actions and address capacity or authority.

**Capability added:** Evidence that the team can complete and assess changes. [judgment]

**Domain:** Complex. Induction from three trials informs the next decision; workload changes or staff changes can break the pattern.

Leverage remains a guess. Three cycles test whether this routine works locally; they do not establish a lasting effect.
