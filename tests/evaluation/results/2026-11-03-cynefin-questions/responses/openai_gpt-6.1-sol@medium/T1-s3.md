### 1. Adopt a one-page review agreement

**Kind:** Deterministic.

### 3. Run a capped culture trial

**Dependency:** Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 2 passes its tests.

**Kind:** Stochastic, because team behavior varies.

**Acceptance test first:**

- **Baseline:** Count formatting-only blocking comments across the last 10 SQL pull requests.
- **Sample:** Observe the next 10 SQL pull requests.
- **Metric:** Compare formatting-only blocking comments per pull request.
- **Pass threshold:** Reduce that rate by at least 75%, with no unresolved concrete readability concern.
- **Zero-baseline rule:** If the baseline is zero, revisit A1 rather than claim improvement.

**Increment:** Run the trial for at most two weeks, with at most one additional team-hour for review.

Stop early if the formatter changes query behavior or blocks supported SQL. At the end, inspect disputed comments with both authors and reviewers. Accept the agreement only after the test passes.

**Capability added:** The team can revise the process using observed results rather than another preference debate. [judgment]
