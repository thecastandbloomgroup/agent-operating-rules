### 2. Establish the baseline and choose the error to fix

**Kind:** Evaluative.

| Criterion | Weight | Required evidence | Score |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Accuracy | 50% | Each claim checked against the source snapshot | 0: major error; 1: minor error; 2: no error |
| Coverage | 30% | Required facts listed before the summary review | 0: major omission; 1: minor omission; 2: complete |
| Usefulness | 20% | Agent review and timed correction task | 0: rewrite; 1: edit; 2: usable unchanged |
