### Relevance

### Information

Language models generate hallucinations because their training objective optimizes likelihood rather than factual correctness [1].
Empirical benchmarks show that retrieval-augmented grounding reduces hallucination rates below 5% compared to over 20% in unconstrained generation [2].
Furthermore, explicit permission to say "I do not know" reduces fabricated claims [3].
