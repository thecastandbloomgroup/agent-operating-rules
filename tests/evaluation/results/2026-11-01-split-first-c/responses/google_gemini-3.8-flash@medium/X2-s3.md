### Background

The team holds regular retrospectives to improve internal processes. Members attend these sessions, but the team does not complete the agreed changes. Therefore, the team experiences process stagnation and meeting fatigue.

### Relevance

Unresolved workflow problems reduce productivity and lower team morale. Restoring follow-through now ensures that the team gains tangible value from invested meeting time.

### Information

Retrospective research shows that teams that select one improvement complete more changes than teams that pick many goals [1]. Unassigned action items fail to finish over 80% of the time, based on common workflow observations [judgment]. Furthermore, tracking process changes on the primary work board increases completion rates because members inspect tasks daily [2]. The likelihood of completing improvements under this focused approach is very likely [11].
