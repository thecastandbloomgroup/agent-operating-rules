Yes, enable gzip for this API, at a mid compression level (4 to 6). Turning it on is low risk and it applies to the exact case you have: large, text based payloads. The 400 KB average sits well above the size where compression pays for itself [judgment]. The main cost is server CPU, which a load test can confirm before full rollout.

Gzip is a standard HTTP content coding. Clients agree to it through the Accept-Encoding header, and servers answer with Content-Encoding [1]. Typical compression on JSON text lands near 20 to 30 percent of the original size [inference], so a 400 KB response should arrive as roughly 80 to 120 KB. Responses under about 1 KB gain little [judgment]. Your payload size gives strong headroom.
