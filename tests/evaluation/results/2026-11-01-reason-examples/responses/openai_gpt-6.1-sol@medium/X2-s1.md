## Information

**Evidence:** Retrospectives aim to produce improvements, not just discussion; Derby and Larsen include “Decide What to Do” in their structure. [2]

**Working diagnosis:** Check four separate stages. [judgment]

- **Choice:** Did the team select a specific change?
- **Ownership:** Did someone accept responsibility and have authority?
- **Execution:** Did the team reserve time and complete the action?
- **Review:** Did anyone check whether the action helped?

These are audit prompts, not questions that require answers before proceeding.

## End

### 1. Audit the last three available retrospectives

**Test first, deterministic:**

```text
Given the available retrospective notes
When the team checks each agreed action
Then each action shows its owner, status, and evidence of effect
And missing information appears as "not recorded"
```

Spend 20 minutes creating this action list. Mark where each action stopped: choice, ownership, execution, or review.

This removes the evidence gap without assigning blame. [judgment]

**Domain: Clear**, because the task extracts facts using fixed fields.

### 2. Run one small change trial

Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 passes.

**Acceptance test first, stochastic:** The result varies because people and work conditions vary.

- **Metric:** Action completion plus one observable work outcome.
- **Baseline:** Record the current outcome before the trial.
- **Pass threshold:** Complete the action and meet a target agreed before work starts.
- **Sample:** One action, one ten-working-day trial, and one review.
- **Limit:** Treat success as grounds for another trial, not proof of cause. [judgment]

Choose one issue within the team’s control. Write this action card:

> By [date], [owner] will change [specific practice] to improve [observable outcome] from [baseline] to [target].

Reserve the work time immediately. Cap the trial at two staff-hours and ten working days. Stop if it exceeds either limit or causes harm.

This adds a feasible change instead of another list of intentions. [judgment]

**Domain: Complex**, because the effect becomes visible only after the trial. Its leverage remains a guess. [judgment]

### 3. Review the result before adding another action

Step 3 follows the trial’s deadline or stop condition.

**Test first, deterministic:**

```text
Given the action card and trial evidence
When the next retrospective starts
Then the team records completion and the observed outcome
And selects keep, revise, or stop
And records a reason supported by that evidence
```

Use the first ten minutes for this review. If the action stalled, identify the missing condition rather than automatically carrying it forward.

This creates a record of results and prevents silent abandonment. [judgment]

**Domain: Clear**, because the review follows a fixed evidence checklist.
