### Background

A journalist published an article stating that yesterday's CEO statement was false. Legal, public relations (PR), and board members are sending uncoordinated messages. The organization currently lacks an aligned response strategy.

### Relevance

Uncoordinated statements create severe legal liability and brand damage [judgment]. An immediate control process stops contradictory messages before they reach the public [judgment].

### Information

This section covers the evidence, assumptions, and alternatives.

The organization faces a public relations crisis. Coombs shows that quick, consistent communication prevents reputational collapse during corporate crises [1]. Conflicting statements from leadership increase public distrust and legal risk [judgment]. Therefore, leadership must centralize communication authority immediately [judgment].

A1. The CEO statement contains disputed factual claims. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Reviewing the audio recording and the published text. If wrong: Disputed statements require simple retractions instead of legal defense.

A2. No staff member has sent an external rebuttal yet. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Polling PR and Legal channel leads. If wrong: Correct unauthorized statements in the follow-up release.

Alternative considered:
Allow PR to issue an immediate public denial [judgment]. This alternative loses because denying claims without verified facts exposes the firm to defamation or regulatory penalties [judgment].
