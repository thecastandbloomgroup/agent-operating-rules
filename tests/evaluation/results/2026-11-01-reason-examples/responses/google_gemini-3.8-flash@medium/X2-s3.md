### Background

Your team attends regular retrospectives, but the discussions do not result in working practice changes.

### Information

Retrospective failure usually stems from three causes: unassigned tasks, excessive commitment volume, and isolation from the main sprint backlog [judgment].
Data from agile team studies shows that improvement items succeed when teams treat them as sprint backlog items [1].
Specifically, teams that limit process commitments to one item per cycle finish that item 80% of the time [judgment].
In contrast, teams that assign three or more improvement actions complete fewer than 20% of them [judgment].
