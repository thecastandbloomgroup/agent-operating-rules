### Headline

Establish an immediate internal communication freeze and verify the facts before releasing any statement.
First, a mandatory holding instruction prevents conflicting statements from staff.
Next, an audit of the CEO's statement against records establishes ground truth.
Finally, an incident group with Legal, PR, and the board chair executes an agreed response.

### Background

A journalist published an article claiming that your CEO made a false statement yesterday.
Legal, PR, and board members are sending uncoordinated messages without consensus on the response.
You must guide leadership toward a single coordinated position under time pressure [judgment].

### Relevance

Uncoordinated statements create legal liability and harm market credibility [judgment].
Therefore, establishing rapid command now protects the organization from contradictory public statements.

### Information

Crisis communication standards require immediate containment to halt divergent messaging before factual verification [1].
Furthermore, factual audit protocols prevent the retraction of hasty follow-up statements [2].

#### Assumptions

A1. You have the authority or access to call an emergency response meeting. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Send a meeting notice to the three stakeholder leads. If wrong: Escalate directly to the CEO or board chair to mandate the meeting.
A2. The journalist has not yet published a second story. Why not measured: Real-time media tracking is not provided. Validate by: Check news feeds and PR media monitoring tools. If wrong: Shorten response windows from hours to thirty minutes.

#### Alternative Considered

Release an immediate public denial drafted by PR. This alternative loses because issuing unverified denials creates severe legal liability if the journalist's claim proves true [judgment].

### End

I recommend that we:

1. Enforce an immediate internal communications freeze.
   - Cynefin domain: Chaotic, because public narrative risk spreads rapidly without visible structure until you halt uncoordinated messaging.
   - Capability added: This action stops unauthorized statements to media and employees.
   - Increment: Send an urgent directive to Legal, PR, the board, and executive staff within fifteen minutes.
   - Test (Deterministic):
     Given uncoordinated internal messaging across departments,
     When leadership distributes the mandatory freeze directive,
     Then all recipients confirm receipt and cease external contact.

2. Verify the statement against primary corporate records.
   - Cynefin domain: Complicated, because expert document review can determine the truth of the statement.
   - Capability added: This action establishes documented facts before any external response.
   - Increment: Review the CEO's exact words, supporting data, and the journalist's claims in one sixty-minute session.
   - Test (Evaluative):
     Use a Kepner-Tregoe rubric [3]. Weight accuracy at 50%, legal risk at 30%, and reputational impact at 20%. The pass threshold is 100% agreement on verified facts between Legal and PR based on primary source records.

3. Convene an emergency response team to adopt a single holding statement.
   - Cynefin domain: Complicated, because structured negotiation between Legal, PR, and the board chair resolves policy trade-offs.
   - Capability added: This action aligns leadership on one approved position.
   - Increment: Hold a thirty-minute decision meeting with one lead each from Legal, PR, and the board. Step 3 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 2 passes.
   - Test (Evaluative):
     Use a Kepner-Tregoe rubric [3]. Criteria are factual truth (pass/fail), legal clearance (pass/fail), and board chair approval (pass/fail). The pass threshold requires unanimous approval on all three criteria.

4. Publish the approved response through PR to the journalist and public channels.
   - Cynefin domain: Clear, because sending a signed-off statement follows standard PR distribution procedures.
   - Capability added: This action delivers a defensible public record.
   - Increment: Distribute the statement and log all media inquiries in one operational session. Step 4 MUST NOT start until Step 3 approves the text.
   - Test (Deterministic):
     Given an approved holding statement from the response team,
     When PR transmits the text to the journalist and company channels,
     Then PR confirms delivery and records no deviations from the approved text.

### Follow-up

If A1 is false, ask the board chair to mandate the freeze in Step 1 immediately.
