### Background

The team experiences friction during code reviews because members disagree on SQL formatting. You asked for a solution to fix this culture problem. This response provides a technical and procedural plan to remove formatting debates entirely.

### Relevance

Formatting debates waste engineering hours and erode psychological safety during reviews. Resolving this issue immediately restores focus to logic, performance, and schema design.

### Information
