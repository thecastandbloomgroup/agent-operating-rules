### Background

The support tool uses a large language model to create ticket summaries for human agents.
Support agents report that generated summaries are wrong without systematic details [1].
The engineering team currently lacks metrics and evaluation datasets to quantify summary accuracy.

### Relevance

Inaccurate summaries cause support agents to give incorrect advice or spend excess time verifying raw ticket logs.
Therefore, establishing accuracy metrics is urgent to prevent customer escalations and agent distrust.
