Background
Agents use a large language model (LLM) to summarise support tickets. They say some summaries are wrong. The tool stores no quality metric. The request is to make the summariser better.

Relevance
Hand edits hide the cost of bad summaries. Therefore a change with no baseline can leave the same complaint in place. The first gain is a shared definition of wrong.
