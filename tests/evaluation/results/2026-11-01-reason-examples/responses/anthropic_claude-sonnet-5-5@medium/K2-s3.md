Choose Northwind Analytics, and confirm its overage cost before you sign.

- Northwind costs 399,000 at 23 TB, which is inside the 400,000 budget by 1,000.
- Bluepeak costs 455,000 before data growth and has no price cap, so it fails the budget.

## Background

The team must pick one data warehouse from three proposals. The budget is 400,000 USD over three years. The current data is 14 TB (terabytes) and grows about 3 TB per year [1].

## Relevance

The proposals expire into a contract that locks cost for three years. A wrong pick either breaks the budget or leaves the team without a safe way to run the system.

## Information

**Cost at projected volume.** The data reaches 17, 20, and 23 TB at the end of years 1, 2, and 3 (14 TB plus 3 TB per year).

Northwind covers up to 20 TB in the fixed licence.
- Year 3 peaks at 23 TB, so 3 extra TB cost 27,000 (3 × 9,000).
- The total is 372,000 + 27,000 = 399,000 [1].

Bluepeak's estimate is 455,000 at current query volume. That is 55,000 (13.75%) over budget. Pricing has no cap, and storage grows with the data [1].

Cedar costs 286,000. Its hardware holds 30 TB, so growth adds no cost [1].

**Comparison.**

| Criterion | Northwind | Bluepeak | Cedar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost at 23 TB (USD) | 399,000 | 455,000 or more | 286,000 |
| Median query time (vendor, 10 TB) | 2.1 s | 1.3 s | 3.4 s |
| Availability guarantee (SLA) | 99.9% | 99.95% | None |
| Exit cost | None | 0.08 per GB | None |
| Migration | 10 weeks, vendor | 16 weeks, our team | 12 weeks, partner |

SLA means service level agreement, a promised uptime with credits if missed. Source for all rows: proposals.txt, each proposal [1].

**Why not Cedar.** Cedar saves 113,000 over Northwind, about 37,700 per year. Cedar has no SLA, and one database engineer would carry all operations [1]. A second engineer likely costs more than 37,700 per year [judgment]. So the saving likely disappears. Cedar is also 62% slower than Northwind on the vendor benchmark (3.4 s against 2.1 s) [1].

**Why not Bluepeak.** It is the fastest and has 24/7 support. However, it breaks the budget and has no cap [1].

**Uncertainty.** The Northwind total stays under 400,000 with about as likely as not odds (33 to 66%) [judgment]. A growth rate of 3.2 TB per year instead of 3.0 raises the total to about 402,600 (3.4 TB × 9,000 extra).

A2. The current team can run Northwind without a new hire. Why not measured: no vendor states staff time. Validate by: ask Northwind for a runbook and review it with our engineer. If wrong: add staff cost, and re-score Northwind against Cedar.

A4. A second engineer costs more than 37,700 per year. Why not measured: the material gives no salary data. Validate by: ask Human Resources for the loaded cost of the role. If wrong: Cedar becomes cost-competitive, so re-score it.

**Alternative considered.** Cedar with a second hire is the strongest alternative. It loses because the hire erases the saving, and it still has no SLA [judgment].
