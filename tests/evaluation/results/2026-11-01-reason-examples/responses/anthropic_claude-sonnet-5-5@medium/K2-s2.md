## Background

Three vendors proposed a data warehouse. The budget is 400,000 USD over three years. The task is to recommend one vendor.

All figures come from the vendors and our team (proposals.txt). No vendor states the staff cost to run the system or the price after year 3.

## Relevance

The decision commits the budget for three years. A wrong choice either exceeds the budget or leaves us without uptime cover.

## Information

**Evidence**

- Northwind costs 372,000. The licence covers 20 TB, and each extra TB costs 9,000 per year (proposals.txt, Proposal 1). [1]
- Data grows from 14 TB by about 3 TB per year. It reaches 23 TB by the end of year 3. That is 3 TB over the cap, or 27,000 extra, so the total is 399,000 (A1). [1]
- Bluepeak estimates 455,000 at current volume. That is 13.75% over budget, with no cap on consumption (proposals.txt, Proposal 2). [1]
- Bluepeak has the fastest median query time, 1.3 s against 2.1 s for Northwind. That is 38% faster. [1]
- Bluepeak asks our team to do the migration, which takes 16 weeks. The vendor states no cost for that staff time. [1]
- Cedar costs 286,000, which leaves 114,000 of headroom. Its median query time is 3.4 s, which is 62% slower than Northwind. [1]
- Cedar has no SLA (service level agreement, a written uptime promise). Our team has one database engineer (proposals.txt, Proposal 3). [1]
- Cedar needs added staff, and this cost is not in the quote. Half of one engineer at 75,000 per year adds 225,000, so the total is about 511,000 (A3). [judgment]
- Theme: all three sources of cost risk are unpriced items. These are growth, staff time, and the price after year 3. Only one document supports this, so confidence is moderate.
