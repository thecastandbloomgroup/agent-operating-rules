## Headline

Verify the facts first, then name one decision owner, then choose one response, and say nothing public until that is done.

- The facts decide everything else, and nobody has confirmed them yet.
- The conflict between Legal, PR, and the board comes from missing decision rights, not from missing advice.
- A short holding line stops new damage while the team checks the facts.
- A scored comparison of response options replaces the argument between functions.

## Background

A journalist published a report that the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board each send you separate guidance. No one has confirmed which parts of the report are accurate.

## Relevance

Each hour without one position raises the chance of a second inaccurate statement. Today is Sunday, so the news cycle on Monday morning sets the deadline [judgment].

## Information

**Evidence**
- Confidence that the statement is false: low confidence, because no transcript or article was seen. See A1.
- Conflicting advice from three groups usually signals unclear decision rights, not a lack of expertise [judgment].
- The Cynefin framework tells you to probe or gather facts first when cause and effect are unclear [1].

**Assumption log**

A1. The CEO statement is false in whole or in part. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: compare the transcript with the documents the article cites. If wrong: skip the correction option in Step 3.

A2. You have the authority to name a decision owner, or can get it from the board chair. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: check the governance charter or ask the chair. If wrong: the chair names the owner in Step 2.

A3. The company is not under a public-disclosure deadline. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: Legal confirms in one message. If wrong: the Legal deadline sets the timing of Step 3.

**Alternative considered**
- Deny the report at once, as PR or the CEO may prefer.
- It loses because a denial before the fact check risks a second false statement [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these four actions in order.

1. **Build a one-page fact sheet within 60 minutes.**
   - Increment: list what the CEO said, the source records for each claim, and which claims in the article are true, false, or unknown.
   - Blocker removed: no one knows what is true.
   - Kind: deterministic. Test written first:
     - Given the CEO transcript and the article
     - When each claim is matched to a source record
     - Then every claim is marked true, false, or unknown with a record name
   - Domain: Confused, because the facts needed to classify the situation are not yet known.

2. **Name one decision owner and issue one holding line.**
   - Increment: send one message to Legal, PR, and the board chair. It names the owner and states that nothing goes public before the fact sheet is signed. The holding line reads: "We are reviewing the report and will update by a stated time."
   - Blocker removed: three groups give three directions.
   - Kind: deterministic. Test written first:
     - Given the three groups have received the message
     - When each group replies
     - Then each reply confirms the owner and the rule in writing
   - Domain: Clear, because the governance charter gives one known rule for who decides.
   - Step 2 MUST NOT send the holding line until Legal approves its wording. Run Step 2 in parallel with Step 1.

3. **Choose the response with a scored comparison.**
   - Increment: score four options (correct, clarify, stand by, no comment) in a Pugh matrix [2], with "no comment" as the baseline.
   - Blocker removed: the argument between functions has no shared standard.
   - Kind: evaluative. Rubric written first, with weights out of 100:
     - Accuracy against the fact sheet: 40
     - Legal exposure: 25
     - Trust of staff and public: 20
     - Speed: 15
     - Pass threshold: 70, with evidence for each score taken from the fact sheet or Legal's written view.
   - Domain: Complicated, because more than one practice fits and expert analysis must pick one.
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start until the fact sheet in Step 1 is signed.

4. **Send the board a one-page written update.**
   - Increment: state the facts, the chosen option, the owner, and the next update time.
   - Blocker removed: board members message you directly because they lack one source of truth.
   - Kind: deterministic. Test written first:
     - Given the option chosen in Step 3
     - When the update is sent to all board members
     - Then each member receives the same text and the next update time
   - Domain: Clear, because a standard board update format exists.
   - Step 4 MUST NOT start until Step 3 passes its threshold.

## Follow-up

Conditions that change the recommendation:
- If Step 1 shows the statement is false, replace Step 3 with an immediate correction, approved by Legal.
- If A3 is false, Legal's disclosure deadline sets the time for Steps 3 and 4.
