## Background

A retrospective (retro) is a recurring team meeting where the team reviews its work and agrees on changes. Your team attends retros, but no change follows. The request does not state team size, the retro format, or what happened to past action items.

## Relevance

Each retro without change costs the team meeting time and lowers trust in the next retro. Attendance alone hides this loss until people stop caring.
