## Background

Hallucination is output that sounds correct but is false or has no support. You asked for one prompt that stops it. The prompt below applies the two techniques that the vendor documentation lists first.

## Relevance

A confident false answer costs more than a refusal. This prompt trades some coverage for fewer false claims.

## Information

```text

Do not use outside knowledge.
Do not guess names, numbers, dates, or links.

Documents:
[paste source text here]
```
