## Headline

I recommend a fact check first, then one owner for all statements, then a scored choice of response.

- The facts decide whether to correct, clarify, or defend.
- One owner stops conflicting messages from creating a second error.
- A shared scoring table aligns Legal, public relations (PR), and the board.
- One approved message closes the gap between all groups.

## Background

A journalist published a claim that the statement by the chief executive officer (CEO) yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board send you conflicting advice. The request does not say whether the claim is accurate.

## Relevance

Every unaligned message adds a chance of a new contradiction. The first 24 hours usually set the story [judgment].

## Information

**Evidence**

- Confidence that the claim is accurate: low confidence, because the request gives no facts.
- Three groups give three goals: Legal limits liability, PR limits reputation harm, and the board limits governance risk [judgment]. No group disputes the need for facts.

**Assumption log**

A1. The CEO statement exists as a transcript or recording. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the communications team for the text. If wrong: Step 1 starts with an interview of the CEO.

A2. You can convene all three groups today. Why not measured: the material does not state your authority. Validate by: ask the board chair. If wrong: Step 2 asks the board chair to name the owner.

A3. No law or listing rule sets a disclosure deadline. Why not measured: the material does not state the company type. Validate by: Legal confirms in Step 1. If wrong: the legal deadline replaces the timing in Step 3.

**Alternative considered**

- Defend the CEO now. It loses because a second false statement compounds the harm if the claim is accurate [judgment].
- Stay silent. It loses because silence leaves the journalist's version unchallenged [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these four steps. Cynefin domains follow Snowden and Boone:

[1]

1. **Verify the facts (Confused).** The domain is Confused because the request does not say which sentence is disputed or which records exist.
   - Increment: within two hours, one named person compares the CEO statement with source records and the article.
   - Blocker removed: nobody knows if the claim is true.
   - Test (deterministic):
     - Given the CEO statement and the article claim are listed side by side
     - When each sentence is checked against a source record
     - Then each sentence is marked accurate, inaccurate, or unverified, with a named source

2. **Name one owner and freeze outside comment (Clear).** The domain is Clear because a single spokesperson is a known practice. Step 2 MAY run in parallel with Step 1.
   - Increment: send a one-paragraph written instruction to Legal, PR, and the board chair.
   - Blocker removed: conflicting messages.
   - Test (deterministic):
     - Given the instruction names one owner
     - When any group receives a press inquiry
     - Then that group forwards it to the owner and does not reply

3. **Choose the response (Complicated).** The domain is Complicated because more than one response fits and analysis must pick one. Step 3 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 1 passes.
   - Increment: score three options (correct, clarify, defend) in one meeting with Legal, PR, and the board chair.
   - Blocker removed: no shared basis for the decision.
   - Test (evaluative), using Kepner-Tregoe decision analysis:

[2]

   | Criterion | Weight | Evidence needed |
   |---|---|---|
   | Factual accuracy | 40 | Step 1 result |
   | Legal exposure | 25 | Written Legal opinion |
   | Trust of key audiences | 20 | PR notes on customers and staff |
   | Speed | 15 | Time to publish |

   The pass threshold is 70 of 100, with a nonzero score on factual accuracy. The weights are [judgment].

4. **Send one approved message (Clear).** The domain is Clear because one identical text for all groups is a known practice. Step 4 MUST NOT start until an option meets the threshold in Step 3.
   - Increment: the owner sends the same text to the board, staff, and the journalist.
   - Blocker removed: groups acting on different versions.
   - Test (deterministic):

[3]

     - Given the approved text is final
     - When the owner sends it
     - Then all three recipient groups hold the same text, and the owner logs the send time

If A3 is false, the legal deadline replaces the timing in Step 3. If Step 1 shows the statement is accurate, remove "correct" from the Step 3 options.
