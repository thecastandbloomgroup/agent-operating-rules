Analysis points:

2. Bluepeak is the fastest at 1.3 s median and has the best SLA (99.95%) [1]. However, its base estimate already exceeds the budget by 55k, and the uncapped model adds risk [1].
3. Cedar is cheapest at 286k. However, it has no SLA, the slowest queries at 3.4 s, and the next-day severity-1 support [1]. Uptime rests on one database engineer [1]. That is a single point of failure [judgment].
4. Migration favors Northwind: vendor-run, 10 weeks. Cedar needs a partner for 12 weeks. Bluepeak needs 16 weeks of in-house team time [1].
5. Exit: Northwind and Cedar have no exit fee. Bluepeak charges 0.08 per GB egress, which is about 1,600 for 20 TB [1].
6. Staff time cost and post-year-3 pricing are missing from every proposal [1]. This gap applies to all vendors, so it does not change the ranking.
