# Headline

Retrospectives fail when discussion has no link to owned, tested change. Fix this by cutting action items to one per session, assigning a named owner, and opening each retro by reviewing the last action. Add one probe to learn why attendance without change happens.

- Cause one: too many vague actions, so none completes [judgment].
- Cause two: no owner or deadline, so follow-through has no mechanism [judgment].
- Cause three: low safety or facilitator fatigue, which needs a probe, not a fix [judgment].

# Background

Your retrospectives run on schedule and people attend. The output does not change how the team works. The request is a plan to restore their effect.

# Relevance

A retro that produces no change still costs the team one meeting per cycle. Over a quarter, that is hours spent with zero return, and attendance alone now hides the failure.

# Information

Known pattern: retros decay when action items lack owners, when the list is too long, or when people do not feel safe to name real problems [judgment, no source in material]. The material states attendance is fine, so safety and structure are the suspects, not participation.

Domain: Complicated, because more than one format fits and the team picks by trial.
