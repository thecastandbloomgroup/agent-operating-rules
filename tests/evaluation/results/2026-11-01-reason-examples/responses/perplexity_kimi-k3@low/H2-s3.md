Background

A journalist published a claim that the CEO's statement yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are messaging separately, and no one is aligned. The requester asks for next steps.

Relevance

Speed matters because outlets follow up within hours. However, a fast wrong answer is worse than a slow right one, so coordination comes before speed.

The situation has two unknowns: whether the CEO statement was false, and whether the journalist's evidence is solid. The policy, audience, and risk tolerance of the company are not in the material, so the steps below assume a standard public-company crisis posture.
