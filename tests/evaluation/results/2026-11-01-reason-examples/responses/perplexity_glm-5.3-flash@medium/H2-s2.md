## Headline

Freeze all outside statements now, verify the facts before any reply, and give one coordinator ownership of the message.
- Legal, PR, and the board each get one owner and one approval channel, so their messages stop conflicting.
- No reply goes out until a fact memo says whether the CEO statement was actually false.
- The written response follows the memo, chosen with a scored rubric and released as a small, measured test.

## Background

A journalist published that our CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board all message you without a shared plan. The facts behind the statement are not yet checked.

## Relevance

Every extra hour with three owners sends mixed signals to the press and grows legal exposure. The first few actions decide whether the story stops or grows.

## Information

The recommendation depends on one fact that no one has checked, so state the uncertainty first: confidence in the freeze step is high, because mixed messages have one known bad outcome [judgment]. Confidence in the response plan is lower until the fact memo is done [judgment].

Assumptions:

- A1. The CEO statement contains at least one factual error. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: the fact memo in Step 2, the cheapest check. If wrong: a rebuttal to the article replaces Steps 3 and 4 [inference].
- A2. No single coordinator exists today. Why not measured: the material says nobody is aligned. Validate by: ask each team whether anyone answers the press. If wrong: Step 1 shrinks to a routing check.
- A3. The journalist checked her evidence before publication. Why not measured: no tool can reach the journalist. Validate by: ask PR for any prior contact with her. If wrong: her case is weaker, and the pressure drops [inference].

Alternative considered: answer the article at once with a denial. It loses without verified facts, because a wrong denial repeats the first failure [inference].

## End

I recommend that we run these four steps in order. Step 1 must start within one hour.

1. **Freeze and stabilise** (Chaotic, deterministic). Name one coordinator and one press spokesperson among legal, PR, and the board. Send a legal hold notice that keeps all related documents. Blocker removed: conflicting messages and lost evidence. Test written first:

```gherkin
Given the freeze notice,
When any team member drafts external text,
Then the coordinator approves it before release.
```

Cynefin: Chaotic, because no one can yet see which move calms the story, so the work is to stabilise [18].

2. **Verify the facts** (Confused, deterministic). One fact-finder writes a one-page memo within 24 hours. Each claim in the CEO statement gets a label: true, false, or unverified, with a source. Blocker removed: the shared doubt that stops the three teams from aligning. Test written first:

```gherkin
Given the CEO statement and the article,
When the fact-finder marks each claim,
Then each claim carries one label and one source.
```

Cynefin: Confused, because the right response depends on facts that only this check can place [18]. Step 2 MUST NOT start before the Step 1 freeze.

3. **Align and choose** (Complicated, evaluative). Hold one meeting of legal, PR, and one board rep after the memo. Score three response options with a Pugh matrix [16]: correct the statement, stand by it, or reply without comment. The rubric comes before the meeting and states the criteria, their weights, the pass threshold, and the evidence each criterion needs. Suggested criteria: legal risk, press trust, and speed [judgment]. Blocker removed: the teams now agree on one scored decision. Cynefin: Complicated, because analysis must pick one practice among several good ones [18]. Step 3 MUST NOT start until the Step 2 memo is done.

4. **Release and measure** (Complex, stochastic). Publish the chosen response as a safe-to-fail probe with a budget. Metric: the share of follow-up coverage that repeats the correction. Baseline: the share of follow-up coverage that repeats the false claim, measured from today's articles. Sample: the next 20 follow-up articles. Pass threshold: at least 60% carry the correction. Stop condition: pause and reassess if any follow-up misstates the response. Blocker added: measured proof of the response's effect. Cynefin: Complex, because the press effect is visible only after release, so leverage is a guess [18]. Step 4 MUST NOT start until the Step 3 decision passes its threshold.
