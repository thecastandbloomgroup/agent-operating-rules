## Background

P95 latency rose from 120 ms to 900 ms after release 2026.10.21. The release changed the ORM layer, cache TTL, and the connection pool. Logs, traces, and the diff are attached.

## Relevance

Error rate rose from 0.02% to 0.40%, and 96% of errors are PoolTimeout [1]. Every hour unfixed means 1,800 to 2,400 pool-exhausted warnings per hour [1].

## Information

Quantitative evidence:

- db.pool.acquire p95: 3 ms in window A to 690 ms in B, a 687 ms rise. Total p95 rose 780 ms. Pool wait explains 88% of the rise [3].
- Pool config: max_size fell from 50 to 8; acquire timeout fell from 30 s to 5 s [2].
- Capacity: 32 worker threads share 8 connections, so 24 threads always queue under load [2].
- DB side health: peak CPU 41% versus 38% before; peak active connections 8 versus 31 before; db.query spans unchanged (orders p95 52 to 58 ms) [3].
- Cache miss rate rose from 12% to 31% after the TTL cut from 300 s to 60 s, but cache.get is 5 ms and the extra misses add about 35 ms per request at most [inference from 3].

Alternative considered: blame the selectinload ORM change. It loses because the two query spans are as fast as before (orders 58 ms p95, order_lines 26 ms p95 in B) and the added span is one extra query, not slow queries [3]. The selectinload change is fine to keep.
