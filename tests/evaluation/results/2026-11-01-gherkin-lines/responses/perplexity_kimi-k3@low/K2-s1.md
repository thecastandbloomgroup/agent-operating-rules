- Northwind costs about 399,000 over three years after the year-three storage overage is added; Bluepeak exceeds the budget at 455,000, and Cedar is cheapest at 286,000 but carries uptime risk [1].

## Background

Three vendor proposals for a data warehouse are on file, with a three-year budget of 400,000 USD. A recommendation among them is required.

## Relevance

The choice commits the budget for three years now. A wrong choice either breaks the budget or carries reliability risk.
