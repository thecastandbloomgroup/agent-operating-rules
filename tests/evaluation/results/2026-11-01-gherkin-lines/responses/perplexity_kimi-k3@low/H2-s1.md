## Headline

Stabilize first: freeze all replies, name one incident owner, verify the facts today, then correct or defend within 24 hours.

- Control: a single owner and one channel end the unaligned messages from Legal, PR, and the board.
- Facts: only primary records can settle whether the statement was false, and the response type depends on that finding.
- Law: the wording of any reply sets legal exposure, so counsel reviews it before release.
- Timing: a short holding statement (a first reply that states only what is known) within four hours keeps every later option open.

## Background

A journalist published a report that calls the CEO's statement from yesterday false. Legal, PR, and the board are messaging without alignment, and next steps are requested.

## Relevance

The first hours decide whether this stays a one-day story or becomes a credibility and legal record that cannot be withdrawn [judgment]. Contradicting replies from three parties would create that record.

## Information

Evidence:

- For situations with no visible cause and effect, the Cynefin framework directs action that stabilizes before analysis [1].
- Crisis research supports one spokesperson and a fast first reply that uses only verified facts [2].
- Silence lets third parties define the event and raises reputational damage [2].
- If the company is public, a false public statement can trigger the securities antifraud rule [3]. That rule is a United States ban on false statements tied to share trading.
- The 24-hour deadline in Step 5 is a judgment call: fast corrections reach the same audience, and delay widens the gap [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take five steps:

1. **Step 1, within one hour.** Freeze all outbound replies about the article. Appoint one incident lead who chairs a joint cell of Legal, PR, and the board. Blocker removed: unaligned messaging. Domain: Chaotic, because no shared cause and effect exists and order must be restored before analysis. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:

2. **Step 2, within two hours.** Preserve the record: the CEO statement, the article, and the data behind the statement, in timestamped copies with access limited to the cell. Capability added: a fixed evidence base. Domain: Clear, because preservation is a known best practice with one right form. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
Given the statement, the article, and the source data,
When the record pack is sealed,
Then each item has an unedited, timestamped copy that only cell members can open.

3. **Step 3, today.** Verify each factual claim in the statement against primary records. Mark each claim supported, unsupported, or disputed. Capability added: the finding that selects correction or defense. Domain: Complicated, because experts can trace each claim to a record. Kind: evaluative. Rubric, written first [4]: criteria are the truth value of each material claim. Weights follow claim materiality: high, medium, or low. Pass rule: a claim passes only when a primary record supports it. Threshold: the statement is false when any high-materiality claim fails. Step 3 MUST NOT conclude until the test in Step 2 passes.

4. **Step 4, after Step 3.** Counsel sets the response posture and the legal risk note. Scope: securities exposure if the company is public, plus defamation risk in both directions. Blocker removed: unpriced legal risk in the reply. Domain: Complicated, because counsel's analysis picks the practice. Kind: evaluative. Rubric, written first: criteria are duty to correct, risk from the wording, and risk from silence. Scale: high, medium, or low, scored against the Step 3 claim table. Pass rule: counsel signs the posture. The final posture MUST wait for Step 3; scoping MAY run in parallel.

5. **Step 5, holding statement within four hours, full reply within 24 hours.** Publish a holding statement now. Then publish a correction if Step 3 finds falsity. Publish an evidence-based reply if Step 3 supports the statement. Capability added: a controlled public record. Domain: Clear for the holding statement, because its form is a known practice [2]. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
Given the holding statement draft,
When the incident lead and counsel approve it,
Then it is published on the original channel within four hours and contains no claim beyond the Step 3 evidence.
The full reply MUST NOT ship until Step 3 concludes.
