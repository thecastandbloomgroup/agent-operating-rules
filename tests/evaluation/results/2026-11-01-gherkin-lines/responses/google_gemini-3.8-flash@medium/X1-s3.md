## Background

You added a large language model summarizer to your customer support software.

## Relevance

## Information

Your team lacks visibility into summarizer performance because you have no baseline data.
Support summarization errors fall into three main types: factual distortion, omission of critical detail, and hallucination [1].
Research shows that prompt refinement and few-shot examples reduce hallucination rates by up to 30% [1].
Therefore, you must measure baseline error rates before changing the model or pipeline [judgment].
Table 1 shows the error taxonomy for evaluation.

Table 1: Summarization Error Taxonomy

| Error Type | Definition | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Factual error | Summary contradicts ticket text | High |
| Hallucination | Summary invents facts not in ticket | High |
| Omission | Summary misses customer issue or resolution | Medium |
