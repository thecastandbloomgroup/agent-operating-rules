## Headline

Measure what happens to retrospective actions, then run a small test that gives one action an owner and a date.

- Diagnosis: count past actions to find where change stops.
- Listening: ask the team why they think nothing changes.
- Practice: limit each retrospective to one owned action and review it first at the next meeting.
- Proof: track the completion rate against a baseline.

## Background

The team holds retrospectives (regular meetings that review how the team works). People attend, but no working change follows. The request asks what to do.

## Relevance

Each retrospective without change costs the team an hour per person. Attendance can also fall once people see no result. [judgment]

## Information

**Evidence**

- A retrospective has five phases: set the stage, gather data, generate insights, decide what to do, and close [1]. Change is lost in the "decide" phase or after the meeting. [judgment]
- The cause is likely (66 to 100%) missing follow-through, not missing ideas. Basis: the symptom is "attend but nothing shifts", and no other data exists. [judgment]
- Cause and effect in team behavior show up only afterward. This is the Complex domain [2]. So the fix is a small probe, not a full redesign.

**Assumption log**

A1. The team has 5 to 10 people and holds retrospectives every 1 to 2 weeks. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the team lead. If wrong: change the sample size in Step 3.

A2. The retrospectives produce action items, but the items are not finished. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: count the items in Step 1. If wrong: replace Step 3 with a change of meeting format to produce decisions.

A3. Managers do not block the changes the team proposes. Why not measured: the user has not said. Validate by: add one survey question in Step 2. If wrong: add a step to escalate the blocker outside the team.

**Alternative considered**

Replace the retrospective with a new format or pause it. This loses because a new format treats a symptom before the cause is known. [judgment]

## End

I recommend that we take these steps in order:

1. **Audit the last six retrospectives.** Increment: one table of actions proposed, owned, dated, and done. Blocker removed: the cause is unknown. Kind: deterministic. Domain: Clear, because counting records has one correct method.

   ```gherkin
   Given the notes of the last six retrospectives
   When we list every action item with its owner, date, and status
   Then the table shows the completion rate as a percentage
   ```

2. **Run a ten-minute anonymous survey.** Ask: "Why does nothing change?" Add: "Does anyone outside the team block our changes?" Increment: one page of themes with the number of respondents per theme. Blocker removed: the team's own reasons are unknown. Kind: evaluative. Domain: Complicated, because thematic analysis [3] finds the cause. Rubric: a theme counts when at least 3 respondents name it (weight 50%). Opposing answers are recorded (weight 25%). Each theme links to a quote (weight 25%). Pass threshold: 75%.

3. **Run a three-retrospective probe.** Each retrospective ends with one action. The action has one named owner, one date, and a place in the sprint backlog. Step 3 MUST NOT be judged until Step 1 gives a baseline. Increment: one retrospective per cycle. Blocker removed: no follow-through. Kind: stochastic. Domain: Complex, because team response shows only afterward. Leverage is a guess. Budget: three retrospectives. Stop condition: stop early if the team rejects the format in the survey. Acceptance test: metric is the share of actions done by the next retrospective. The baseline is the Step 1 rate. The pass threshold is the baseline plus 30 points. The sample is three retrospectives, so treat the result as weak evidence.

4. **Open every retrospective with a five-minute review of the last action.** This runs in parallel with Step 3. Increment: one agenda line. Blocker removed: no one checks the outcome. Kind: deterministic. Domain: Clear, because a standing agenda item is a known practice.

   ```gherkin
   Given a retrospective with an action from the last meeting
   When the meeting opens
   Then the owner reports the status before any new topic starts
   ```

If A2 is false, replace Step 3 with a format change that forces a decision in the "decide" phase.
