## Headline

**Northwind Analytics (Proposal 1) is the best fit, if its overage fees are capped in the contract.**

- Cost: Northwind fits the 400,000 budget, and Bluepeak does not.
- Risk: Northwind has an SLA (service level agreement) and a vendor-run migration, and Cedar has neither.
- Gap: no vendor states staff cost or the year-4 price, so Northwind needs written terms first.

## Background

Three vendors proposed a data warehouse. The budget is 400,000 USD over three years. The data is 14 TB now and grows about 3 TB per year, per the proposals file [1]. The request is to pick one vendor.

## Relevance

The decision locks in cost and operations risk for three years. Northwind's margin under budget is small, so unclear terms can erase it.

## Information

**Cost.** Northwind includes 20 TB. The data reaches 23 TB at the end of year 3 (14 + 3 × 3, see A1).

Per the proposals file, Section Proposal 1, each extra TB costs 9,000 per year [1]. The worst case adds 3 TB × 9,000 = 27,000.

| Vendor | Stated 3-year cost | Adjusted cost | Against 400,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northwind | 372,000 | 399,000 (worst case) | 1,000 under |
| Bluepeak | 455,000 | 455,000, no cap | 55,000 over (13.8%) |
| Cedar | 286,000 | 286,000 plus staff | 114,000 under |

**Risk.** Bluepeak pricing has no cap, so its estimate can rise with query volume. Cedar has no SLA, and one database engineer would run it [1]. That is a single point of failure [judgment].

**Performance.** Vendors ran the benchmark on 10 TB, not on 14 to 23 TB. Bluepeak scored 1.3 s, Northwind 2.1 s, and Cedar 3.4 s [1]. No vendor stated the speed the business needs (A4).

**Exit.** Northwind and Cedar charge no exit fee. Bluepeak charges 0.08 per GB, about 1,840 for 23 TB [1].

**Assumption log**

A1. Growth stays at 3 TB per year. Why not measured: the material gives only the team's estimate. Validate by: read 12 months of storage logs. If wrong: each TB above 3 TB per year adds 9,000 and removes the margin.

A2. The 10 TB benchmark holds at 14 to 23 TB. Why not measured: no tool can reach the vendor systems. Validate by: run the 12 queries on our data (Step 3). If wrong: re-rank with the new speeds.

A3. Northwind needs less staff time than Cedar. Why not measured: the material does not state staff cost. Validate by: ask our operations team for hours per month. If wrong: the cost gap to Cedar narrows.

A4. A median near 3 s meets the business need. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: ask the main report users. If wrong: Bluepeak ranks higher.

**Confidence.** Medium confidence, based only on vendor-stated figures and no independent test.

**Alternative considered.** Cedar is the strongest alternative. It costs 113,000 less than Northwind's worst case. It loses on operations risk: no SLA, next-day severity 1 response, and one engineer [1]. If A3 is wrong, this changes.
