## End

### 1. Trace the last three retrospectives

**Test first, deterministic:**
```text

When the team traces each agreed action
Then each action has a status and a known next decision
```

Spend 20 minutes reviewing those records together. Mark each action as absent, unfinished, completed without review, or completed with a result.

For unfinished actions, record the actual obstacle rather than “lack of accountability.” Examples include no time, no authority, or an unclear task.

**Output:** One short action record that locates the failure.  
**Blocker removed:** Uncertainty about where follow-through stops.  
**Domain:** Clear, because the task checks records against fixed fields.

If records do not exist, record that gap rather than reconstructing uncertain details.

### 2. Write one change agreement

**Test first, deterministic:**
```text
Given the failure identified in Step 1
When the team writes one change agreement
Then it names an owner, work time, authority, measure, target, and review date
```

Step 2 MUST wait until Step 1 passes.

Choose one change within the team’s control. Assign one owner, but have the team agree on capacity and support.

Use this template:

> For two cycles, we will **[change]** to reduce **[specific problem]**.  
> **[Owner]** will use **[reserved time]**, capped at one working session.  
> We will compare **[measure and units]** against **[baseline]**.  
> Success means **[target]** without worsening **[harm measure]**.  
> We will review it on **[date]**.

Use the previous two cycles as the baseline where records exist. Otherwise, measure the current process before the trial.

**Output:** One complete change agreement, not a list of intentions.  
**Blocker removed:** Ambiguous ownership and unsupported commitments.  
**Domain:** Clear, because the agreement has fixed acceptance fields.

### 3. Run and review the trial

**Acceptance test first, stochastic:** The outcome varies with team behavior.

- **Metric:** The outcome measure selected in Step 2.
- **Baseline:** The recorded pre-change measure.
- **Pass threshold:** The written target, without breaching the harm limit.
- **Sample:** Two work cycles.
- **Stop condition:** The trial exceeds its time budget or breaches the harm limit.

Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 2 passes.

Run the trial within the agreed budget. Begin each retrospective with its result before discussing new changes.

After two cycles, keep, revise, or stop the change. Do not count “task completed” as proof that the problem improved. [judgment]

**Output:** A recorded result and a decision.  
**Capability added:** A repeatable link between retrospective actions and observed effects.  
**Domain:** Complex, because the effect on team behavior becomes visible only after the trial.

The trial is small and safe to stop; its expected benefit remains a judgment. [judgment] Two cycles support a local decision, not a firm causal claim. [judgment]
