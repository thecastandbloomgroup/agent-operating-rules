## Headline

Send me the Q3 report and name the figures that look wrong; without the numbers, no check can start. A wrong figure almost always comes from one of three places: a bad input, a broken formula, or a timing mismatch between periods [judgment].

## Background

The user reports that the numbers in the Q3 report look wrong. The report itself is not in the conversation, so no figure, total, or variance is available to test. The request is to find and explain the error.

## Relevance

A wrong number in a quarterly report can reach leadership and, after that finance closes the quarter, it is costly to reverse. The fix is cheapest before anyone acts on the report.

## Information

**Evidence.** Classify errors by checking, in order: inputs, calculations, then period boundaries. Inputs fail most often and cost the least to correct; formulas come next; timing mismatches are rare but reach across reports [judgment]. Every claim here stays a judgment until the report arrives, because no source exists in this session.

**Alternative considered.** The strongest alternative is a calculation-first approach: audit formulas before inputs. It loses because inputs outnumber formulas in a typical report, so input checks find errors faster [judgment].

## End

3. **Compare against the baseline.** Compare each recomputed total with Q2 actuals and the Q3 plan. List every variance of 5 percent or more. 4. **Trace the variances.** Trace each variance from Step 3 to its source entry and classify it as input error, formula error, or timing error. This adds the capability of a root cause. The analysis in Step 4 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 3 passes. Test: a rubric scores each variance on the criteria "reproducible from source" (weight 50), "consistent across periods" (weight 30), "documented in the ledger" (weight 20), pass threshold 70 of 100. Domain: Complicated, because the cause is knowable with analysis, and more than one good check path exists.

**Conditions that change the recommendation.** If A2 is false and the issue is display only, replace Steps 3 and 4 with a formatting and rounding check.
