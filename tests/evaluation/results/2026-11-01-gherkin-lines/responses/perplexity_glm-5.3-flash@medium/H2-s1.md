## Headline

Stabilize first: freeze every external channel, verify the facts in writing within 24 h, then respond once, with one voice.

1. Nothing else goes public until legal and a named fact-checker agree on a written verdict per claim.
2. The board, staff, and media receive the same message at the same time, after that verdict.
3. One spokesperson owns every channel until the response is out.
4. Monitoring runs as a small probe in parallel; it does not drive the first response.

## Background

A journalist published that the CEO statement from yesterday contains false claims. Legal, PR, and the board each send separate messages to you, so no one owns the response.

## Relevance

Every hour with mixed messages raises the risk of a second false statement, which is the failure that created this crisis [judgment].

## Information

**Key unknowns and how they shape the path.** The material does not say whether the statement was in fact false, and it does not say who may speak for the company.

Assumption log:

- A1. No internal verification of the claims exists yet. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: check with legal for any existing fact comparison. If wrong: skip Step 2 and go straight to Step 3.
- A2. No single spokesperson is named and no approval path exists. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the comms lead which channels already require sign-off. If wrong: Step 1 shrinks to a memo, and most of the work is already done.
- A3. The journalist holds further evidence that is not yet public. Why not measured: no tool can reach private source material. Validate by: legal reads the article and lists each claim with its cited evidence. If wrong: the window for a calm, evidence-based response is wider, so the deadline in Step 2 loosens.

**Analysis.** The situation itself is not yet classifiable: cause and effect of the article on reputation are unknown until the facts are checked [judgment]. Per the Cynefin frame, that makes the first actions stabilize and gather, not decide [18]. Single-voice messaging and a response that follows verification are standard practice in this frame [18]; the specific 24 h deadline is a judgment call, sized so the article does not harden into the accepted version of events [judgment].

**Alternative considered.** Respond at once with a denial. It loses because a denial before verification risks a second false statement, so the response inherits the same failure mode [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we run these steps. Steps run in this order, except Step 5, which runs in parallel from the start.

1. **Freeze external messaging for 4 h and name one spokesperson.**
   Increment: a one-page freeze notice to legal, PR, and the board, with the spokesperson named. Blocker removed: the current mixed messages to you stop producing public output. Kind: deterministic. Test, write before publishing:
   ```gherkin
   Given the freeze notice is sent to all three audiences
   When any press, social, or legal statement is drafted at hour 2
   Then it routes to the single named approver and no unapproved text goes public
   ```
   Cynefin: Chaotic, because output must stop before cause and effect can be assessed [18].

2. **Verify every claim in writing within 24 h.** This step MUST NOT start until Step 1 is in force.
   Increment: a table of every clause in the CEO statement, each with a verdict of true, false, or unverifiable, and a cited record. Owner: legal plus the owner of the source records. Blocker removed: the Confused state ends, so the response path becomes decidable [18]. Kind: deterministic. Test:
   ```gherkin
   Given the statement text and the list of source records
   When the clause-by-clause comparison runs
   Then each clause has one verdict and one cited record, and the table is signed by legal
   ```
   Cynefin: this is Confused work that feeds classification; it gathers what the rest of the steps need [18].

3. **Draft and score three response options with a rubric.** This step MUST NOT start until the Step 2 table exists.
   Increment: scored options for no response, correction with evidence, and reaffirmation with the verified records. Blocker removed: the board, legal, and PR argue over one scored shortlist instead of three private ones. Kind: evaluative. Rubric first, Kepner-Tregoe form [15]: criteria are factual accuracy (weight 4), legal exposure (weight 3), audience trust (weight 2), speed (weight 1); pass threshold is a total of 7 of 10; each score cites the Step 2 table. Cynefin: Complicated, because expert analysis picks among known good practices [18].

4. **Publish one message to all three audiences within 2 h of the Step 3 decision.**
   Increment: one text, sent to the board, then staff, then media, in that order within the same window. Blocker removed: alignment, because every audience reads the same words. Kind: deterministic. Test:
   ```gherkin
   Given the approved response text
   When the send sequence runs
   Then board, staff, and media each hold the same text within 2 h, and no other text goes out
   ```
   Cynefin: Clear, because the best practice is one message from one voice [18].

5. **Monitor as a capped probe, in parallel with Steps 1 to 4.**
   Increment: a daily count of negative press and social mentions of the company name. Metric: share of negative mentions. Baseline: the 7 days before the article [judgment]. Sample: 200 mentions per day [judgment]. Trigger: if the share stays above 40% for 2 days after Step 4, reopen Step 3 [judgment]. Budget: 2 analyst hours per day, stop after 7 days. Blocker removed: the team learns whether the response worked before the next news cycle. Kind: stochastic, with the acceptance test above. Cynefin: Complex, because cause and effect of the response show up only afterwards, so the probe stays small with a stop condition [18]. Leverage here is a guess, not a measurement.

## Follow-up

Open questions:
1. Which records hold the source data for the CEO statement, and who owns them?
2. Is a board meeting scheduled inside the 24 h window?

Conditions that change the recommendation:
- If A1 is wrong and the claims are already verified as false, replace Step 2 with a legal read of the journalist's article, then run Steps 3 and 4 sooner.
- If A3 is wrong, raise the Step 5 trigger threshold from 40% to 60%, because the negative press share should fall faster [judgment].
