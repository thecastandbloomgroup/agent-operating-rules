# Headline

Freeze external statements now, assemble a fact pack, give one named owner authority over legal, PR, and the board, and publish a verified response after legal review. The supporting points:

1. One named owner approves every public statement, so the three groups stop sending different lines.
2. A fact pack compares each article claim against the exact words of the CEO statement.
3. Legal scores the exposure with a written rubric before anyone drafts an answer.
4. The owner issues one approved public line, or decides on no response, based on the scores.

# Background

A journalist published an article stating that the chief executive officer's (CEO) public statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are sending you requests at the same time, and each group wants a different action. The company therefore has no single line and no agreed facts today.

# Relevance

Mixed external signals extend the damage period, because each contradictory quote becomes a fresh story [judgment]. The sequence below restores one voice before anyone answers the article.

# Information

No external sources came into this session, so the evidence below carries [judgment] labels. Three claims drive the plan:

1. Unaligned replies to a press story produce contradictory quotes, and each contradiction becomes a fresh story [judgment].
2. A fast wrong correction costs more than 24 hours of silence, because a published correction cannot be taken back [judgment].
3. A single accountable owner is the fastest way to align three groups under time pressure [judgment].

The fact question (the article is right, wrong, or partly right) is about as likely as not on today's information. The sequence does not depend on that answer. Therefore it starts with facts, not with a defense.

**Assumption log**

A1. The article is public and names the company. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: read the article at hour 0. If wrong: legal exposure is lower, and Step 5 gains a correction request to the journalist.

A2. A transcript or recording of the CEO statement exists. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: ask the CEO's office for the source text at hour 1. If wrong: the fact pack uses witness notes, and Step 3 scores factual accuracy with lower confidence.

A3. The statement touched a legal or market sensitive topic. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: the Step 3 legal screen. If wrong: PR owns the file alone, and the exposure criteria drop out of the Step 3 rubric.

**Alternative considered**

Respond to the journalist immediately, before the fact pack. It loses because a second wrong statement compounds the first one [judgment]. That approach risks turning a one day story into a correction cycle.

# End

I recommend that we run the five steps below, with one named owner appointed today who approves every public statement. The Cynefin label describes how cause and effect behave at each step [18]. "Owner" means one accountable person for this incident.

**Step 1. Freeze external communication.** Send a notice to legal, PR, and the board within 2 hours: no external comment except through the owner. This removes the blocker of three channels sending three different lines. Test, written first [14]:

```gherkin
Given the freeze notice went out at hour 0
When a team member receives an external press question within 24 hours
Then the team member answers with the approved line or with no comment
```

Kind: deterministic. Domain: Chaotic, because the situation moves faster than cause and effect can be seen, so stabilization comes first [18].

**Step 2. Build the fact pack.** Produce one document that lists each article claim beside the matching statement text, with the source transcript, recording, or witness notes. This removes the blocker of debating opinions instead of the record. Test, written first [14]:

```gherkin
Given the CEO statement and the article both exist
When the owner collects the source text and the article
Then the fact pack lists each article claim beside the matching statement text
```

Kind: deterministic. Domain: Confused, because the first job is to classify what actually happened [18]. Step 2 may begin while the Step 1 notice circulates.

**Step 3. Run the legal screen with a written rubric.** Score four criteria against the fact pack: factual accuracy of the article (weight 0.4), regulatory or contractual exposure (0.3), defamation exposure (0.2), cost of a correction (0.1). A total score at or above 0.60 directs the company to publish a correction; each criterion needs cited evidence from the fact pack [15]. This removes the blocker of legal and PR pulling in opposite directions. Kind: evaluative, with the rubric written and scored before any draft text. Domain: Complicated, because expert analysis picks the response practice [18]. Step 3 waits on the Step 2 fact pack.

**Step 4. Align and approve one line.** Hold a 30 minute meeting that produces one document with an approved line per claim, or "no comment" per claim. This removes the blocker of no single company voice. Test, written first [14]:

```gherkin
Given the fact pack and the legal scores exist
When the owner, the legal lead, and the PR lead meet
Then the document carries one approved line or "no comment" for each claim
```

Kind: deterministic. Domain: Clear, because alignment on one approved document is a known practice [18]. Step 4 waits on Step 3.

**Step 5. Publish and monitor as a probe.** Release the approved line, or state no response, then monitor for 48 hours with one spokesperson as the budget. Acceptance test stated first: metric, the negative share of new coverage mentions; baseline, the share on the article's first day; pass threshold, the negative share falls below the baseline; sample size, the next 20 coverage mentions. Stop condition: if the negative share rises above the baseline, escalate to the board chair and pause all response drafting. Kind: stochastic. Domain: Complex, because the public reaction becomes visible only after publication, so the probe stays small and capped [18]. Step 5 waits on Step 4.

# Follow-up

Open questions: none, because the plan covers both outcomes of the fact check.

Conditions that change the recommendation: If the statement touched a regulated disclosure, route the Step 4 line through securities counsel before Step 5. If A3 is false, cut the exposure criteria from the Step 3 rubric and shorten the cycle. If A2 is false, name witness notes as the record in the Step 5 publication.
