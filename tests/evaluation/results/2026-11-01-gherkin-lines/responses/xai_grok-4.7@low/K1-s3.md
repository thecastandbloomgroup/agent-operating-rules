Headline
The 95th percentile (p95) rose because requests wait for a database connection. Restore spare pool connections first. Pool acquire p95 rose from 3 ms to 690 ms, and that wait is most of the 900 ms total. Each pod now stops at 8 connections, and the logs show waiters after the cut from 50. Order-query p95 stayed near the old value, and database CPU stayed near 40%. Cache time-to-live fell from 300 s to 60 s, and the miss rate rose from 12% to 31%.

Background
Release 2026.10.21 changed the pool, the cache time-to-live, and the order query. After that release, p95 latency on GET /customers/{id}/orders rose from 120 ms to 900 ms. The request is to name the cause and the next actions.

Relevance
The same release still serves about 405 requests per second, so the slowdown is in this build, not in traffic. Error rate rose from 0.02% to 0.40%, and pool timeouts are 96% of those errors.
