# Headline

Establish one response team in the next hour, verify the facts before any public reply. First, appoint one decision owner so Legal, PR, and the board stop acting in parallel. Second, freeze all external statements until a fact check confirms or refutes the journalist's claim. Third, prepare two statements, one for each outcome, so the reply is fast and accurate.

## Background

A journalist published that the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are messaging the reader separately, and nobody is aligned.

## Relevance

Parallel replies from three groups create contradictions, and contradictions cause more damage than the original claim. The next few hours set the public record.

## Information

This is a live reputation matter with legal exposure. The evidence available here is thin: the article itself is not provided, and the CEO statement content is unknown. Key uncertainties follow.

- A1. The journalist named a specific verifiable claim. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: read the article and extract the exact claim. If wrong: the response plan changes to correction, not defense.
- A2. The CEO statement exists in writing or on recording. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: pull the transcript or post. If wrong: evidence collection becomes harder and slower.
- A3. One person on the executive team can act as decision owner today. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: the CEO or general counsel appoints one in a 30 minute call. If wrong: escalation to the board chair is required.

Alternative considered: issue an immediate denial now. It loses because an unverified denial, later disproved, doubles the crisis [judgment].

The dominant risk theme is contradiction across spokespeople. Standard crisis practice supports one spokesperson and one holding line [1].

Confidence: high on rapid alignment and fact check first, medium confidence on legal posture until the claim text is known.

## End

I recommend that we:

1. Convene one 30 minute crisis call now and name one decision owner and one spokesperson. This removes the alignment blocker, which is the largest current risk. Cynefin domain: Chaotic, because many actors move at once and order must come first. Test (deterministic): Given all stakeholders are invited, When the call ends, Then one owner, one spokesperson, and a written "no external comment until cleared" rule exist.

2. Run a fact check within four hours. Extract the journalist's exact claim and compare it word by word to the CEO statement source. This adds the verified-facts capability that every later step needs. Cynefin domain: Complicated, because cause and effect are knowable through document analysis. This step depends on step 1 only for staffing and can start at once. Test (deterministic): Given the article and the source transcript, When the comparison is done, Then a one page table shows claim, source text, match or mismatch.

3. Freeze all external replies and send one holding line to media. This removes the contradiction risk from parallel statements. Cynefin domain: Clear, because a standard holding practice exists. Test (deterministic): Given an inbound media query, When the spokesperson or staff receive it, Then only the approved holding line goes out.

4. Draft both outcome statements in parallel after step 2 starts: a correction with the correct facts, and a substantiated defense with citations. This adds reply speed without an early risk. Cynefin domain: Complex, because reaction depends on facts not yet known, so treat it as a capped probe with a four hour budget and a stop when the fact check lands. Test (evaluative): rubric of accuracy 40%, tone 20%, legal review sign off 40%, pass at 80%, evidence from the step 2 table.

5. Hold a decision meeting at fact check plus one hour, and publish exactly one statement through the one spokesperson. Cynefin domain: Complicated, because the step 2 analysis picks the statement. Step 5 MUST NOT start until the test in step 2 passes. Test (deterministic): Given the step 2 result, When the meeting ends, Then one statement is approved and stamped for release.

If the fact check shows the claim is true, replace step 4 defense draft with a corrective action plan.
