Background
A retrospective is a regular meeting where the team inspects its way of work. People still attend, but the work does not change after the meeting. The request is for a next step that restores that link.

Relevance
A retrospective that does not change the work spends the meeting time and trains people to ignore it. Therefore the next meeting should test follow-through before it adds new topics.
