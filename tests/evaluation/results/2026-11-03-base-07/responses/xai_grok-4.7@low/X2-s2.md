Headline
Keep one action from each retrospective and review that action at the next meeting.
No person owns one change with a date, so attendance alone does not change the work.
Many actions at once leave no time for any action.
The next meeting does not check the last action before new topics start.
A new meeting format does not add an owner, a date, or a check.

Background
The team holds retrospectives, which are regular meetings to inspect how the team works. People attend, but the work after each meeting stays the same. The request is what to do so that a meeting produces a change.

Relevance
Each meeting that ends without a finished change spends team time and lowers trust in the next meeting. Therefore fix the review of actions before you change the meeting format.

Information
The Scrum Guide requires the team to address the most useful improvements as soon as possible.
[1]
A standard retrospective method ends with a decision about what to do, not with discussion alone.
[2]
Likely (66 to 100%) the first gap is a missing review of the last action. Basis: the symptom is no change, and the Scrum Guide requires the team to address improvements [1].
The follow-through theme has two supporting sources and no disagreeing source in the material.
[1]
[2]
About as likely as not (33 to 66%) a safety problem remains if owned actions still fail [judgment].

A1. The team keeps notes from recent retrospectives. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: open the last three notes. If wrong: write one shared note before you assign an owner.
A2. The next meeting does not start with the status of the last action. Why not measured: the material does not state the agenda. Validate by: read the last agenda. If wrong: keep the status check, then test safety and authority.
A3. The team can change its own way of work inside one cycle. Why not measured: the material does not state authority. Validate by: check whether the last idea needed a manager outside the team. If wrong: name that approver before a peer owns the action.

The strongest alternative is a new retrospective format or an outside facilitator. It loses because a format does not create an owner, a date, or a review of the last action [judgment].

End

1. Score the last three retrospective notes in one session. This removes the blocker of an unknown failure mode. The domain is Complicated, because the notes can show the cause.
Write the rubric first. Ownership named weighs 40. A due date weighs 30. A status check at the next meeting weighs 30. Pass is a score of 70 or more on at least two of three notes. Evidence is those three notes.

When the note is saved.

Sample size: at least 5 people. Budget: 20 minutes and one question. Stop if fewer than 5 people reply. Do not spend a second meeting on this probe.

If A3 is false, name the external approver in Step 3 instead of a peer owner.
If Step 1 scores 70 or more on two notes, stop Steps 2 and 3 and read the probe before the next retrospective.
