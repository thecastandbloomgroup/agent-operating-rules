Headline
Stop new public comments until one owner checks the chief executive's words against records.

Uncoordinated replies from legal, communications, and the board can contradict each other.
The right reply depends on records, not on the article alone.
A short holding line buys time without admitting or denying the claim.
The board needs known facts now and a set time for the next update.

Background
A journalist published that the chief executive's statement yesterday was false.
Legal, communications, and the board are all messaging, and they do not share one plan.
The request is for the next steps while those groups remain apart.

Relevance
The article is already public, so each new message can become part of the record.
Therefore a split response raises legal risk and weakens trust with the board.

Information
This section covers the evidence, the assumptions, and the alternative.

The request states three facts.
First, a journalist published the claim.
Second, the chief executive made the statement yesterday.
Third, the three groups are not aligned.

Crisis practice limits speakers and checks facts before a full reply [2].

A1. No response owner is named. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask counsel who owns the reply. If wrong: use the named owner in Step 1.
A2. No one has compared the statement with source records. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask counsel for that status. If wrong: skip Step 3.
A3. Listing status and jurisdiction are unknown. Why not measured: the material does not state them. Validate by: counsel checks both. If wrong: drop any disclosure branch.

The strongest alternative is a fast public denial to meet board pressure.
It loses because records may later contradict that denial [2].

End
I recommend that we take these four steps in order.

Step 1. Domain: Chaotic, because many people speak before a shared cause is known [3].
Test first.
Given no approved fact sheet exists,
When a reporter or director asks for a comment,
Then the person uses only the holding line and names the owner.
In one session, counsel sends a stop note to legal, communications, and the board.
The note removes split comments that can enter the public record.
The holding line is: "We saw the report. We are checking our records. We will update when that check ends."
The note also says not to delete messages until counsel sets a hold scope [judgment].

Step 2. Domain: Confused, because the duty type is not known yet.
This step gathers what is needed to class the later work.
Rubric first.
Criteria are listing status, weight 40, claim type, weight 40, and any deadline inside 24 hours, weight 20.
Pass needs each criterion marked with a source note.
In one session, counsel classifies disclosure duty, litigation risk, and any deadline.
This removes the blocker of an unknown legal track.
Step 3 MUST NOT start until this note exists, because the note sets which records are in scope.

Step 3. Domain: Complicated, because experts can match words to records.
Test first.
Given the exact statement and the source records,
When staff compare each claim in the statement,
Then a variance log marks each claim as match, gap, or absent.
In one session, staff complete that log.
The log removes the blocker of an unknown fact pattern.
Step 4 MUST NOT start until this log and the Step 2 note both exist.

Step 4. Domain: Complicated, because the reply follows the analysis in Steps 2 and 3.
Rubric first.
Record match weighs 50, legal duty weighs 30, and one voice weighs 20.
A must-pass rule bars any sentence with no record or counsel note.
Pass needs a score of at least 80 of 100 and both must-pass checks.
In one session, the owner picks correct, clarify, or contest with evidence.
This removes the blocker of no agreed reply.
Then the owner sends the board the decision and the next update time.

Follow-up
If A1 is false, Step 1 uses the named owner.
If A2 is false, skip Step 3 and use the existing log in Step 4.
If the log shows a false statement, Step 4 must pick correct, not contest.
If counsel finds a deadline inside 24 hours, that deadline task goes before Step 4.
