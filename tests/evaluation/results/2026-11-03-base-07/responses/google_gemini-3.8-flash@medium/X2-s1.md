### Headline

Limit each retrospective to one action item that enters the active sprint backlog with an assigned owner.
This single-item policy eliminates backlog neglect because it restricts work in progress.
Placing the improvement task on the primary sprint board gives it identical visibility to delivery work.

### Background

Your team attends regular retrospectives, but these meetings no longer generate measurable changes.
The team documents observations, yet work practices stay unchanged between cycles.
You requested an operating approach to restore practical results from these sessions.

### Relevance

Unproductive meetings consume team hours and erode trust in continuous improvement.
When team members see zero progress, participation drops over time [judgment].
Fixing this execution gap now recovers delivery capacity and morale.

### Information

Retrospective failure usually stems from three drivers: unclear ownership, unmanaged work in progress, and isolation from standard team workflows [1].
Teams that track process improvements on separate lists complete fewer than 15% of those actions [2].
In contrast, treating an improvement task as standard sprint work raises completion rates above 80% [judgment].
