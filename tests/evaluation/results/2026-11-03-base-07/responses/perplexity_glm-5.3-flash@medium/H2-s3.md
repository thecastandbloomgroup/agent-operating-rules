## Headline

Stabilize within hours: freeze all external statements, route them through one person, and verify the journalist's claim against source documents before any final response. The next actions are: one holding statement from one channel, a fact check against the original sources, and then one final decision by legal, PR, and the board together, not in parallel.

## Background

A journalist published a claim that the CEO's statement of November 3 is false. Legal, PR, and the board each send separate messages. No single owner or message exists today.

## Relevance

Every hour without a single message lets the story set the frame instead of the company. Uncoordinated statements can also create legal exposure, because one team's wording can undercut another's.

Because this is a crisis with no visible cause and effect, the first step must stabilize the situation [18].

Assumptions:

- A3. No regulator, partner, or customer has publicly responded to the article. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: monitoring scan in step 4. If wrong: move to legal review of contract or disclosure duties before step 3.

Alternative considered: answer the journalist directly and fast with a denial. It loses because an unverified denial can become a second false statement, and because it repeats the pattern that produced the problem [judgment].

## End

Increment: one short written notice today to legal, PR, and the board, naming the single message owner and the rule "no external words until final decision".
