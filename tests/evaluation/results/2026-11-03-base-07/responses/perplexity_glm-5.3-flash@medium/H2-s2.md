# Headline

Stabilize the message flow first, verify the facts second, and reply once with one voice.

1. Step 1 names one coordinator today and holds every outbound statement.
2. Step 2 puts the CEO statement and the journalist's evidence side by side on one page.
3. Step 3 scores the response options with legal, on that page.
4. Step 4 releases one message and measures the result against a set threshold.

# Background

Yesterday the CEO gave a public statement. Today a journalist published that the statement is false. Legal, PR, and the board each sent direction, and no direction matches.

# Relevance

Every group now acts on partial facts, so a split message can land on the record today. One shared fact sheet prevents that.

# Information

**Evidence**

- The article, the full statement text, and the internal messages are not in this session. So the plan rests on [judgment] entries and on the cited practice sources.
- The facts decide how this crisis is classified. Therefore the first non-stabilizing step must gather them [18].
- Legal, PR, and the board argue from different records today. One fact sheet becomes their shared input [judgment].
- Outright denials written before a fact check invite a second story with a new correction cycle [judgment]. So the reply waits for the check.

**Assumption log**

- A1. The statement concerns a company or public matter, not a private one. Why not measured: the article is not available in this session. Validate by: read the article today. If wrong: legal scope narrows, and the plan shifts to personal guidance for the CEO.
- A2. The journalist holds evidence that supports the claim of a false statement. Why not measured: no article text is available in this session. Validate by: list each source the article cites and check each one. If wrong: the first outbound message becomes a correction request to the outlet.
- A3. The statement triggers no regulatory disclosure duty. Why not measured: the subject of the statement is unknown. Validate by: legal checks the subject against listing and disclosure rules, within the same session. If wrong: the filing deadline sets the clock, and all steps compress to meet it.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is an immediate public denial. It loses under A2, the most likely case, because an unverified denial invites a second story [judgment].

# End

I recommend that we take four steps in this order.

**Step 1. Stabilize: appoint one coordinator and hold all statements.** (Chaotic, deterministic.)

- Increment: a one page protocol with one approved holding line, completed in one session.
- Blocker removed: three groups speaking in three directions.
- Cynefin: Chaotic, because cause and effect are not visible in the message flow, so stabilize first [18].
- Test, written before the work [14]:

```gherkin
Given a media request that reaches any of the three groups
When a person answers
Then the person sends only the holding line and routes the contact to the coordinator
```

Sample holding line: "We have seen the article and are reviewing the facts."

**Step 2. Gather: build the fact sheet.** (Confused, evaluative.)

- Increment: one page that lists each statement sentence beside the article's evidence, completed in one session.
- Blocker removed: legal, PR, and the board now argue from one record.
- Cynefin: Confused, because the checked facts decide the next step, so gather before you classify.
- Rubric, written before the work. Pass threshold: a failure in criterion 1 or 2 marks the statement as not defensible as written.

| # | Criterion | Weight | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Each source named in the article checks as real and as cited | 50% | The source list from the article |
| 2 | At least one statement sentence fails against the evidence | 30% | The fact sheet rows |
| 3 | Two of three reviewers read the article's context the same way | 20% | Written reviewer notes |

**Step 3. Assess: legal scores the response options.** (Complicated, evaluative.)

- Increment: a one page option review of correct, clarify, correct plus a leadership change, and no reply.
- Blocker removed: no plan exists that fits both the verified facts and the legal duties.
- Cynefin: Complicated, because expertise picks the practice, so the action names its analysis. The analysis is a scored option table [15]:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Duty to disclose | 40% |
| Litigation exposure | 30% |
| Credibility with the outlet | 20% |
| Board and staff confidence | 10% |

- Test, written before the work: pass threshold is the highest weighted score wins, and no criterion scores zero. Evidence: the fact sheet from step 2.
- Dependency: step 3 does not start until step 2 produces the fact sheet.

**Step 4. Respond: publish one message, then measure.** (Complex, stochastic.)

- Increment: one message that clarifies or corrects, placed by PR with counsel approval, in one session.
- Blocker removed: silence and a split record both cost credibility, so one aligned message answers both.
- Cynefin: Complex, because the audience reaction appears only after the message, so run it as a capped probe.
- Acceptance test, written first. Metric: an on the record company response in the outlet that published the claim. Baseline: 0 of 1 outlets today. Pass threshold: 1 of 1 outlets notes the reply on the record within 48 hours. Sample size: that outlet plus up to five outlets that run the story.
- Stop condition and budget: one message, no press conference, and a stop after the 48 hour readout.
- Dependency: step 4 does not start until step 3 produces the option review.

# Follow-up

Conditions that change this recommendation:

- If A2 is false, step 4 changes to a documented correction request to the outlet, sent before any public message.
- If A3 is false, legal files first, and the filing text becomes the public message.
