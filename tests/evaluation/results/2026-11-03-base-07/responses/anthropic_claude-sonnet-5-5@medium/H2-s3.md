## Headline

Freeze outside statements first, verify the facts second, and then decide one response through one owner.

- One person must own the decision, because three advisers now give conflicting guidance.
- The facts decide the response, so check the CEO's statement against records before anyone publishes.
- Legal, PR, and the board need one shared brief, so each works from the same facts.

## Background

A journalist published a report that the CEO's statement from yesterday (Nov 3, 2026) was false. Legal, PR, and the board each message you with different views. You ask what to do next.

## Relevance

Each hour without a single position raises the risk of a second, conflicting public statement. A second statement would add a new problem to the first.

## Information

**Evidence**

- The report may be right, wrong, or partly right. No facts have been reviewed yet. [judgment]
- Several advisers acting at once is a common cause of mixed public messages. [judgment]
- Domain labels in the End section follow the Cynefin framework.
  [1]

**Assumption log**

A1. You coordinate the response but do not own the final decision. Why not measured: the material does not state your role. Validate by: ask the CEO's office who decides. If wrong: skip the owner request in Step 1 and act as owner.

A2. The company has no legal duty to disclose or correct on a set deadline. Why not measured: the material does not state the company's status. Validate by: ask Legal for the deadline in one line. If wrong: Legal sets the timing in Step 3.

A3. Records exist that can confirm or refute the disputed claims. Why not measured: no tool can reach them. Validate by: ask the CEO's office for the source documents. If wrong: Step 2 becomes an interview-based review, and confidence stays low.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is to issue a firm denial now to stop the story. It loses because the facts are unchecked, and a false denial costs more than a delay of a few hours. [judgment]

## End

I recommend that we take these five actions in this order:

1. **Freeze and name an owner (Chaotic: advice conflicts, the clock runs, and no cause and effect is clear).** Increment: within 1 hour, send one message to Legal, PR, and the board chair. It says all outside statements pause and asks the CEO's office to name one decision owner. This removes the blocker of three competing voices. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   - Given Legal, PR, and the board chair have received the freeze message
   - When any of them asks to publish a statement
   - Then the request goes to the named decision owner before release

2. **Verify the claims (Complicated: records can be checked by analysis).** Increment: within 3 hours, build a sheet that lists each disputed claim, the CEO's words, the journalist's words, and the source record. This removes the blocker of unknown facts. Step 3 MUST NOT start until this test passes. Kind: evaluative. Rubric, written first:
   - Criterion A, weight 40: each disputed claim has a primary record attached.
   - Criterion B, weight 30: a second reviewer confirmed each verdict.
   - Criterion C, weight 30: the CEO's own account is recorded in writing.
   - Pass threshold: 90 of 100, with Criterion A fully met.

3. **Choose the response (Complicated: a known set of options exists, and analysis picks one).** Increment: score four options in a Pugh matrix, with "hold and say nothing" as the baseline. The options are hold, clarify, correct, and dispute. This removes the blocker of an unchosen position. Kind: evaluative. Rubric, written first:
   - Fit with verified facts, weight 35.
   - Legal exposure, weight 25.
   - Trust of customers and staff, weight 25.
   - Speed, weight 15.
   - Pass threshold: the chosen option scores above the baseline and Legal raises no objection in writing.
   [2]

4. **Align Legal, PR, and the board (Clear: a one-page brief is a known practice).** Increment: send a one-page brief with the verified facts, the chosen option, the owner, and the time of the next update. Step 4 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 3 passes. This removes the blocker of misalignment. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   - Given the brief has reached Legal, PR, and the board chair
   - When 1 hour has passed
   - Then each has confirmed the facts section or disputed a named fact in writing
   [3]

5. **Run a reaction probe (Complex: public reaction shows its effect only afterward).** Increment: one person counts media mentions of the story each hour, for at most 4 hours. This adds the capability to see whether the story grows. Leverage is a guess. Run this in parallel with Steps 2 to 4. Kind: stochastic. Acceptance test, written first:
   - Metric: mentions per hour.
   - Baseline: the count in the first hour.
   - Threshold: escalate to the owner if the count rises 50% or more in each of 3 consecutive hourly readings.
   - Sample size: 3 readings.
   - Stop condition: 4 hours, or the owner calls it off.
