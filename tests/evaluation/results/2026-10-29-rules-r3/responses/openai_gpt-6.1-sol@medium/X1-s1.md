### Define “wrong”

Use the following proposed rubric to review summaries against source tickets. The weights and thresholds are starting choices, not measured facts. [judgment]

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Factual accuracy | 40% | Each claim matches the source, including names, numbers, and negation |
| Required information | 30% | The summary preserves the issue, current status, and next action when stated |
| Attribution and sequence | 20% | The summary identifies who said or did what, in the correct order |
| Readability | 10% | An agent can understand the summary without resolving unclear wording |

Score each criterion as 0, 0.5, or 1: fail, partial, or pass. Multiply each score by its weight.

**Proposed pass threshold:** 90/100, with no serious error. [judgment] Define a serious error as one that can change an agent’s action, customer commitment, or privacy exposure. [judgment]

**Current score:** unscored for every criterion because no tickets or summaries accompany the request. Agent reports do not establish an error rate. [1]

Record these measures:

- **Serious-error rate:** summaries with at least one serious error divided by summaries reviewed.
- **Overall failure rate:** summaries below the rubric threshold divided by summaries reviewed.
- **Correction time:** seconds agents spend checking and correcting each summary.
- **Generation cost and delay:** cost per summary and seconds until the summary appears.

Keep these measures separate. A readable summary does not compensate for a false claim. [judgment]
