## Background

You asked for the best prompt to stop a model from stating false facts. Research treats hallucination as a property of how language models generate text, not as a rare bug.

This answer gives the prompt first, then the evidence and a test plan.

## Relevance

A false claim that looks confident costs you time or trust when you act on it. A tested prompt gives you a known error rate instead of a guess.
