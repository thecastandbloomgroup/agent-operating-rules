## End

I recommend that we take these five actions in this order:

1. **Stabilise ownership (Chaotic, deterministic).** Within one hour, hold a 30-minute call with the CEO, Legal, PR, and the board chair. Name one decision owner and one channel. Freeze all external comment until Step 3 is done.
   - Blocker removed: conflicting advice.
   - Test first: Given the call ended, When anyone asks who decides, Then one named owner appears in a written note sent to all four parties.

2. **Build the fact file (Complicated, deterministic).** Legal leads. Within four hours, list each claim in the article and the CEO's exact words. Match each claim to a document or a witness. Mark it true, false, or unclear. Add a check of disclosure duties (see A3).

   - Test first: Given the article, When Legal reviews it, Then every claim has a verdict and a named source document.
   - Step 4 MUST NOT state facts that Step 2 has not checked.

3. **Issue a holding statement (Complicated, evaluative).** Write two to three sentences: the company knows of the article, is reviewing it, and will update by a stated time. Do not confirm or deny.
   - Blocker removed: silence that others fill with their own story.
   - Rubric first: accuracy 40%, no legal admission 30%, plain language 30%. Pass is 80 points or more, with sign-off from Legal and PR.
   - Step 3 MUST NOT go out before Step 1 is complete.

4. **Send the board one brief (Clear, deterministic).** Use one page: what is known, what is unknown, who owns the decision, and when the next update arrives.

5. **Choose the response (Complicated, evaluative).** After Step 2 passes, score three options: correct the record, clarify, or defend. Use a Pugh matrix with the baseline "hold and wait".

   - Rubric first: legal exposure 35%, factual accuracy 35%, trust with customers and staff 30%. Pass is the highest score, at 70 points or more.
   - Step 5 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 2 passes.

If A2 is false and a review already exists, replace Step 2 with a check of that review.
