## Headline

Take control of the facts and the voice before you take any side. Do this in four steps.

- One person must own decisions, so Legal, PR, and the board stop issuing separate messages.
- Verify the CEO's statement against primary records before anyone defends or retracts it.
- Publish a short holding statement that makes no new factual claim.
- Choose between correction, clarification, and defense by a scored rubric, not by whoever speaks loudest.

## Background

A journalist published that the CEO's statement from yesterday (Thursday, October 29, 2026) is false. Legal, PR, and the board each give you different advice. You need an order of actions for the next hours.

## Relevance

Each hour without one position lets the story set the facts. Conflicting messages from inside the company also create a second story about internal disorder. [judgment]

## Information

**Evidence**

- The disagreement is about the facts, not about the response. No one has yet checked whether the statement is false. [judgment]
- Legal, PR, and the board optimize for different goals: liability, reputation, and governance. Each advice is valid for its own goal. [judgment]
- When no one can yet see cause and effect, the first move is to stabilise, then gather facts. This is the Chaotic and Confused guidance in the Cynefin framework.

  [1]

**Assumption log**

A1. The company has not issued a public reply to the article. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: check the press page and social accounts. If wrong: skip Step 3 and align the next message with the one already out.

A2. You have authority to convene the three groups, or can ask the board chair to grant it. Why not measured: the material does not state your role. Validate by: ask the board chair for a 15-minute call. If wrong: ask the chair to name the decision owner in Step 1.

A3. The company may be listed on a stock exchange. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask Legal in Step 1. If wrong: remove the disclosure item from the Step 3 checklist.

**Alternative considered**

Defend the CEO now and verify later. It loses because a defense of a false statement adds a second false statement. The cost of a wrong defense is higher than the cost of a delay of a few hours. [judgment]

## End

I recommend that we take these steps in this order:

1. **Name one decision owner and freeze all outside messages.** (Chaotic, deterministic.)
   - Increment: a 30-minute call with the CEO, one Legal lead, one PR lead, and the board chair. The call ends with one named owner and one rule: no outside message without the owner's approval.
   - Blocker removed: three groups issue conflicting advice.
   - Test first:
     - Given the call has ended
     - When any person asks who approves an outside message
     - Then all four attendees give the same name in writing.

2. **Verify the statement against primary records.** (Confused, evaluative.) Step 3 MUST NOT start until this step reports.
   - Increment: one analyst compares each factual claim in the CEO statement with the source record and each claim in the article. The analyst works for up to 2 hours. [judgment]
   - Blocker removed: no one knows who is right.
   - Test first (rubric):
     - Criteria: record found for each claim (weight 50), the record is primary (weight 30), a second person confirms (weight 20).
     - Pass threshold: 80 of 100 per claim.
     - Evidence needed: a document or data export for each claim.
   - Output: each claim is marked true, false, or unverified. The mark classifies the next domain.

3. **Publish a holding statement.** (Complicated, deterministic.) This step MUST NOT start until Step 1 is complete.
   - Increment: a statement of 3 sentences or fewer. It says the company is reviewing the article and will share findings by a stated time.
   - Blocker removed: silence lets the article stand as the only account.
   - Test first:
     - Given the draft statement
     - When Legal and PR review it
     - Then it contains no new factual claim, it names a time for the next update, and the owner from Step 1 approves it.

4. **Choose the response by a scored rubric.** (Complicated, evaluative.) This step MUST NOT start until Step 2 reports.
   - Increment: score three options with the decision owner, Legal, PR, and the board chair: correct the record, clarify, or defend.
   - Blocker removed: the groups argue from positions, not from shared criteria.
   - Test first (Kepner-Tregoe style):

     [2]

     - Criteria and weights: factual accuracy 40, legal exposure 25, stakeholder trust 25, speed 10.
     - Pass threshold: an option scores at least 70 of 100, and no criterion scores zero.
     - Evidence: Step 2 results, Legal's written view, and one board statement.
   - If no option passes, return to Step 2 for more facts.

If Step 2 shows the statement is true, replace Step 4 with a defense plan that cites the primary records. If it shows the statement is false, the correction option gains weight on factual accuracy.
