## Headline

Freeze external messaging and name one spokesperson today; then verify the facts before any substantive response. The work breaks into four phases: contain, verify, align, then decide. Containment comes first because every extra voice creates a new public contradiction. The full decision on whether the CEO must correct, apologise, or defend waits until the facts are labelled.

## Background

A journalist published yesterday that your CEO's public statement was false. Legal, PR, and the board all message you, and no one agrees on the response. No verified fact base exists yet.

## Relevance

The first hours set the public record. Contradictions between your teams create new stories and force later corrections [judgment].

## Information

Cause and effect are not visible yet; no one can say which claims are false or what the records show. This is a chaotic situation, so the rule is: act to stabilise, then analyse [18]. Because of that, the step order below holds whichever side is factually right [inference]. Only the wording of the holding message depends on the verified facts.

1. Unaligned replies to press queries produce a contradictory public record within hours [judgment].
2. Confidence in the containment-first order: high confidence, the source analysis fits any unverified public conflict [18].

**Assumption log**

- A1. The article concerns one public statement made yesterday. Why not measured: the article is not in this chat. Validate by: read the article and the full statement transcript. If wrong: the response scope widens to earlier statements.
- A2. No group has verified the facts yet. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: one 15-minute call with legal. If wrong: shorten the plan by one step.
- A3. Records exist to check the statement. Why not measured: the material does not name them. Validate by: ask legal for the record list in Step 2. If wrong: the holding line becomes "we are checking".

**Alternative considered**: an immediate public denial today. It loses because the facts are unverified; a later reversal doubles the damage [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we run five steps in this order:

1. **Freeze and route (today, first two hours). Cynefin: Chaotic. Deterministic.** Send one written directive to legal, PR, and the board. It names one spokesperson for all press contact. Blocker removed: unaligned replies. Test, written first:

   ```gherkin
   Given any press query received today
   When a team member replies
   Then the reply routes to the spokesperson, and no one sends a direct statement
   ```

2. **Verify (today, one session of four hours). Cynefin: Complicated. Deterministic.** Compare yesterday's statement, claim by claim, with the records. Blocker removed: no group can align without facts. Test, written first:

   ```gherkin
   Given the full transcript and the source records
   When each claim is compared
   Then each claim gets one label, true, false, or unverifiable, with a record attached
   ```

3. **Hold the line (today, within eight hours). Cynefin: Complicated. Evaluative.** Draft one paragraph, cleared by legal. Blocker removed: the press needs one answer before the full review ends. Rubric first: accuracy against the Step 2 labels (weight 3); no statement on liability before the facts exist (weight 3); one message for all channels (weight 2); plain language (weight 2). Pass threshold: 8 of 10. Evidence per criterion: the Step 2 labels and legal's written note. Dependency: Step 3 goes out only after Step 2 labels the claims named in the article.

4. **Brief the board (today, end of day). Cynefin: Clear. Deterministic.** Send one fact sheet with the claim labels and the holding line. Blocker removed: boards leak when they lack facts. Test, written first:

   ```gherkin
   Given the fact sheet
   When a board member is asked by anyone
   Then the member answers with the holding line and adds no new claim
   ```

5. **Monitor (48 hours, parallel run). Cynefin: Complex, a capped probe. Stochastic.** One person counts new articles and repeated inaccuracies. Budget: two hours total. Metric: the count of new articles and of inaccuracies; baseline: yesterday's coverage. Stop condition: no new inaccurate article for 24 hours, or the budget ends. Leverage here is a guess. Run it in parallel with Steps 2 to 4.

Sequence: Step 2 and Step 5 start only after Step 1 is sent. Step 3 waits on the part of Step 2 that covers the named claims. Step 4 waits on Step 3.

## Follow-up

**Conditions that change the recommendation**: if the article relies on leaked internal documents, add a document-protection review before Step 3 goes out. If the statement covered numbers reported to a regulator, legal sets the wording and Step 3 becomes regulator-led.
