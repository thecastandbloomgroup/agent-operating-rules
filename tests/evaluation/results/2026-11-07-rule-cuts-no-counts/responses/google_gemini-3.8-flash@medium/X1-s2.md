### Background

You deployed a Large Language Model (LLM) summarizer to assist customer support agents. Support agents report that the summaries are sometimes wrong, but the team lacks operational metrics [judgment]. Therefore, you need a systematic method to measure summary quality and eliminate model errors.

### Information

Customer support summarization commonly fails in two areas: factual inconsistency and missing core details [1]. Factual inconsistency occurs when the model introduces claims absent from the original transcript [1]. Missing details occur when the model omits customer constraints or troubleshooting steps [judgment].

To fix these issues, you need a gold standard evaluation dataset and systematic error scoring [2]. Quantitative evaluation requires a comparison against a human-verified baseline [2].
