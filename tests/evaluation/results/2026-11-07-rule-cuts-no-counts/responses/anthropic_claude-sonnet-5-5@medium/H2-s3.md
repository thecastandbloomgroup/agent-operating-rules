## End

I recommend that we take these steps in order:

1. **Stabilize control (Chaotic: act first, then observe).** In the next hour, name one decision owner. Freeze all outside comment. Ask Legal to issue a document hold. This removes the blocker of conflicting direction.
   Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   Given three groups send conflicting advice
   When the board chair names one decision owner in writing
   Then Legal, PR, and the board confirm they route decisions through that owner, and a document hold is on file

2. **Verify the facts (Complicated: analysis by counsel picks the finding).** Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 passes. Counsel compares the CEO statement with records, under privilege. Finish in one working session. This removes the blocker of unknown truth.
   Kind: evaluative. Rubric, written first. Pass threshold: 80 of 100.
   - Source match, weight 40: each claim in the statement matched to a dated record.
   - Journalist claims, weight 30: each claim marked supported, unsupported, or unknown, with evidence.
   - Gap list, weight 30: open items with owner and due time.

3. **Issue a holding statement (Complicated: PR and Legal draft, then apply the matching practice).** Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 2 passes. Draft three to five sentences. Say the company is reviewing the facts and will update at a set time. Correct the record at once if Step 2 finds an error. This limits the gap in which the journalist's framing stands alone.
   Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   Given Legal and PR approve one text
   When the decision owner publishes it
   Then the text contains no unverified claim, names an update time, and matches internal talking points

4. **Brief the board once (Clear: a known practice).** Send a one-page note after Step 3. Include facts found, the statement, the next update time, and open decisions. Use one channel only. This removes the blocker of side messages.
   Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   Given the board has received side messages
   When the decision owner sends the note
   Then every director gets the same page, and later questions go to the owner

If Step 2 shows the statement was true, replace Step 3 with a factual rebuttal that Legal approves.
