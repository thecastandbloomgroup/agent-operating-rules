## Headline

Yes, pin every version, including indirect dependencies, and update them on a schedule.

- Unpinned versions let upstream releases change your build without any change in your code.
- Pinning only direct dependencies leaves indirect ones free, so pin the full set.
- Pinning moves update work from surprise breaks to a planned weekly task.
