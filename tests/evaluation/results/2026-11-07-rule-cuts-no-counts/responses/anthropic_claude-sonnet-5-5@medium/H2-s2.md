## Headline

Name one decision owner now, freeze outside statements, and verify the facts before anyone takes a position.

- Conflicting advice from three groups is the largest immediate risk, so control comes first.
- The truth of the CEO's statement decides every later choice, so check it next.
- A short holding statement protects the company while the facts are checked.
- The final response (correct, clarify, or defend) follows the verified facts.

## Background

A journalist published a claim that the chief executive officer (CEO) made a false statement yesterday. Legal, public relations (PR), and the board each give you different advice. The material does not say which parts of the article are accurate.

## Relevance

Each hour without one owner raises the chance of contradictory messages. Contradictory messages can become a second story [judgment].

## Information

Evidence:
- Three groups message you with no shared position (per the request).
- Before the facts are checked, the article is about as likely as not to be correct [judgment].
- Typical positions: legal limits admissions, PR wants speed, the board wants low risk [judgment].
- A crisis with no verified facts calls for stabilising first, then analysis [1].

Assumption log:
- `Assumption: The statement is unverified and could be true, partly true, or false. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: compare each claim to primary records (Step 2). If wrong: if fully true, Step 4 becomes a request for correction.`
- `Assumption: You can ask the board chair to name the owner. Why not measured: the material does not state your role. Validate by: message the chair. If wrong: ask legal to escalate to the chair.`
- `Assumption: No law sets a disclosure deadline. Why not measured: the material does not state the jurisdiction or listing status. Validate by: ask legal in Step 2. If wrong: do the disclosure before Step 3.`

Alternative considered: deny or defend publicly at once. It loses because an unverified denial that proves false adds a second false statement [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these four actions in order:

1. **Name one decision owner and freeze outside statements.**
   - Increment: one 30-minute call with legal, PR, and the board chair. It ends with a written owner, a freeze, and set briefing times.
   - Blocker removed: no single authority.
   - Kind: deterministic. Cynefin: Chaotic. Act first to stabilise, then analyse.
   - Test [2]:
     Given legal, PR, and the board chair are on the call
     When the call ends
     Then one named owner and a written freeze reach all three groups

2. **Build a fact table.** Step 2 MUST NOT start until the Step 1 test passes.
   - Increment: a table of each claim in the CEO statement and the article. Each row has a primary record (email, data, minutes) and a status: confirmed, refuted, or unknown. Include the CEO's source for the statement and any disclosure duty.
   - Blocker removed: no shared facts.
   - Kind: deterministic. Cynefin: Complicated. Expert analysis of records picks the facts.
   - Test:
     Given the CEO statement and the article
     When legal reviews the primary records
     Then every claim has a status and a record reference

3. **Draft a holding statement.** Step 3 MAY be drafted while Step 2 runs. It MUST NOT be released until the owner approves it and Step 2 passes.
   - Increment: three sentences that acknowledge the article, say a review is under way, and give an update time.
   - Blocker removed: silence while facts are checked.
   - Kind: deterministic. Cynefin: Clear. A known best practice applies.
   - Test:
     Given the draft holding statement
     When legal and PR compare it to the fact table
     Then it has no refuted or unknown claim and it states an update time

4. **Choose the response.** Step 4 MUST NOT start until the Step 2 test passes.
   - Increment: one scored rubric for four options: correct, clarify, defend and request correction, stay silent.
   - Blocker removed: the disagreement between legal, PR, and the board.
   - Kind: evaluative [3]. Cynefin: Complicated. Analysis of the options against the facts picks the practice.
   - Rubric, with evidence from the fact table:
     - Accuracy to the fact table: weight 40.
     - Legal exposure: weight 25.
     - Trust of the audience: weight 20.
     - Speed: weight 15.
     - Pass threshold: a total of 70 or more, and at least 30 on accuracy.
   - The owner decides. The board receives the scored rubric.

- If a disclosure deadline exists, move that disclosure ahead of Step 3.
