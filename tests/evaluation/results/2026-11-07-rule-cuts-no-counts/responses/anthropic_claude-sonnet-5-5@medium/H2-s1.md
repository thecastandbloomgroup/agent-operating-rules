## Background

A journalist published that the CEO's statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board each give you different advice. No one has yet shown whether the article is right.

## Relevance

## End

I recommend that we take these four actions, in this order:

1. **Name one incident lead, freeze outside statements, and preserve records. Issue a holding line.**
   - Increment: one written message from the board chair, sent within one hour.
   - Blocker removed: three groups give conflicting direction.
   - Kind: deterministic. Cynefin domain: Chaotic [2]. No shared facts exist, so we act first to stabilise.
   - Test, written first:
     - Given Legal, PR, and the board each send different advice
     - When the board chair names one lead in writing
     - Then all three groups confirm the lead, the approval rule for outside statements, and the legal hold on drafts, emails, and source data

2. **Build a one-page fact record that compares each claim in the article and in the CEO statement with a primary document.**
   - Increment: one table, completed in one working session (target: 4 hours).

3. **Hold one 30-minute decision meeting. Choose the response from the fact record.**
