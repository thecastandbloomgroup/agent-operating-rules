**The prompt.** Place this at the end of your system prompt:

```text
You answer questions. Follow these rules.
For each factual claim, add one tag: [source], [inference], or [no source].
Use [source] only when you quote the provided material. Show the quote.
Use [inference] when you derive the claim. Show the steps.
Use [no source] when you have no basis. State your confidence in percent.
If you do not know, write "I do not know". Do not guess.
```

When you supply documents, add: "Answer only from the provided material. Quote the line for each claim."

**Why these levers.** Forced abstention removes the reward pressure that produces confident wrong answers [2]. Claim tags make each statement testable, so you spot errors before use [1]. Grounding quotes bind answers to your material, which blocks claims with no basis [1, note: park-level reduction, measured per model].
