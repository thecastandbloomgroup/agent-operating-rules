# Headline

Act within the next 24 hours: freeze all external statements, form one decision group, verify the claim against evidence, and prepare a single holding statement.

- First, stabilise: name one spokesperson and stop all other replies, because conflicting messages cause the most damage now.
- Next, verify: compare the CEO's statement against internal records before choosing a response.
- Then, respond: issue a holding statement within hours, and a substantive statement only after verification.

## Background

A journalist published a claim that the CEO's statement yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are messaging separately, and no one is aligned.

## Relevance

Unaligned public responses in a credibility dispute create legal exposure and reputational harm that outlast the original claim [judgment]. The first hours set the record that courts, regulators, and the press will cite later.

## Information

This situation has no single correct playbook because facts are unverified and stakeholders have conflicting interests. Per the Cynefin framework, the communications chaos is a Chaotic problem and the underlying fact question is Complicated [1].

Assumptions:

`A1. The CEO's statement was public and on the record. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: pull the statement text now. If wrong: crisis scope shrinks and Step 4 changes.`

`A2. The journalist's claim cites specific evidence. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: read the article in full. If wrong: the response can challenge the claim's basis rather than the facts.`

`A3. No regulator is already involved. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask legal today. If wrong: legal review moves ahead of all public statements.`

Alternative considered: issue an immediate public rebuttal. It loses because an unverified denial that later proves false compounds the damage, and retraction is costlier than a one-day delay [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these five actions, in order:

1. **Stabilise communications today.** Designate one spokesperson and tell legal, PR, and the board that all external responses route through that person. This removes the blocker of conflicting messages. Cynefin: Chaotic, so it comes first. Test (deterministic): Given an inbound press query, When any staff member receives it, Then the single spokesperson responds and others decline.
2. **Form a decision group of three: legal counsel, PR lead, and one board member, within 2 hours.** This removes the alignment blocker. Cynefin: Chaotic. Test: Given the group exists, When a response is drafted, Then all three sign off before release.
3. **Verify the claim against records within 24 hours.** Pull the exact statement, the data behind it, and the article's evidence. Task kind: evaluative. Write this rubric first: criteria are factual accuracy (weight 50%), completeness or missing context (30%), and legal exposure (20%). Pass threshold: every criterion scored against documentary evidence, not recollection. If the CEO's statement was inaccurate, the group decides between correction and clarification before any denial. Cynefin: Complicated, and Step 3 MUST NOT conclude until Step 2's group reviews its findings.
4. **Release a holding statement within 4 hours, before verification ends.** One to two sentences: the company is aware of the report, is reviewing it, and will respond with facts. This is Clear practice in crisis response [judgment]. Test (deterministic): Given a press inquiry before verification completes, When the spokesperson replies, Then only the approved holding statement is used.
5. **Publish a substantive statement within 48 hours of Step 3's conclusion.** Content depends on Step 3: correction if the claim is right, evidence-based rebuttal if it is wrong. Task kind: mixed, so it splits into the Step 3 evaluation plus this deterministic release. Cynefin: Complicated. Step 5 MUST NOT start until Step 3 passes its rubric.

If A3 proves false, replace Step 4's timing with "only after legal clearance."
