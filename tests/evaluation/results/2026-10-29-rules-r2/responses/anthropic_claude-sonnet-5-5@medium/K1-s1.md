**Candidate causes (MECE by the layer that adds the delay first)**

| Layer | Typical failure | Trace sign |
|---|---|---|

1. **Roll back on a canary slice.** Route 10% of traffic to the previous version for 60 minutes.
   - Blocker removed: users see 900 ms now, and we do not know if the release is the cause.
   - Kind: stochastic. Test first: metric is p95 on the canary. Baseline is 120 ms. Pass is p95 at or below 150 ms over at least 10,000 requests. Stop and widen the rollback if the pass holds.

2. **Rank the layers from the traces.** Split the slow traces into ORM, cache, and pool spans.

3. **Isolate the cause with a one-change test.** Replay production traffic in staging. Revert one change per run: ORM, cache, then pool.
