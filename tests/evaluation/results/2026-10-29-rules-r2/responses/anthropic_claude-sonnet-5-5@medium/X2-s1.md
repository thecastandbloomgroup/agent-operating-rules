## Background

Your team holds retrospectives (regular meetings to review how the team works). People attend, but nothing changes afterward. You ask what to do.

The material gives no data on action items, owners, or team size.

## Relevance

A meeting that changes nothing costs the team time each sprint. It also teaches people that their input does not matter, and attendance then falls.

## Information

Evidence:

- A retrospective has five phases: set the stage, gather data, generate insights, decide what to do, and close. The "decide what to do" phase produces the change. [1]
- The Cynefin framework separates work by how cause and effect relate. Here the cause is unknown, so the domain is Confused. [2]
- Four causes are possible: no action is chosen, an action has no owner, an action is not tracked, or the actions are too small to matter. [judgment]
