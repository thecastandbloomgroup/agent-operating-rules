## Information

**Use these error categories.** They separate content errors from presentation defects. [judgment]

| Category | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported claim | The summary states something the ticket does not support. | “Refund approved” when someone only requested it. |
| Contradiction | The summary conflicts with the ticket. | It names the wrong product. |
| Material omission | The summary excludes a fact needed for the next support action. | It omits a failed troubleshooting step. |
| Presentation defect | The facts are correct, but the summary is hard to use. | It repeats details or hides the next action. |

Mark an error **severe** when it can change the agent’s next action incorrectly. Treat incorrect amounts, commitments, account identities, and resolution status as severe. These are proposed review rules, not measured findings. [judgment]

**Track four measures.** Proposed definitions follow. [judgment]

- **Content-error rate:** summaries with an unsupported claim, contradiction, or material omission divided by summaries reviewed.
- **Severe-error rate:** summaries with at least one severe error divided by summaries reviewed.
- **Usable-summary rate:** summaries an agent can use without a material correction divided by summaries reviewed.
- **Operational cost:** response time in seconds and model cost per summary.

Report counts alongside percentages. For example, report “3 of 20,” not just “15%.” [judgment]

### 2. Review the pack and choose one failure cause

**Kind:** Evaluative. **Domain:** Complicated. Use source comparison and pipeline inspection to choose the fix.

**Rubric first:**

| Criterion | Weight | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Factual correctness | 40% | Each claim matches a source passage. |

After Step 1 passes, have an agent score all 20 cases. Have a second reviewer check every severe error and five other cases.

These branches are proposed diagnoses, not findings. [judgment] Produce one versioned candidate and its passing regression checks in one session.

**Acceptance test first:**

- **Pass:** Zero severe candidate errors; content-error rate at least 30% below baseline; usable-summary rate no lower.
- **Operational limits:** Agree response-time and cost limits before the test.
- **Budget:** One review session and at most 80 model calls.
