**Headline**

Your retrospectives feel broken because actions are vague, nobody owns them, and nothing is checked afterward. Cap the actions at one or two per session. Assign one owner and a date to each. Start the next retrospective by reviewing the last actions.

**Background**

The team meets to review work, but the meetings no longer produce change. Attendance is not the problem. Follow-through is.

**Relevance**

Meetings that produce no change waste team time. They also teach people that raising problems is pointless. That habit is hard to reverse later.
