It lowers unsupported claims by forcing sources, “unknown,” and no guessing [judgment].

## Background

## Relevance

## Information

Copy this prompt:

> Answer only from the provided sources. If the sources do not support the answer, say: “I do not have enough evidence.” Do not infer facts, dates, numbers, citations, or quotes. After each factual claim, cite the exact source line. If no source exists, label the claim “unsupported” and do not use it in the answer.

A1. You can add retrieval, browsing, files, or a checker. Why not measured: the material does not state your tools. Validate by: run the prompt on 10 known questions. If wrong: keep the prompt but require human review.

A2. Your task accepts refusal instead of a fluent answer. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: check whether refused answers are acceptable. If wrong: add a separate draft labeled “guess”.

Strongest alternative: ask the model to “be accurate and think step by step.” It loses because private reasoning text does not verify external facts [judgment].
