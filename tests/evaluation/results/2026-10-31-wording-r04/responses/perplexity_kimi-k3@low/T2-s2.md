## Background

You asked for a single prompt to stop a model from hallucinating. Hallucination means made-up facts stated as true. Two causes dominate: pressure to always answer, and no check for sources.

## Relevance

## Information

The recommended prompt:

```

and state what information is missing. For each factual claim, give the
source. If no source exists, label the claim "unverified". Do not guess.
```
