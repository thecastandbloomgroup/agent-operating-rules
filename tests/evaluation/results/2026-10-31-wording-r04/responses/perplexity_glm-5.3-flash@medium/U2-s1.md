## Information

Common causes of "off" numbers in quarterly reports, with no overlap between them [judgment]:

1. Arithmetic: row sums do not match the stated total.
2. Source data: the total does not match the system of record.
3. Definitions: the two sides use different quarter boundaries, currencies, units, or inclusion rules (for example, fiscal Q3 vs calendar Q3).
4. Presentation: rounding or a stale cut-off date hides a real change.
