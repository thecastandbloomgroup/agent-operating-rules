## Headline

Freeze all external statements now, verify the disputed claim with one named owner today, and then respond with one message sent through one voice.

- A single coordinator replaces the parallel channels from legal, PR, and the board.
- Nobody speaks outside that channel until the verification step is complete.
- After verification, the response follows one of three paths, set by the verified facts.

## Background

A journalist reports that the CEO's statement from yesterday is false. Legal, PR, and the board each contact you with their own view. You ask for the next steps. Nothing is verified yet.

## Relevance

Uncoordinated spokespeople create a second story about leadership, and that story can outlast the original claim [1]. The first hours decide the record.

## Information

Evidence and themes:

- Theme: respond fast and speak with one consistent voice. One source in this session supports it, and no source disagrees [1]. The framework behind the steps below also treats a sudden event with no visible cause and effect as Chaotic, where the first move stabilizes the situation [2].
- [judgment]: a freeze of a few hours costs less silence than unchecked replies cost in errors.

Assumptions:

- A1. The CEO's statement was public speech. Why not measured: the material does not state the venue. Validate by: read the article and the transcript. If wrong: an internal statement changes the audience and the legal exposure.
- A2. The statement is not a regulated financial disclosure. Why not measured: no tool could reach it in this session. Validate by: check the venue, for example an earnings call. If wrong: legal owns the timing, and Step 4 waits for their approval.
- A3. You are the coordinator until someone outranks you takes it. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: confirm in one message today. If wrong: hand over the Steps below to that person unchanged.

Alternative considered: answer each party as they ask. It loses because inconsistent messages undermine crisis credibility [1].

## End

I recommend that we run Steps 1 to 4, in this order.

1. Stabilize. Appoint one communication owner and send an internal notice today: no external comment until the owner approves it.
   - Increment: one notice, done in one hour.
   - Blocker removed: parallel messaging by legal, PR, and the board.
   - Test, written first (deterministic): Given the notice is sent, when any team member receives a press request, then they route it to the owner, unanswered.
   - Cynefin: Chaotic.

2. Verify. Collect three artifacts in one working session: the statement transcript, the article, and the underlying data.
   - Increment: a one page fact sheet that labels the mismatch as one of: the journalist is wrong, the CEO was imprecise, or the CEO was false.
   - Blocker removed: the three parties cannot converge without shared facts.
   - Test, written first (deterministic): Given the three artifacts, when they are compared line by line, then the fact sheet carries exactly one of the three labels.
   - Cynefin: Complicated.

3. Decide. Hold a 30 minute meeting today with the owner, one lawyer, the PR head, and one board representative.
   - Increment: one chosen path: demand a correction with evidence, publish a correction with disclosure, or clarify the imprecision.
   - Blocker removed: no aligned decision, so no approvable message.
   - Test, written first (evaluative, Kepner-Tregoe [15]): criteria are legal exposure (0.3), public trust (0.4), shareholder trust (0.3); the pass threshold scores highest on weighted total, with one line of evidence per criterion.
   - Cynefin: Complicated.
   - Dependency: this step MUST NOT start until the test in Step 2 passes.

4. Respond. Publish the approved message through the owner today, then monitor coverage for 24 hours.
   - Increment: one message plus one 24 hour monitoring window.
   - Blocker removed: the uncorrected record.
   - Test, written first (stochastic): metric is the count of follow-up stories that repeat the false version; baseline is the count before publication; pass threshold is no increase; sample is every story in the window.
   - Cynefin: Complex, so cap it at one 24 hour window before the next decision.
   - Hedge: if Step 2 labels the mismatch as a regulated disclosure, replace Step 4 with legal's approval gate.
