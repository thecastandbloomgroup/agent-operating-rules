## Headline

Freeze all external statements today, build one verified fact base, and publish one correction through one channel within 48 hours. The first blocker is the missing single decision owner, not the journalist's article. Legal, PR, and the board each hold part of the answer, so one owner must collect those parts. The correction decision follows the facts, not the pressure.

## Background

The CEO made a public statement yesterday. Today a journalist reported that the statement is false. Legal, PR, and the board are sending separate messages, and no one owns the response.

## Relevance

Every hour without an owner raises the risk of conflicting public replies. A public correction is hard to reverse, so the first steps control the whole case.

## Information

Evidence:

1. Response speed and the fit between response and crisis type drive reputation outcomes in crisis research [1].
2. A false organizational statement maps to strong organizational responsibility. In that class, correction or apology is the tested response type, and silence damages reputation [1].
3. Multi-party groups without an owner tend to produce inconsistent replies. [judgment]
4. The response should therefore move in the order: stabilize, verify, decide, correct, observe. Confidence: moderate [judgment], because it rests on the pattern in [1] and [2].

Alternative considered: publish nothing and let the story fade. It loses because the story is live and unopposed, and silence in a high responsibility crisis damages reputation [1].

Assumption log:

- A1. The journalist's report is materially accurate. Why not measured: the material does not state which evidence the report cites. Validate by: the fact memo in Step 2. If wrong: the output becomes a rebuttal and the order of Steps 3 and 4 stays.
- A2. The statement carried no legal privilege and sat in no regulated filing. Why not measured: the material does not state the sector. Validate by: one question to counsel through the owner. If wrong: counsel leads Steps 3 and 4.
- A3. No single decision owner exists today. Why not measured: three separate message streams suggest no owner. Validate by: board chair confirms a name by end of session. If wrong: skip the naming and go straight to Step 2.

## End

I recommend that we run these five steps in this order.

1. **Freeze external communication.** Domain: Chaotic. Kind: deterministic. Increment: in one session today, the board chair names one decision owner and sends a written freeze to legal, PR, and the board. Blocker removed: uncoordinated public replies. Test, written first: Given the freeze notice is sent, When any recipient receives a media question, Then the recipient routes it to the owner within 30 minutes and publishes nothing.

2. **Build the verified fact base.** Domain: Confused. Kind: deterministic. Increment: in one session, the owner compiles a memo with three parts: the exact CEO statement, the journalist's claim with its cited evidence, and all internal documents. Blocker removed: the three groups lack shared facts. Test, written first: Given the memo, When a claim has no source document attached, Then the memo marks the claim "unverified".

3. **Decide the response.** Domain: Complicated. Kind: evaluative. Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 2 ends. Increment: in one session, counsel and the owner score the options: correction, clarification, or silence. Blocker removed: no agreed choice between legal and PR priorities. Rubric, written first, Kepner-Tregoe form [wait for citation numbering; see 3]: legal exposure 40%, reputation effect 30%, consistency with the memo 20%, speed 10%, pass threshold 3.5 of 5 [judgment on weights]. Test: a completed score sheet that cites the memo per criterion.

4. **Publish one correction.** Domain: Clear. Kind: deterministic. Step 4 MUST NOT start until the Step 3 score sheet passes the threshold. Increment: in one session, publish the approved text on one channel, and state the accurate facts in plain terms. Blocker removed: the false record stays uncorrected. Test, written first: Given the approved text, When the channel publishes, Then the published text matches the approved text word for word.

5. **Run a reaction probe.** Domain: Complex. Kind: stochastic. Run Step 5 in parallel with Step 4, capped by its budget: 5 review hours total. Increment: track media and social tone for 48 hours after publication. Blocker removed: no early warning if the correction fails to contain the story. Acceptance test, written first: metric is the share of negative mentions; baseline is that share in the 24 hours before publication; pass threshold is a share below baseline at 48 hours with at least 200 mentions in the sample. Stop condition: no change at 48 hours, so escalate to the board with the data. [judgment]

- If A1 is false, replace Step 4 with an evidence-backed rebuttal that cites the internal documents from Step 2.

Open question (to counsel, through the owner): does privilege or a regulated filing cover the CEO statement? The answer decides who leads Step 3.
