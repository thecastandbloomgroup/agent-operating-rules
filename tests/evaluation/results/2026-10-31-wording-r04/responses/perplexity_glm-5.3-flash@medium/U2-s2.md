### Information

Number errors fall into four separate, checkable groups. This split does not overlap and covers the whole problem space. [judgment]

1. Arithmetic inside a cell: a formula or manual step produced a wrong value.
2. Totals and subtotals: rows or columns do not add to the stated sum.
3. Rates and growth: percentages, year-over-year change, or margin use the wrong divisor or period.
4. Cross-section mismatch: the same figure appears with different values in two sections, or it does not match the ledger.

The most likely case is group 4 or group 2, because both appear when figures are updated in one place only. [judgment] Confidence: low, because the message contains no data.
