Headline

Adopt a formatter, not a debate. SQL style stops causing review friction when a tool enforces it, so the fix is to remove the decision from humans and machinery.

1. Pick one tool, one dialect, and one rule set, and apply them to all SQL.
2. Enforce the rules in commits and in CI (continuous integration), so style stops reaching review.
3. Change the team norm: style comments are for unformatted SQL, and correctness comments are for logic.
4. Measure the effect, because whether the norm holds is not knowable in advance.

Background

The team disagrees on SQL formatting. Every review has arguments about it. You asked to fix this as a culture problem.
