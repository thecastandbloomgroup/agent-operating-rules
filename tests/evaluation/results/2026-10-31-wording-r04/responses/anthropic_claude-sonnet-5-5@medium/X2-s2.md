## Headline

Fix follow-through first, because the likely cause is that retrospective actions have no owner, date, or place in the daily work.

- A retrospective is a team meeting that reviews the last work period and picks improvements.
- First, measure how many past actions the team finished.
- Next, limit each retrospective to one change that has an owner and a date.
- Last, test a new format as a small probe, and escalate blockers outside the team.

## Background

Your team attends retrospectives, but nothing changes afterward. The request gives no data on past actions, team size, or method. I assume a software team that works in sprints (fixed work periods).

## Relevance

Each retrospective without a change costs the team time and trust. People stop offering honest input, so the problem grows.

## End

I recommend that we take these steps in order:

1. **Audit past actions.** Kind: deterministic. Domain: Confused (the cause is unknown, so this step gathers facts to classify it). Increment: one 45-minute session that lists every action from the last three retrospectives with owner and status. Blocker removed: we do not know the real completion rate. Test: Given the notes of the last three retrospectives, When we list each action with its owner and status, Then we have a completion rate and a list of the reasons for each unfinished action.

2. **Adopt a one-change rule.** Kind: deterministic. Domain: Clear. Increment: at the next retrospective, pick one change with a named owner and a due date, and put it in the backlog. Blocker removed: actions have no owner or place in daily work. Test: Given a finished retrospective, When we open the backlog, Then it holds one improvement item with an owner and a due date. Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 gives a baseline.

3. **Run a format probe.** Kind: stochastic. Domain: Complex (leverage is a guess). Increment: change the facilitator or format for two retrospectives, 60 minutes each. This probe runs in parallel with Step 2. Blocker removed: a stale format may hide real issues. Test: the metric is the share of actions finished within one sprint. The baseline comes from Step 1. The pass threshold is 80%. The sample is three retrospectives. Stop condition: stop after three retrospectives or 180 minutes in total, whichever comes first. Accept only when the threshold is met at that sample size.

4. **Escalate outside blockers.** Kind: evaluative. Domain: Complicated. Increment: take the unfinished items from Step 1 that need outside help and score them in a rubric. Criteria: impact on delivery (weight 50%), effort to fix (30%), team control (20%). Pass threshold: a weighted score of 3 or more on a 1 to 5 scale. Evidence for each criterion: the notes from Step 1. Blocker removed: items that the team cannot fix alone. Send the items that pass to the manager with a request for a decision date.

If A3 is false, move Step 3 to the first position and run it before Step 2.
