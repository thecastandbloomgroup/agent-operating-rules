I recommend Northwind Analytics, but only after the 3-year total is confirmed to stay within 400,000.

- Northwind costs 399,000 with expected data growth, which is 1,000 under budget. [judgment on the calculation, see A1 and A2]

## Background

We have three data warehouse proposals and a budget of 400,000 over three years. The data is 14 TB today and grows about 3 TB per year, per our team. No vendor states the staff cost to run the system or the price after year 3.

## Relevance

Northwind passes the budget by a very small margin. A small error in growth or billing terms changes the answer, so the checks below come before signature.

## Information

**Evidence**

- Northwind states 372,000 for three years. The licence covers 20 TB, and each extra TB costs 9,000 per year. [1, Proposal 1]
- Data reaches 17 TB, 20 TB, and 23 TB at the end of years 1, 2, and 3. Year 3 has 3 TB over the limit, so the overage is 3 × 9,000 = 27,000. The total is 399,000. [judgment]
- At 4 TB growth per year, the overage is 18,000 in year 2 and 54,000 in year 3. The total is then 444,000, which is over budget. [judgment]
- Bluepeak estimates 455,000 at current query volume. Pricing has no cap, so a query volume increase raises the cost. [1, Proposal 2]
- Cedar states 286,000, which leaves 114,000 of budget. Its hardware fits 30 TB. [1, Proposal 3]
- Cedar offers no SLA and next-day response for severity 1. Our team has one database engineer. [1, Proposal 3]
- Vendor benchmarks on a 10 TB sample give medians of 2.1 s (Northwind), 1.3 s (Bluepeak), and 3.4 s (Cedar). The data is vendor-stated only. [1, Proposal 1 to 3]
- Northwind offers 99.9% SLA and a free Parquet export. Both reduce the risk of lock-in. [1, Proposal 1]

Confidence that Northwind stays within 400,000: about as likely as not (33 to 66%). The basis is a 1,000 margin and one growth estimate from our own team.
