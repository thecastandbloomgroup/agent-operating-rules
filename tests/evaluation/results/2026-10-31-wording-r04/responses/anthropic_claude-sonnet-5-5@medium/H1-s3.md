## Background

## Relevance

- Failure for all users at one start time points to a shared component or a change, not a load spike. [judgment]
- The situation has no known cause and needs action now. Cynefin calls this Chaotic: stabilise first, then analyse. [2]

Assumption log:

A1. A deploy, config change, or flag change happened near 09:12. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: read the deploy log and flag history for 09:00 to 09:12. If wrong: skip Step 1 and start Step 3 at once.

Alternative considered: find the root cause before any rollback. It loses because a rollback restores service in minutes, while diagnosis time is unknown. [judgment]

1. **Roll back the latest change (Chaotic, deterministic).**
