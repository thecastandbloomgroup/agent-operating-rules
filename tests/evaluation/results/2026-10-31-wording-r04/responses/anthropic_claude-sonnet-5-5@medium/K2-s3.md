## Headline

Choose Northwind Analytics, but sign only after the overage terms and a test on our own data confirm the price.

- Bluepeak fails the budget: its estimate is 455,000 against 400,000, and its price has no cap.
- Cedar is cheapest but carries the highest run risk: it has no SLA and we have one database engineer.
- Northwind fits the budget by a narrow margin: 399,000 in the expected case, so the overage terms decide it.

## Background

We hold three vendor proposals for a data warehouse. The budget is 400,000 USD over three years. The current data size is 14 TB, and our team expects growth of about 3 TB per year.

## Relevance

The decision sets a three-year cost and a migration plan. Northwind has only 1,000 USD of margin, so one wrong input can break the budget.

## Information

**Evidence**

- Northwind states a base cost of 372,000, with a 20 TB licence and 9,000 per extra TB per year. [1]
- Data reaches 17, 20, and 23 TB at the end of years 1, 2, and 3. Only year 3 exceeds 20 TB, by 3 TB, which adds 27,000. The total is 399,000. [1]
- Bluepeak estimates 455,000 at current query volume. That is 55,000 (13.75%) over budget before any growth. [1]
- Cedar states 286,000, which leaves 114,000 (38,000 per year) for staff. It does not state staff cost. A half-time engineer likely costs more than 38,000 per year [judgment].
- Cedar offers no SLA, and its support responds the next day for severity 1. Northwind offers a 99.9% SLA and a 4-hour response. [1]
- All benchmarks use a 10 TB sample, not our 14 to 23 TB. The medians are 2.1 s for Northwind, 1.3 s for Bluepeak, and 3.4 s for Cedar. [1]

The pass threshold is 700 [judgment]. Northwind passes. Cedar does not.

**Uncertainty.** Northwind staying under 400,000 is about as likely as not (33 to 66%). The basis is a 1,000 margin on a growth figure that our team estimated. Each extra TB in year 3 adds 9,000.

**Assumption log**

A1. Growth is 3 TB per year, and Northwind bills overage on the year-end volume. Why not measured: the material states growth only as a team estimate. Validate by: read 12 months of storage logs and the billing clause. If wrong: cost rises 9,000 per TB, and growth of 3.5 TB per year gives about 421,500.

A2. Query speed on 14 to 23 TB stays near the 10 TB benchmark. Why not measured: no tool can reach the vendor systems. Validate by: run our 12 queries on a trial of our own data. If wrong: Cedar and Northwind lose speed, and the ranking may change.

A3. Business-hours support is enough for our users. Why not measured: the material does not state the support need. Validate by: ask the data users for their required hours. If wrong: Bluepeak's 24/7 support gains weight, but its price still fails.

A4. Northwind's price after year 3 is not yet known. Why not measured: no vendor states it. Validate by: request a written year-4 price. If wrong: the cost after year 3 may exceed the budget run rate.

**Alternative considered.** Cedar is the strongest alternative. It costs 113,000 less than Northwind, but it has no SLA, one engineer, and an unstated staff cost. It loses on run risk, which scores 3 against 8 [judgment].
