## Headline

Verify the facts first, hold all public comment until one person decides, and then give all three groups one message.

- Name one decision owner now, because three voices with equal weight produce no decision.
- Check every claim in the article and in the CEO statement against documents.
- Pick the response with a scored comparison, so the loudest voice does not win.
- Brief the board on one page, then watch the reaction for 24 hours.

## Background

A journalist published a report that says the CEO's statement from October 31 was false. Legal, PR, and the board each give you different advice. You are the person who must bring them together.

## Relevance

A second inaccurate statement would add legal and trust cost to the first. Each hour without alignment also lets the article set the frame of the story [judgment].

## Information

**Evidence**

- No document has been reviewed yet. Confidence that the article is accurate: low confidence, zero documents reviewed.
- The three groups disagree because each guards a different risk. Legal guards liability. PR guards reputation. The board guards company value [judgment].
- The Cynefin framework sorts work by how cause and effect relate, and it sets the form of each action below [1].

**Assumption log**

A1. The CEO statement is not yet checked against records. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the CEO for the source documents behind each claim. If wrong: if the statement is accurate, replace Step 3 with a factual rebuttal and a correction request.

A2. You can convene Legal, PR, and the board today. Why not measured: the material does not state your authority. Validate by: read the crisis plan or ask the board chair. If wrong: ask the chair to name the decision owner in Step 1.

A3. The company may be listed on a stock exchange. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask Legal in Step 1. If wrong: remove disclosure checks from Step 3.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is an immediate, firm denial to stop the story. It loses because the facts are unverified. A second false statement would raise legal exposure and damage trust more than a short delay [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these five actions in order:

1. **Stabilise: name one decision owner and one holding line.**
   - Increment: a 30-minute call with Legal, PR, and the board chair. A "holding line" is a short public sentence that says the company is reviewing the report.
   - Blocker removed: three groups give conflicting direction.
   - Kind: deterministic. Domain: Chaotic, so it goes first.
   - Test: Given three groups give conflicting advice. When the call ends. Then one named owner and one written holding line exist.
   - Also order a legal hold, that is, a written order to keep all related documents.

2. **Build a one-page fact sheet.**
   - Increment: a table with each claim in the article and in the CEO statement. Each row shows the supporting document and a status of confirmed, false, or unknown.
   - Blocker removed: nobody knows which claims are true.
   - Kind: deterministic. Domain: Complicated. Legal leads and the CEO supplies documents.
   - Test: Given the article and the statement. When Legal reviews the sheet. Then every claim has a document link or the status "unknown".
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start until this test passes.

3. **Choose the response with a Pugh matrix.**
   - Increment: a scoring grid that compares four options: stand by, clarify, correct and apologise, or stay silent.
   - Blocker removed: the options are argued by opinion, not by evidence.
   - Kind: evaluative. Domain: Complicated.
   - Rubric: accuracy against the fact sheet (35%), legal exposure (25%), stakeholder trust (25%), speed and reversibility (15%). Score each from 1 to 5 with evidence from Step 2. The pass threshold is a weighted score of 3.5, and the owner decides if two options tie.

4. **Align the board and issue one message.**
   - Increment: a one-page board brief with the facts, the chosen option, and the draft statement.
   - Blocker removed: three separate message streams.
   - Kind: deterministic. Domain: Clear. Use your standard approval process.
   - Test: Given the chosen option. When Legal, PR, and the board chair review the draft. Then all three approve in writing before release.
   - Step 4 MUST NOT start until Step 3 is scored.

5. **Run a 24-hour listening probe, in parallel with Steps 2 to 4.**
   - Increment: two people log outlet pickups and the questions from staff, customers, and investors.
   - Blocker removed: nobody knows how fast the story spreads.
   - Kind: stochastic. Domain: Complex. Leverage is a guess.
   - Budget: two people, 24 hours. Stop condition: three or more major outlets run the story, then escalate to the full crisis plan.
   - Acceptance test: metric is the share of the first 20 inbound questions that the holding line or the final message answers. Baseline: 0%. Pass threshold: 80%. Sample size: 20 questions.
