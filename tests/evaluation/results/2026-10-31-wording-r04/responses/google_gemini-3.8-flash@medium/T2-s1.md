### Headline

A single prompt cannot eliminate hallucination, but grounding constraints reduce false statements.
First, instructions that bind answers directly to provided text prevent unsupported facts.
Second, refusal rules give the model explicit permission to decline answering when information is absent.
Third, citation constraints require the model to identify source evidence before writing the conclusion.

### Background

You requested the best prompt to prevent language model hallucination.
Generative language models predict tokens through statistical patterns, which creates false statements without grounding [1].
Controlled studies show that constraint prompts and source grounding reduce these generation errors [2].

### Relevance

Therefore, selecting effective prompt patterns protects the accuracy of your system outputs.
