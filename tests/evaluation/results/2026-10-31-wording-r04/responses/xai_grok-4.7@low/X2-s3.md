Make each retrospective close one change before it collects the next idea.

Background
A retrospective is the regular meeting where the team looks for improvements.
People still attend.
The way the team works does not change after the meeting.
You asked for the next step.

Relevance
A meeting that never closes a change gets weaker each cycle.
Therefore the next meeting should finish one change instead of starting a new list.

A3. The team can complete a process change in one cycle without outside approval. Why not measured: the material does not state authority. Validate by: see if the last undone action needed a manager. If wrong: escalate that blocker and keep the team action inside team control.
