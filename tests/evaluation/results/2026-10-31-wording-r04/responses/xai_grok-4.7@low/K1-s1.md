Background
p95 is the latency that 95% of requests beat. It rose from 120 ms to 900 ms after release 2026.10.21. That release cut the pool, cut cache TTL (time to live), and added an ORM (object-relational mapper) load of order lines. This section covers what the logs, the diff, and the traces show.

Relevance
The slow span is pool acquire, so a query rewrite will not restore the prior p95. Error rate rose from 0.02% to 0.40%, and 96% of those errors are pool timeouts. Therefore the same queue that slows p95 also returns 503s.
