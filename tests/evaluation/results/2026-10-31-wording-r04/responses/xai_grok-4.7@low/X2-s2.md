Background
A retrospective is a regular meeting where a team inspects its work and picks a change. This team still meets, and people attend. Nothing in the work changes after the meeting.

Relevance
A meeting that never changes the work teaches people to ignore it. Therefore the next cycle should spend its time on one change the team can finish and check.
