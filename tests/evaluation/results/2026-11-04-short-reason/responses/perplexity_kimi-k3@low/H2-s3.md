## Background

A journalist published a claim that the CEO statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and the board are each messaging separately, and no shared position exists. The request asks for the next steps.

## Relevance

Contradicting replies within the first 24 hours of a public dispute raise litigation risk and reputational cost. Aligning the three groups on one verified factual basis protects every later decision, from correction to legal defense.

## Information

Holding all outbound comment for up to 24 hours is standard crisis practice because an unverified denial can convert a recoverable dispute into a credibility loss [judgment]. Preserving records before review matters because later claims of evidence destruction carry legal consequences. Internal privilege rules apply only when counsel directs the fact review, so route verification through Legal, not PR [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take four actions, in this order.

1. **Contain the situation (Chaotic).** Increment: within one hour, send one message to Legal, PR, and board contacts naming you as incident owner and freezing all external comment. Blocker removed: contradicting statements. This is Chaotic because cause and effect are not yet visible, so acting first to stabilize is required. Test (deterministic):
   - Given the incident owner message is sent,
   - When any party prepares an external statement,
   - Then it routes to the owner and gets Legal sign off before release.

2. **Separate fact from claim (Confused).** Increment: one working session to list each factual claim in the article against the CEO statement, word by word. Blocker removed: responding to an undefined accusation. This is Confused because the domain cannot be classified until the factual basis is known. Test (evaluative): rubric with two criteria, clarity (each claim quotes one sentence, weight 50%) and completeness (every claim listed, weight 50%), pass at 100% completeness, evidence is the article text and the statement text.

3. **Verify against primary records (Complicated).** Increment: one session, led by Legal, matching each claim to a document, recording, email, or data extract. Blocker removed: uncertainty about whether the CEO statement was true. This is Complicated because the answer is deducible from records once they are gathered. Test (evaluative): Pugh matrix, each claim scored supported, contradicted, or unverifiable against its primary record, and the verfication memo states the counts. Step 4 MUST NOT start until this test passes.

4. **Draft one aligned response (Complex).** In parallel with Step 3 and capped at two cycles, PR drafts three options: correction, partial clarification, or structured denial. Blocker removed: no ready message once facts land. This is Complex because audience reaction is visible only afterward, so treat each option as a probe with a stop condition. Test (stochastic): acceptance metric is approval by Legal, PR, and the board lead for exactly one option, baseline zero options approved today, threshold one approved option, sample size one cycle per draft before the freeze lifts.

If A3 is false, replace Step 1 with an immediate counsel approved holding statement.
