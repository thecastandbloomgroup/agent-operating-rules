## Background

The team still attends retrospectives. People come, but the work does not change. The request is what to do next.

## Relevance

Meetings without change train the team to ignore improvement talk. Therefore the next retrospective is the cheapest place to reverse that pattern.

## Information

The Scrum Guide requires the team to address the top improvement as soon as possible [2].

Kerth links a project review to follow-up after the meeting [3].
