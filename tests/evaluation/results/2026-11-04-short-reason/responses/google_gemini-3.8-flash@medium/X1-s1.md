### Background

Your support tool now includes a summariser based on a large language model.
Support agents report that the generated summaries are inaccurate at times.
The system currently lacks quantitative metrics and systematic tracking of error rates.

### Relevance

Deploying prompt changes without metrics creates regression risks that compound agent distrust.
Establishing an evaluation benchmark immediately reveals where the model fails and tracks future improvements.

### Information

Text summarisation models frequently hallucinate unsupported facts when prompt instructions lack strict source grounding [19].
Without an evaluation harness, teams cannot distinguish prompt improvements from random variance [20].
Subjective feedback from agents indicates a real quality issue, but it does not specify whether errors involve omissions, hallucinations, or formatting issues [judgment].
