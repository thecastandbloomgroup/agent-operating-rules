### Background

You asked for the best prompt to stop model hallucinations. Large language models generate tokens based on statistical probability rather than factual truth [1]. Therefore, prompt text alone cannot guarantee zero hallucinations across all outputs [2].

### Relevance

### Information

Evidence shows that retrieval-augmented generation combined with strict system prompts reduces factual errors. In benchmark studies, instructing models to rely only on provided text and say "I do not know" reduced hallucinations by over 70% compared to open-ended generation [1]. Conversely, open-domain prompts without context produce hallucination rates between 3% and 20% depending on the model and task [2].
