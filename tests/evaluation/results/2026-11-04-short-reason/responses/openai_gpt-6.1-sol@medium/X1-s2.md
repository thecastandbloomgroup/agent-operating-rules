### Define “better”

Use this initial scorecard. The categories and thresholds below are proposed acceptance criteria, not measured results. [judgment]

| Measure | Definition | Initial release gate |
|---|---|---|
| Serious error | Wrong customer, invented commitment, reversed outcome, or other mistake that changes the agent’s next action | Zero in the candidate sample |
| Other factual error | Unsupported or contradicted claim without a serious consequence | At least 30% fewer affected summaries than baseline |
| Required-fact omission | Missing customer issue, action already taken, current status, or next step, when present | No worse than baseline |
| Agent usefulness | Agent rates the summary at least 4 out of 5 for helping with the next reply | No worse than baseline |

Count affected summaries, not just errors. Also record error counts for diagnosis. [judgment]

Keep error causes separate:

- **Source failure:** the model never receives the required record.
- **Summary failure:** the record contains the fact, but the summary changes or invents it.
- **Requirement failure:** the summary follows its instructions, but agents need different content.

Classify each defect by its earliest cause to avoid counting it twice. [judgment]
