### 2. Write one complete change agreement

Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 passes.

**Test first, deterministic:**
```text

```

**Work:** Use 15 minutes during the retrospective to complete the agreement. Limit the change to one cycle and a stated effort budget.

Use this sentence:

**Blocker removed:** An idea cannot remain an unowned intention.

**Domain:** Clear. Deduction from the agreement’s required fields determines whether the document is complete.

### 3. Review the result before collecting new ideas

Step 3 MUST NOT start until the agreed review date, unless the stop condition occurs.

**Test first, deterministic:**
```text
Given the agreement and the available evidence,
When the next retrospective begins,
Then the team records the result and a decision before discussing new changes.
```

**Work:** Reserve the first 10 minutes for this review:

- Target met: retain the change.
- Target missed: stop it or write a revised agreement.
- Evidence insufficient: record what is missing and set one further review date.
- Authority blocked: name the decision-maker and request a decision.

**Capability added:** Each commitment receives an explicit outcome.

**Domain:** Clear. Deduction from the agreed target and review rules determines the next recorded step.

These checks establish follow-through; they do not guarantee that a change improves team performance. [judgment]
