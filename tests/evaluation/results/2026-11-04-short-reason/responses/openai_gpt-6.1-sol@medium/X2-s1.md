## Background

People attend your retrospectives, but you report that nothing changes. [1] The immediate task is to find the failed step, not assume that attendance means commitment. [judgment]

## Relevance

Another discussion format does not, by itself, address missing authority, work time, or review. [judgment] Therefore, make the next retrospective produce a small, visible commitment that the team can check. [judgment]

## Information

The Agile Manifesto links reflection with an adjustment in team behavior, not reflection alone. [2]

Use three checkpoints to locate the problem:

| Checkpoint | Evidence to inspect | Response if absent |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement | A specific change, an owner, and authority to act | Make the commitment explicit |
| Delivery | Evidence that someone completed the agreed work | Reserve work time or resolve the blocker |
| Results | Evidence against the intended outcome | Check the result before choosing another change |

These checkpoints are a practical diagnostic, not a diagnosis of your team. [judgment]

**Confidence:** High confidence that your reported problem concerns results; low confidence about its cause, because no past commitments are available. [1]
