## Headline

Make the retrospective produce one owned, tracked action per session, and check each action at the next session.

- The likely cause is that actions are not owned, not tracked, or too many [judgment].
- A short audit of past actions confirms or refutes this cause before you change the format.
- Small rule changes come first, because they cost little and reverse easily.
- A format change runs only as a capped trial.

## Background

Your team holds retrospectives (regular meetings to review how the team works). People attend, but no change follows. The message gives no data on past actions, team size, or meeting format.

## Relevance

Each meeting without change costs team time and lowers trust in the meeting. Attendance alone hides this loss.

## Information

Evidence:
- A retrospective has five phases: set the stage, gather data, generate insights, decide what to do, and close. Meetings that skip the "decide" phase produce talk but no change.

  [1]

- It is likely (66 to 100%) that the cause is weak follow-through on actions, not weak discussion [judgment].
- Work on a team's own process has cause and effect that are visible only afterward. This favors small trials.

  [2]

Assumption log:

A1. The team raises actions but does not finish them. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: count actions from the last three retrospectives. If wrong: the cause is in discussion quality, so replace Step 2 with a format change.

A2. The team has 5 to 9 people and meets every two weeks. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the facilitator. If wrong: adjust the sample size in Step 4.

A3. The team can edit its own backlog. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: check the tool. If wrong: track actions on a shared page instead.

Alternative considered: replace the format at once (new games, new facilitator). It loses because it treats the symptom, costs more, and gives no baseline to compare against [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these steps in order:

1. **Audit past actions (15 minutes).** The blocker removed: we do not know why nothing shifts. Cynefin domain (a framework that sorts work by how cause and effect relate): Confused, because the cause is unknown and this step gathers data to classify it. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   - Given the last three retrospective notes
   - When we count actions raised, owned, and finished
   - Then a table shows the three counts and the finish rate

2. **Limit each retrospective to one action with a named owner and a due date, placed in the team backlog.** Step 2 MUST NOT start until the table in Step 1 exists. The blocker removed: actions with no owner. Cynefin domain: Complicated, because the audit data picks the right rule by analysis. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   - Given a retrospective has ended
   - When we open the team backlog
   - Then it holds exactly one new action with an owner and a due date

3. **Open each retrospective by reviewing the previous action (5 minutes).** The blocker removed: no check on follow-through. Cynefin domain: Clear, because a known good practice applies by rule [1]. Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
   - Given a retrospective starts
   - When the facilitator opens the agenda
   - Then the first item is the status of the last action, marked done or not done

4. **Run a capped trial of a one-problem format, in parallel with Step 3.** Use one problem, one root-cause question chain, and one action. Budget: four retrospectives at 45 minutes each. Stop if attendance or participation drops in two sessions in a row. The blocker removed: shallow discussion, if A1 is wrong. Cynefin domain: Complex, because induction from trials applies and team mood could break the pattern. Leverage is a guess. Kind: stochastic. Test, written first:
   - Metric: share of actions finished before the next retrospective.
   - Baseline: the finish rate from Step 1.
   - Pass threshold: baseline plus 30 points, or at least 80%.
   - Sample size: four retrospectives (a small sample, so treat a pass as low confidence).

If A1 is false, drop Step 2 and run Step 4 as the main action.

Open questions: none. The assumptions in A1 to A3 cover the gaps.

Conditions that change the recommendation: see A1 and A3 above.
