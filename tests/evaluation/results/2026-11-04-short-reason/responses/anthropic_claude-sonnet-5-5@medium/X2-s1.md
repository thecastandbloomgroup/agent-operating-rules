## Headline

Find where the chain from retrospective to change breaks, then fix that link with one owned, scheduled action per session.

- First, measure what the last three retrospectives produced and what happened to it.
- Next, make each action small, owned, dated, and part of the team's normal work queue.
- Then, check whether fear or lack of authority blocks the team, because better actions cannot fix that.
- Last, run a capped trial of a changed meeting format.

## Background

A retrospective is a regular team meeting that reviews how the work went and picks changes. Your team attends, but nothing changes afterward. The request gives no data on the actions the meetings produce.

## Relevance

Each retrospective without change costs the team an hour per person. It also teaches people that the meeting does not matter, so attendance and honesty fall over time [judgment].

## Information

Evidence:

- A full retrospective has five phases. The last two are "decide what to do" and "close" [1].

- Many failures occur after the meeting, when no one owns the action. This is 55% likely [judgment], basis: no team data.

- No interviews or surveys were provided. No theme count is possible yet [no source].

Assumption log:

A1. The team runs retrospectives on a fixed cadence. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the facilitator for the last three dates. If wrong: Step 4 needs a new baseline.

A2. The meetings produce action items, but few get done. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: the count in Step 1. If wrong: replace Step 2 with a step that writes actions.

A3. The team controls its own process and board. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the manager one yes/no question. If wrong: Step 3 results go to the manager.

Alternative considered: stop retrospectives for a month and use 1:1 talks. This loses because it removes the shared forum, and Step 1 costs under one hour [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we:

1. **Audit the last three retrospectives.**
   - Increment: list each action with its owner and status (30 minutes).
   - Blocker removed: we do not know where the chain breaks.
   - Kind: deterministic.
   - Cynefin: Confused. The cause is unknown, so we gather data before we choose (Section 6.5).
   - Test:
     ```gherkin
     Given the notes of the last three retrospectives
     When the facilitator lists each action with owner and status
     Then every action is marked done, open, or dropped
     And the completion rate is recorded as the baseline
     ```

2. **Close the next retrospective with one committed action.**
   - Step 2 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 1 passes.
   - Increment: pick one action, name one owner, set a due date, and add a card to the team board. Open the following retrospective by reviewing that card.
   - Blocker removed: actions have no owner and no time.
   - Kind: deterministic.
   - Cynefin: Complicated. The Step 1 analysis picks which fix applies, so this is deduction.
   - Test:
     ```gherkin
     Given the retrospective is ending
     When the team closes the session
     Then exactly one action exists with a named owner and a due date
     And the action has a card on the team board
     ```

3. **Run a three-question anonymous poll on safety and authority.**
   - Increment: ask whether people speak openly, whether they expect change, and what blocks them. Close the poll after three working days.
   - Blocker removed: hidden fear or missing authority.
   - Kind: deterministic.
   - Cynefin: Complicated. Analysis of the answers sorts causes into themes, so this is deduction.
   - Test:
     ```gherkin
     Given a poll sent to every team member
     When the poll closes after three working days
     Then at least 80% of members have answered
     And each reported blocker is placed in a theme with a count
     ```

4. **Probe a changed format for three sessions.**
   - Step 4 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 1 passes. It then runs in parallel with Steps 2 and 3.
   - Increment: cut the meeting to 45 minutes, discuss one problem in depth, and end with one action.
   - Blocker removed: the current format may produce long lists that no one acts on.
   - Kind: stochastic.
   - Cynefin: Complex. Trials show what works (induction). A staffing change could break the pattern. The leverage is a guess.
   - Test, written first:
     - Metric: share of actions done by the next retrospective.
     - Baseline: the Step 1 rate.
     - Threshold: baseline plus 20 percentage points.
     - Sample size: three retrospectives, which is low power, so treat the result as a signal.
     - Stop condition: stop after two sessions if the rate is at or below baseline.
     - Budget: 3 x 45 minutes.
