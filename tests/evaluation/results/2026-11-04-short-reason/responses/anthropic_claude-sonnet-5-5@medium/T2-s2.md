## Headline

No prompt removes hallucination, but a grounded prompt with a refusal rule cuts it, so use the prompt below and test it.

- The prompt limits the model to supplied source text and gives it a safe way to say "unknown".
- The prompt fails when the source is missing or wrong, so the risk stays above zero.
- A small test on your own questions shows whether the prompt works for your model.

## Background

You asked for one prompt that stops a model from making up facts. A model writes the most likely next words, not checked facts. Therefore it fills gaps with plausible text.

## Relevance

Made-up facts in an answer cost you time or trust when you act on them. A tested prompt costs about one working session.

## Information

**The prompt.** Paste it before your question and your source text.

```text
Answer only from the SOURCE text below.
Do not use outside knowledge. Do not guess.
First, quote the exact sentences that support each claim.
If the SOURCE does not contain the answer, write: "The source does not say."
If you cannot quote support for a claim, delete the claim.
After your answer, list each claim with its quote.
```
