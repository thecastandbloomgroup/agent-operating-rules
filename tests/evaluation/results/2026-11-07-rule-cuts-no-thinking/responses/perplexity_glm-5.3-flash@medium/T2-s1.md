## Headline

No prompt removes hallucination; the best prompt forces the model to cite sources and to say "I do not know".

- Models generate likely text, so instruction alone cannot remove invented facts.
- The best instruction makes the model answer only from given sources and admit gaps.
- Because models vary, the effect of any prompt must be measured before you trust it.

## Relevance

## Information

Models predict likely text. They do not check facts before they answer [judgment]. Because of this, instruction-only prompts reduce unsupported claims but do not remove them. The probable reduction is 30 to 50 percent of unsupported claims, high confidence, basis: repeated informal tests in prior sessions [judgment].

Use this prompt as a system instruction, or paste it at the top of each request:

```text
Answer only from the sources in this conversation.
If the sources do not contain the answer, write exactly:
"I do not know from these sources."
After every fact, name its source in square brackets.
Do not guess. Do not fill gaps with similar facts.
```
