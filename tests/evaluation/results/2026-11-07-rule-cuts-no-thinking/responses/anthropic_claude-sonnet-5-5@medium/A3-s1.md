- JSON is repetitive text, so gzip usually cuts it by 70 to 90% [judgment].
- A smaller payload cuts transfer time and egress cost.
- The CPU cost at a middle compression level is small, and a canary test can confirm it.
- The gzip format is standard, and clients opt in through a request header, so the risk is low.
