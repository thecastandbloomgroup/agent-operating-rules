### Background

The support platform now uses an LLM to summarise customer support tickets.
Support agents report that generated summaries are sometimes wrong.
The engineering team currently tracks no quality metrics or error categories for these summaries.

### Relevance

Modifying the generation pipeline without metrics risks lower accuracy and harms agent trust [judgment].
