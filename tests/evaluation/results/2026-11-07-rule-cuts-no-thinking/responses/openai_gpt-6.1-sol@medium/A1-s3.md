## Information

A **version pin** selects an exact version, such as `package==1.2.3`. A **transitive dependency** is a package that another package requires.

Pinning only your direct dependencies leaves transitive dependencies free to change. Therefore, pin the complete resolved dependency set used by the build. [judgment]
