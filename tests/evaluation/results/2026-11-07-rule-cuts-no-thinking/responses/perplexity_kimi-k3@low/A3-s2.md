The analysis: JSON is text, and text compresses to about 10% to 20% of its original size, so a 400 KB response becomes roughly 40 to 80 KB on the wire [judgment][inference]. That cuts transfer time by a similar fraction, which reduces latency most on low-bandwidth links [judgment]. Gzip compression at moderate levels adds a few milliseconds of CPU per 400 KB response on current server hardware, a small cost compared to network time [judgment].
