### What to measure

Use the **summary** as the measurement unit. Count each summary once per defect type, even when it contains several defects.

| Defect | Definition | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Contradiction | The summary conflicts with the available ticket | Percentage with a contradiction |
| Unsupported claim | The summary states something the ticket does not support | Percentage with an unsupported claim |
| Omission | The summary leaves out information needed for the next support action | Percentage with an action-relevant omission |
| Clarity defect | The facts remain intact, but the text obscures their meaning | Percentage with a clarity defect |

These definitions form a proposed review standard, not measured results. [judgment] Give each defect one label; a summary can contain several distinct defects.

Also record severe errors separately. Examples include a false refund promise, an incorrect resolution status, or disclosure beyond the intended viewer’s access. [judgment]
