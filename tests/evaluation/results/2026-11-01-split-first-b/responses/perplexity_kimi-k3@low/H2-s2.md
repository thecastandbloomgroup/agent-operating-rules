# End

I recommend that we run these steps in order.

1. **Freeze outbound statements (Chaotic, first regardless of leverage).** Tell Legal, PR, and the board: no one comments for the next four hours except through one named spokesperson. This removes the blocker of contradictory public replies. Test (deterministic):
   Given the freeze notice is sent to Legal, PR, and the board,
   When each party replies with written acknowledgment,
   Then only the named spokesperson may issue any statement.

2. **Preserve evidence (Chaotic).** Issue a legal hold on the CEO's statement, drafts, and supporting records. This removes the blocker of lost proof later. Test (deterministic):
   Given the hold notice reaches IT and the CEO's office,
   When they confirm in writing,
   Then no related document or message may be deleted or edited.

3. **Verify the claim (Complicated).** In a two-hour working session, one fact owner compares the CEO's statement against primary records and the article's evidence, and produces a one-page verdict: true, false, or uncertain with a confidence level. This adds the capability of a shared fact base. Test (evaluative, rubric first): criteria are direct primary evidence (50%), corroboration by a second source (30%), and absence of contradictions (20%). Pass threshold is 80 of 100. The verdict MUST state its score and sources.

4. **Run one alignment session (Complicated).** One 45-minute meeting with Legal, PR, and the board lead. Input: the fact verdict from Step 3. Output: one agreed message and one spokesperson, signed off by all three. This removes the blocker of parallel decision chains. Step 4 MUST NOT start until Step 3 is complete. Test (deterministic):
   Given the verdict is circulated before the meeting,
   When the meeting closes,
   Then all three parties confirm the message and spokesperson in writing.

5. **Publish one statement within 24 hours (Complicated).** If the statement was false: a direct correction, no blame, next steps. If true: rebuttal with evidence and a request for correction. If uncertain: state what is known, what is checked, and when an update follows. Test (deterministic):
   Given the approved message from Step 4,
   When the statement is released by the single spokesperson,
   Then its content matches the approved text word for word and one owner tracks inbound reactions for 48 hours.

Hedge: if A2 later shows the article verifiably false, replace the correction in Step 5 with the rebuttal path.
