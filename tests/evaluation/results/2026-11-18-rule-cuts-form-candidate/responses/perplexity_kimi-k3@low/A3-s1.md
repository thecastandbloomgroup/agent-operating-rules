Yes, turn on gzip. JSON compresses well, typically 5 to 10 times smaller, so 400 KB responses drop to roughly 40 to 80 KB. This reduces bandwidth cost and speeds up transfers for clients on slower connections. Compress only at a moderate level (for example gzip level 6) to balance CPU cost against size, and skip compression for clients that do not send Accept-Encoding: gzip. Also note that large texts compress less effectively than typical JSON with repeated keys, so measure your actual ratio after enabling it.
