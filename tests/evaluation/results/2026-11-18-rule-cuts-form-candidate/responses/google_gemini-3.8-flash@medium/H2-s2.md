## Background

A journalist published an article that accuses the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of making a false statement yesterday.
The board of directors, Legal, and Public Relations (PR) are sending conflicting demands without alignment.
The company faces immediate external scrutiny and internal operational conflict.

## Relevance

Conflicting statements to the public or the board create legal risk and governance failure.
Therefore, command control must be established within the hour to protect the organization.
