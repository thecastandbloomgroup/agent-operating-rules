### Information

Retrospectives stall when teams generate too many action items without dedicated delivery capacity [1].
In addition, teams often write vague resolutions rather than verifiable changes [1].
Derby and Larsen show that limiting output to one high-priority experiment per retrospective increases completion rates [1].
The Scrum Guide also specifies that the sprint backlog must include at least one high-priority process improvement item [2].
