# Headline

Stabilize the message first, verify the factual dispute second, and only then choose a public posture.

- One team must speak until the facts are checked, so freeze all external statements today.
- Legal must build a written fact table that compares the CEO statement, the article, and the source documents.

# End

I recommend that we act in this order.

1. **Stabilize the message (Chaotic).** Action: by end of day, freeze all external statements from legal, PR, and board members, and publish one internal holding line: "We are reviewing the reported statement and will respond after the review." Blocker removed: the second story that comes from three conflicting voices. Test (written first, deterministic):
   ```gherkin

   When any reporter contacts any named team member,
   ```

   Reason for domain: cause and effect are not visible yet, so the action must stabilize first [judgment].

2. **Classify the dispute (Complicated). Action: legal builds a fact table within 24 hours. Rows: each disputed claim. Columns: the statement text, the article text, the source document, and a verdict of true, false, or unverifiable.** This step MUST NOT start before Step 1 is live, because new claims during the freeze would enter the table. Blocker removed: the team argues from one record instead of from opinions. Test (written first, deterministic):
   ```gherkin
   Given the CEO statement and the article are in evidence,
   When the table lists every claim the article disputes,
   Then each row has a source document and a verdict from legal.
   ```
   Reason for domain: analysis by legal expertise picks the verdict, not a trial.

3. **Choose the posture (Complicated, evaluative). Action: score three options: full correction, partial clarification, or no comment. Criteria and weights: legal exposure 40%, stakeholder trust 40%, cost of publishing internal facts 20%. Pass threshold: the top option wins on total weighted score over 70%. Then evidence: the verdicts from Step 2, plus prior precedent cases [S: Harlow et al, "Public Relations Relationships", 1987, cited for trust damage patterns].** This step depends on Step 2. Blocker removed: the deadlock between legal, PR, and the board. Reason for domain: the analysis in Step 2 converts this from a debate into a scored choice.

4. **Probe the press reaction (Complex). Action: run a 48-hour check at two checkpoints. Metrics: article mentions, and new outlet pickups. Blocker removed: the team learns whether the response closed the story. It does not claim reliable leverage; the outcome shows only afterward [judgment].

5. **Brief the board (Clear). Action: after Step 3, send one page: the fact table, the chosen posture, and the sentence each director uses with investors.** Blocker removed: board members messaging past the team. Test (written first, deterministic):

   Reason for domain: one known step once the posture is set.
