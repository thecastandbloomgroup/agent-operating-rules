## Headline

Retrospectives stop producing change when action items have no owners, no dates, and no check for completion; repair the loop after the meeting, not the meeting itself. Supporting points:

1. First measure where items stop, because the fix depends on the stop point.
2. Then allow only one action item per retro, with one owner, one date, and one checkable outcome.
3. Then open every retro by testing the previous action item.
4. Then measure the completion rate over several cycles to confirm the loop works.

## Background

A team holds retrospectives, people attend, and no process change follows. The request is a plan to make retrospectives produce change again.

## Relevance

Repeating a meeting that produces nothing costs hours each cycle and erodes the habit of speaking up. This plan gives change in one month.
