## Headline

Stabilize the message flow first, verify the facts second, and publish one corrected statement only after legal, PR, and the board agree in writing.

- Today's job is to stop three groups from sending three different messages. Divergent messages change a bad story into a credibility story [judgment].
- One written alignment decision today removes the largest blocker: no group can act alone.
- Verification of the journalist's claim decides whether the response is correction, defense, or silence.
- A public response within the first 24 hours beats a fast response that is later proven wrong [judgment].

## Background

The CEO made a public statement yesterday. A journalist published today that the statement is false. Legal, PR, and the board all contacted the same person at the same time, and their messages do not agree.

## Relevance

The first public response sets the story for the next news cycle. Because three teams compete for control now, the cost of delay is a message the company does not control.

## Information

First, separate the two problems. Problem one is coordination: three groups with authority and no shared decision. Problem two is truth: whether the CEO statement matches the record. Problem two decides the content of problem one, so verify before you align on wording. However, do not wait for verification to stop divergent messages [judgment].

Verify with this sequence:

1. Collect the exact text of the CEO statement, the article, and the underlying record, such as filings, minutes, or data.
2. Have one legal officer mark each disputed point as true, false, or unverifiable.

Assumption log:

- `Assumption: the disputed statement may create a legal or disclosure duty. Why not measured: the material does not state the subject of the statement. Validate by: ask legal to screen the claim against disclosure rules today. If wrong: the response is a PR task only, and the legal review step shortens.`
- `Assumption: the full statement text and the journalist's article are retrievable now. Why not measured: no tool can reach it in this session. Validate by: one person collects both texts and confirms they are complete. If wrong: verification stalls, so the alignment meeting uses the recorded summary only.`
- `Assumption: one incident exists, not a pattern of disputed statements. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: search prior statements for the same topic. If wrong: the response plan changes to a governance review, not a single correction.`

Alternative considered: publish a rebuttal today without verification. It loses because a rebuttal that later proves wrong costs more than one silent news cycle [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we act in this order:

1. **Freeze external statements for 12 hours.** No one outside this group speaks for the company, and every internal contact routes through one owner. Blocker removed: divergent messages. Test, deterministic:
   ```gherkin
   Given a press inquiry arrives
   When any employee responds
   Then the response is "Our spokesperson will contact you" and nothing else
   ```
   Domain: Chaotic, because no response pattern is visible yet, so stabilization comes before analysis. Leverage: a guess.

2. **Verify the disputed statement against the record.** Legal marks each point true, false, or unverifiable, and writes it down. Blocker removed: the company does not know what it must correct. Test, evaluative, rubric: each disputed point scores verified (weight 50%), contradicted (30%), unverifiable (20%); pass threshold is 100% of points classified; evidence is the document quoted for each point. Domain: Complicated, because the claim is knowable by document comparison. Leverage: reliable after this step.

3. **Hold one alignment meeting after the verification in Step 2 is complete.** Legal, PR, and one board member decide one of three paths: correct, defend, or no comment. The decision is written and signed by all three. Blocker removed: no group can act alone. Test, deterministic:
   ```gherkin
   Given the three groups meet
   When they select a path
   Then one named person signs the decision and it is sent to all three groups
   ```
   Domain: Clear, because a decision protocol exists and all parties apply it. Leverage: reliable.

4. **Publish the agreed response within one news cycle of the decision.** PR drafts it from the Step 2 verification record, and legal approves the final text before release. Blocker removed: the company controls its first public correction. Test, evaluative, rubric: the text matches the Step 2 record point by point (weight 60%), it states a correction date when any point is false (30%), it names the signatories (10%); pass threshold is 100%; evidence is the diff against the verification record. Domain: Complicated, because drafting follows the known record through analysis. Leverage: reliable.

5. **Run a 30-day post-incident review as a capped probe.** One measure: number of disputed public statements in the trailing quarter. Stop condition and budget: one meeting, four hours total. Blocker added: capability to detect a pattern, per the third assumption. Domain: Complex, because cause and effect between statements and trust are visible only afterward. Leverage: a guess.
