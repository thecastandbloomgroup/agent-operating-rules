3. **Verify (Complicated, deterministic).** Within 24 hours, general counsel leads a fact matrix. Baseline: coverage today. Pass: above 50% of at least 20 new articles in 48 hours. Stop condition: counsel flags new exposure, or claim volume doubles. Budget: two communications staff and no paid media. Domain: Complex; induction from a trial, and the pattern breaks if a second outlet publishes new documents. Leverage for this step is a guess.
