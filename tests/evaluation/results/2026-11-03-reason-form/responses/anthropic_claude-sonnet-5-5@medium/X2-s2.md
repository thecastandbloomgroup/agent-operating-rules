## Headline

I recommend that we fix the follow-through on retrospective actions before we change the retrospective itself.

- Retros likely end without actions that have an owner and a date. [judgment]
- Improvement work has no reserved capacity in the sprint. [judgment]
- A repeated format produces the same shallow insights each time. [judgment]

## Background

The team attends retrospectives (retros), but no process change follows. The request asks what to do. The material gives no data on action items, so Step 1 collects it.

## Relevance

A retro that changes nothing costs team time every sprint. It also teaches people that speaking up has no effect, so attention drops further.

## Information

**Evidence**

The Scrum Guide says the team should address the most impactful improvements as soon as possible. It adds that the team may put them in the next Sprint Backlog [1].

Derby and Larsen describe a retro in five phases. The fourth phase is "decide what to do" [2].

A retro that skips this phase, or leaves its output unowned, produces talk without change. That is, the failure sits after the discussion, not in it. [inference]

Confidence that follow-through is the main cause: medium confidence, because one pattern from two sources supports it and there is no team data yet.

**Assumption log**

A1. The team produces action items, but few are finished. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: count the items from the last three retros. If wrong: replace Step 2 with Step 4.

A2. The team can change its own process without outside approval. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: ask the team lead. If wrong: add an escalation action.

A3. The team uses a regular sprint cycle with a backlog. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: check the team board. If wrong: use any visible task list.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is to replace the retro with a new format at once. It loses because a new format with no owned actions gives the same result. [judgment]

## End

I recommend that we:

1. **Audit the last three retros.** Increment: one table with items, owners, due dates, and status. It removes the blocker of an unknown cause. Domain: Confused, because the cause of the stall is not visible, so this step gathers data to classify it. Kind: deterministic.
   - Given the notes of the last three retros
   - When the team counts items, owners, and finished items
   - Then a table shows the completion rate as a percentage

2. **Cap each retro at two actions, each with one owner and a due date, and place them in the sprint backlog.** Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 confirms A1. Increment: one retro run with this rule. It adds reserved capacity. Domain: Clear, because the Scrum Guide gives a known practice [1]. Kind: deterministic.
   - Given a retro has ended
   - When the team lists its chosen actions
   - Then each action has one owner, one due date, and one backlog card

3. **Open every retro with a ten-minute review of the previous actions.** Increment: one agenda change. It makes unfinished work visible. Domain: Clear, because it applies a known practice. Kind: deterministic.
   - Given the previous retro created actions
   - When the new retro starts
   - Then the team reports each action as done, open, or dropped before any new topic

4. **Run a probe: change the retro format for two retros.** Step 4 runs in parallel with Steps 2 and 3. Increment: two retros with a new format, such as a different prompt or a focus on one topic. Domain: Complex, because the effect of a format is visible only afterward. Leverage is a guess. The pattern could break if team trust, not format, is the cause. Kind: stochastic.
   - Metric: share of committed actions finished by the next retro.
   - Baseline: the rate from Step 1.
   - Pass threshold: 75% finished, or 20 points above the baseline.
   - Sample size: two retros, with a third to confirm.
   - Budget: 45 minutes per retro.
   - Stop condition: stop if attendance or finished actions fall below the baseline.

Conditions that change the recommendation:

- If A2 is false, add a first action to take the blocked items to the manager.
