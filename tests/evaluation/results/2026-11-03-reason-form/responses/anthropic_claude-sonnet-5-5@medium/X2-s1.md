## Headline

Make every retrospective end in one owned, dated change, and review that change at the start of the next retrospective.

- The likely cause is weak follow-through, not the meeting itself. [judgment]
- The fix is to put one change in the team backlog with an owner and a date.
- Run any change to the meeting format as a separate, capped trial.
- Measure the result by the share of agreed changes that reach done.

## Background

The team holds retrospectives (meetings where a team reviews how it works and picks improvements). People attend, but no change follows. The request gives no data on team size, format, or past action items.

## Relevance

Each empty retrospective costs the whole team one meeting per sprint (a fixed work cycle, often 1 to 2 weeks). Trust also falls, because people learn that speaking up changes nothing. [judgment]

## Information

Evidence:

The Scrum Guide says the team picks the most helpful changes and addresses the most impactful ones as soon as possible.

[1]

Derby and Larsen set "decide what to do" as its own phase of a retrospective, followed by a close.

[2]

Theme: stalled retrospectives come from items with no owner, no date, or no place in the backlog. Two sources support the theme. No source disagrees. Confidence is low, because the basis is two general sources and no data from this team.

Assumption log:

A1. The team holds retrospectives on a fixed cycle and keeps notes. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: count the action items in the last three sets of notes. If wrong: Step 1 becomes "start recording action items."

A2. The main cause is weak follow-through, not low trust in the room. Why not measured: no tool can reach the team. Validate by: Step 1 results plus a one-question anonymous poll on whether it is safe to speak. If wrong: replace Step 4 with a trial on safety.

A3. The team has authority to change its own process. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask the team lead. If wrong: add an escalation path for items outside team control.

Alternative considered: cancel retrospectives and fix problems as they appear. It loses because it removes the only scheduled place to raise shared problems. [judgment] A new facilitator or a new format also loses as the first move. Neither one fixes a missing owner or date. [judgment]

## End

I recommend that we take these steps, in this order:

1. **Audit the last three retrospectives.**
   - Increment: one hour. List each action item with its owner, date, and outcome.
   - Blocker removed: the cause of the stall is unknown.
   - Kind: evaluative. Rubric written first. Criteria and weights: had an owner (30), had a date (20), reached done (30), was within team control (20). Evidence: the retrospective notes and the backlog. Pass threshold: if fewer than 50% of items reached done, follow-through is the cause.
   - Cynefin domain: Complicated. Cynefin sorts work by how cause and effect relate [3]. Analysis of records picks the practice (deduction from data).

2. **Limit each retrospective to one item, or two at most. Put each in the sprint backlog with an owner and a date.**
   - Increment: one retrospective.
   - Blocker removed: items have no owner and no place in the work plan.
   - Kind: deterministic. Test written first, in Gherkin (a Given, When, Then test format):

     Given a retrospective has ended
     When the team lists the agreed changes
     Then each change has one owner, one date, and one sprint backlog entry

   - Cynefin domain: Clear. Owner, date, and backlog entry are known good practice (apply a known rule).
   - Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 is done, so the team knows whether the cause is follow-through.

3. **Open each retrospective with a review of the prior items.**
   - Increment: one 10-minute agenda slot.
   - Blocker removed: no one checks whether items were done.
   - Kind: deterministic. Test written first:

     Given a retrospective starts
     When the facilitator opens the agenda
     Then the first item is a status check of each prior change: done, open, or dropped

   - Cynefin domain: Clear (apply a known rule).
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start until Step 2 has produced at least one item.

4. **Run a format trial in parallel with Steps 2 and 3, after Step 1.** Change one thing: for example, vote on the topic first, then discuss only the top topic.
   - Increment: one changed agenda, 15 minutes of preparation.
   - Blocker removed: the format may not surface the right topics. This is a guess. [judgment]
   - Kind: stochastic. Acceptance test written first. Metric: share of items done by the next retrospective. Baseline: the Step 1 result. Pass threshold: baseline plus 25 percentage points. Sample size: 4 retrospectives, which is small, so treat a pass as a signal, not proof.
   - Cynefin domain: Complex. Effects show only afterward (induction from trials). What could break the pattern: a change in team members or a release crunch. Leverage is a guess.
   - Budget: 4 retrospectives. Stop condition: if the done rate falls below baseline after 2 retrospectives, revert to the old format.

Condition that changes this: if A2 is false, replace Step 4 with a trial on safety, such as anonymous topic submission.
