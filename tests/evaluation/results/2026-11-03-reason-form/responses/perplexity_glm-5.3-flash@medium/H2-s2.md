## Headline

Stabilize the message flow before you craft any message. First, freeze external statements and name one spokesperson. Second, build a verified, claim-by-claim fact base. Third, pick a response posture with scored criteria, with legal, PR, and one board delegate together. Fourth, issue one approved statement, internally first, then externally.

## Background

A published article says the CEO's public statement from yesterday was false. Legal, PR, and board contacts each send direct messages, and no shared line of response exists. Uncoordinated replies during an active dispute are the main risk here [judgment].

## Relevance

Every independent message can be quoted, contradicted, or entered into a legal record [judgment]. The first hours shape both the press narrative and the legal position, so stabilization buys the time that the fact check needs [judgment].

## Information

The first two steps do not depend on whether the article is correct. Confidence: high for their value, because they hold under both outcomes of the fact check.

The situation matches the Chaotic domain of Cynefin: no clear cause and effect is visible yet, so the rule is to act first to stabilize, then analyze [18].

Assumption log:

- A1. The article is public and gaining reach. Why not measured: the material does not state the reach. Validate by: check the article page and one social measurement, inside 30 minutes. If wrong: the freeze still applies, but the response timeline can slow by a day [judgment].
- A2. The CEO's statement exists as a transcript or recording. Why not measured: no tool can reach it from here. Validate by: collect the highest-fidelity copy from comms archives. If wrong: rebuild the statement from emails and meeting notes before Step 3 [judgment].
- A3. No one has sent a media statement yet. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: ask each team lead for copies of anything sent in the last 24 hours. If wrong: audit those messages at once, because they may become evidence [judgment].

Alternative considered: respond at once with a short denial from the CEO. It loses because an unverified denial that later fails costs more trust and legal exposure than 12 quiet hours [judgment].

## End

I recommend that we take these four steps, in this order.

**Step 1, within 1 hour. Freeze all outbound statements and name one spokesperson.**
- Increment: one message to legal, PR, and board leads: hold all external statements until Step 3 closes. Name the single spokesperson (the one person who talks to press) and one deputy.
- Blocker removed: independent messages from the three groups, the current largest source of risk.
- Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
  - Given the freeze notice exists with the two names,
  - When each lead receives it,
  - Then each lead replies with confirmation, and zero outbound statements go out before approval.
- Cynefin: Chaotic, because the move is act-first stabilization, per [18]. Leverage estimate: a guess at this stage.

**Step 2, within 4 hours, only after Step 1 confirms. Build the fact base.**
- Increment: pull the statement and the article. For each claim in either text, code it: supported, false, or unverified, with one source per code.
- Blocker removed: the unknown facts that Step 3 needs.
- Kind: evaluative. Rubric, written first: criteria: correctness (weight 60), completeness (weight 40). Evidence: primary documents only. Pass threshold: 100% of claims coded, 0 conflicting codes left open.
- Cynefin: Complicated, because analysis by legal and comms picks the findings.

**Step 3, same day, only after Step 2 passes. Decide the response posture.**
- Increment: one 30-minute meeting. Attendees: legal lead, PR lead, one board delegate, the CEO as needed. Decision: correct, clarify, stay silent, or escalate.
- Blocker removed: the alignment gap between the three groups.
- Kind: evaluative. Rubric, written first, as a weighted score per Kepner-Tregoe [15]: legal exposure (40), trust recovery (35), speed (25). Pass: the top-scoring option wins, and the score sheet is the written record.
- Cynefin: Complicated, because the scored analysis picks the option.
- Dependencies: Step 2 MUST NOT end without 100% claim coverage. Step 3 MUST NOT start until then.

**Step 4, within 24 hours. Issue one approved statement, internally first, then externally.**
- Increment: one text, approved by legal and PR leads, sent to employees first, then to the journalist and press channels.
- Blocker removed: the public information vacuum that others then fill.
- Kind: deterministic. Test, written first:
  - Given the board's approved text,
  - When the statement is published,
  - Then the published text matches it word for word, and every prior internal channel receives a link to it.
- Cynefin: Clear, because publishing an approved text is one known step.

Condition that changes this plan: if A3 is false and a bad statement is already public, replace Step 1 with a recall and audit of that statement before anything else.

## Follow-up

Three questions, each with its use:

1. What is the article's URL and reach? This sets the response clock in A1.
2. Has anyone outside the company already received a company statement? If yes, this becomes the first audit item in Step 4.
3. Is any regulator, investor, or contract notice involved? If yes, Step 3 adds a disclosure-obligation criterion with weight above legal exposure [judgment].
