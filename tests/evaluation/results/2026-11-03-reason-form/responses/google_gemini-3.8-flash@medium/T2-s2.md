### Background

You asked for the best prompt to stop hallucinations in language models.
Language models generate text based on statistical token prediction rather than factual lookup [1].
Therefore, models invent details when context is missing or ambiguous [2].

### Relevance

Hallucinations cause errors in production tasks.
Adding grounding constraints to prompts reduces manual review time immediately [judgment].

However, prompt engineering alone cannot reduce hallucinations to zero percent [judgment].
