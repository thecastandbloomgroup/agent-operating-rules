## Headline
Teams restore retrospective impact by limiting commitments to one visible sprint backlog item and reviewing past actions first.
First, tracking past commitments at the start of each meeting exposes execution failures immediately.
Second, placing improvement items into the sprint backlog gives them equal priority with delivery work.
Third, restricting changes to one item per sprint prevents split focus and abandoned work.

## Background
The team attends regular retrospective meetings, but discussions no longer generate practical changes.
Team members experience meeting fatigue because prior action items produce no visible results in daily work.
The user seeks an intervention to restore actionable outcomes from these recurring sessions.

## Relevance
Unproductive retrospectives consume team labor hours without improving delivery speed or team morale.
Therefore, restoring traction on team improvements prevents process stagnation and disengagement.

## Information
This section covers the evidence, assumptions, and alternative options.
Derby and Larsen show that retrospectives fail when teams create long lists of unassigned improvements [1].
Kerth observes that without an explicit review of past commitments, teams repeat discussions without closing work [2].
The Scrum Guide requires at least one high-priority process improvement in the sprint backlog to guarantee execution [3].
However, teams struggle when commitments exceed execution capacity, so limiting work in progress preserves momentum [judgment].
A confidence level of high confidence, based on three independent industry texts, supports focusing on one backlog item [judgment].

Evidence and assumptions follow below.
See Section 5.2 for the recorded project assumptions:
A1. The team uses two-week sprints and a standard task board. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Inspecting the team project management tool. If wrong: Adapt the action cadences to match the team delivery cycle.
A2. The team retains the authority to modify its own internal working agreements. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Asking the team manager during the next standup. If wrong: Escalate proposed workflow changes to external management for approval.

Alternative considered:
Replace retrospectives with an asynchronous feedback form.
This alternative loses because asynchronous forms lack immediate collaborative debate, that is, collective commitment drops without direct discussion [judgment].

## End

1. Update the retrospective agenda template to include an opening review of past action items.
- Increment: Add a dedicated review section to the meeting agenda template.
- Blocker: The team forgets past commitments because meeting documents lack an opening review section.
