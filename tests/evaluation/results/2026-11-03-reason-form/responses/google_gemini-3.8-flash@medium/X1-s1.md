### Background

Support agents report that the new language model summariser generates incorrect information.
However, the system currently lacks performance metrics, error logging, and ground truth datasets.
The team needs a reliable measurement process to diagnose errors and track improvements.

### Relevance

You cannot fix an unmeasured system without risking regressions in support workflows.
Establishing metrics now prevents wasted engineering effort on untested prompt changes.

### Information

Language model summaries often fail through factual hallucination, omission of critical ticket details, or wrong entity attribution [1].
Without an evaluation harness, prompt adjustments degrade output quality unpredictably [judgment].
Therefore, we must build an evaluative dataset from production tickets [2].
