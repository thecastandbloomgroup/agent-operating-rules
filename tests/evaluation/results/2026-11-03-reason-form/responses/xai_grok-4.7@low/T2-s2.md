## Background

The request asks for one prompt that stops hallucination. Hallucination here means a fluent claim that is false or unfaithful to the given source. Surveys treat both forms as open problems in current models.

## Relevance

An open prompt still lets the model invent missing facts on the next answer. A source-bound prompt cuts that path before you spend time on a wrong reply.

## Information

Ji and coauthors define hallucination as output that is nonsensical or unfaithful to the provided source.

[1]

Huang and coauthors report that detection and mitigation remain unsolved across model families.
