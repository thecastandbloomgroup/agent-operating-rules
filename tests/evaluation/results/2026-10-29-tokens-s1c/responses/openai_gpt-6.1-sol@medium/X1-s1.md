### Define “better” before changing the model

Use these separate error classes:

| Class | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported claim | The source does not support a summary claim. | “Refund approved” when approval never occurred. |
| Material omission | The summary excludes a fact needed for the next support action. | It omits an unresolved account lock. |
| Unclear text | The facts appear, but their meaning or ownership is unclear. | It confuses the customer with the agent. |

These definitions are proposed review rules, not measured findings. [judgment]

Research on abstractive summaries documents unsupported content, so readable text alone does not establish factual accuracy. [2]
