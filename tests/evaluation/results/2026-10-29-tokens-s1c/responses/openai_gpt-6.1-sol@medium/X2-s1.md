### 2. Use the next retrospective to launch one bounded experiment

**Kind:** Stochastic, because the result depends on team behavior.  
**Domain:** Complex, meaning the result becomes known through a small trial.

**Acceptance test first:**
- **Metric:** Completed changes with an observed effect.
- **Baseline:** Establish the count from Step 1.
- **Sample:** One change over five working days.
- **Pass threshold:** Complete the change and review its chosen effect measure.

This tests whether the team can complete the cycle, not whether the change has lasting value. [judgment]

After Step 1, spend at most 45 minutes selecting one recurring problem. Choose one reversible change with:

- One owner who accepts responsibility.
- A task that takes at most two work hours.
- Reserved time, with a named commitment removed if necessary.
- One effect measure and a review date.

For example, test a shared handoff checklist and count missing handoff details.

Do not assign another action. Stop the trial if it creates harm or exceeds its time budget. The expected benefit remains a judgment until the trial produces evidence. [judgment]

If the action remains incomplete, identify the blocker without blaming the owner. Then reduce its scope, secure capacity, or escalate authority. [judgment]

If the action finishes, compare its effect with the starting measure. Keep, adjust, or stop it based on that result. This closes the cycle instead of creating another unchecked promise. [judgment]
