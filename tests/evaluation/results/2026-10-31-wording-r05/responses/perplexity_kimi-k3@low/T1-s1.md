The team disagrees about SQL layout. That disagreement appears in every review and creates friction [1]. The request is to fix the culture problem, not only pick a style.

## Relevance

Review time is scarce. Style debate consumes it and weakens trust. Automation removes low value dispute, and norms protect review purpose.
