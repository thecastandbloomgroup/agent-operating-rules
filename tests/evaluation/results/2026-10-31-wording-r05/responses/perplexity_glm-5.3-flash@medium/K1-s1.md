# Headline

Restore the connection pool size and the cache TTL from release 2026.10.21, because connection starvation in the pool causes both the latency and the errors.

- The delay sits in db.pool.acquire: p95 rose from 3 ms to 690 ms, and 690 ms plus the query and serialize spans matches the 900 ms total [1].
- The diff cut pool max_size from 50 to 8 while the service runs 32 workers on 4 pods [3].
- PoolTimeout causes 96% of window B errors, and query spans stayed flat, so queries are not the driver [1].

# Background

The API p95 rose from 120 ms to 900 ms after release 2026.10.21, deployed 2026-10-21 14:05 UTC [3]. The release changed the ORM layer, the cache TTL, and the pool settings. Logs, traces, and the diff are attached.

# Relevance

Error rate rose from 0.02% to 0.40%, and 96% of those requests return 503 after a 5 s wait [1]. Users see failures now, so the fix should deploy today.

# Information

**Evidence.** The material supports one driver, connection starvation.

- The diff reduced max_size from 50 to 8 and acquire_timeout_s from 30 to 5 [3].
- The deployment note states 4 pods with 8 worker threads each, 32 workers in total, unchanged in the release [3].
- Logs show every pod exhausted at 8 of 8 connections, with 6 to 14 waiters [2].
- Hourly pool exhausted warnings went from none on 2026-10-20 to 1800 to 2400 on 2026-10-22 [2].
- PoolTimeout causes 96% of window B errors, against an error rate of 0.40% [1].
- Peak DB connections from the service fell from 31 to 8, while DB CPU stayed at 38% to 41% [1].
- Traffic stayed flat: 410 rps peak in window A against 405 in window B [1].
- Query spans stayed flat: orders 52 to 58 ms p95, order_lines 30 to 26 ms p95 [1].
- The diff cut cache default_ttl_s from 300 s to 60 s [3].
- Cache miss rate rose from 12% to 31%, so queries run more often and hold connections longer [1].

Theme from qualitative evidence: connection starvation is the driver, supported by 3 sources [1] [2] [3]. No source disagrees; the flat query spans act as the counter-check, because they rule out the ORM change [1].

Confidence in the pool fix: high confidence, because three independent signals agree: config diff, warning counts, and the acquire span [1] [2] [3].
