## Information

IEEE book format is: A. B. Author, *Title*, Nth ed. City, State/Country: Publisher, year [13]. Rules applied per entry:

1. Forenames become initials before the surname ("Barbara Minto" becomes "B. Minto").
2. Two authors are joined with "and"; initials keep the "A. B." pattern for each author.
3. The title stays in title case and is set in italics in rendered text.
4. The edition moves after the title and uses the abbreviation "ed." ("3rd" becomes "3rd ed.").
5. The place comes before the publisher, split from the attached "Publisher" field, and ends with a comma.
6. The year ends the entry with a period.
