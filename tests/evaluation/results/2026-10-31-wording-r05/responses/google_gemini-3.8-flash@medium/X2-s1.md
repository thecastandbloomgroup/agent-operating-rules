### Headline

Limit each retrospective to one owned action item that enters the sprint backlog immediately.
First, teams abandon action items when they take on too many items at once [judgment].
Second, placing items into regular work tracking ensures visible progress [judgment].
Third, reviewing the prior item at the start of each session creates accountability [judgment].

### Background

Your team attends regular retrospective meetings as scheduled.
However, these meetings fail to generate observable changes in team operations.
The team needs a practical way to turn discussion into completed improvements.

### Relevance

Unproductive meetings cause disengagement and waste team capacity.
Fixing this process restores team agency before attendance declines further.

### Information

Retrospective decay typically occurs when teams do not track improvement items alongside regular delivery work [1].
Teams that select three or more goals per cycle often complete zero goals [judgment].
Therefore, limiting work in progress restores momentum [1].

**Assumption log**
A1. The team possesses authority to change its internal working agreements. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Check if past action items required external approval. If wrong: Reframe actions to target external escalation instead.
A2. The team creates too many unassigned action items during sessions. Why not measured: The material does not state it. Validate by: Audit action items from the past three retrospectives. If wrong: Focus on root causes of safety or motivation instead.

**Alternative considered**
Cancel retrospectives and collect feedback through an anonymous survey.
This alternative loses because surveys eliminate collaborative problem solving and reduce team ownership [judgment].

### End

I recommend that we take these actions:

1. Cap retrospective output at exactly one improvement item per sprint.
   - Increment: Establish a team policy that permits only one action item per retrospective.
   - Capability added: Focuses team effort on a single bottleneck instead of divided attention.
   - Cynefin domain: Clear, because work-in-progress limits are an established operational practice.
   - Deterministic test:
     Given the team concludes a retrospective,
     When they select action items,
     Then the total count of accepted items MUST be exactly one.

2. Place the single action item directly into the active sprint backlog.
   - Step 2 MUST NOT start until the team completes Step 1.
   - Increment: Create a delivery ticket for the action item and assign a single owner.
   - Blocker removed: Eliminates the separation between product delivery and process improvement.
   - Cynefin domain: Complicated, because integrating process work requires team capacity allocation.
   - Deterministic test:
     Given the team creates a retrospective action item,
     When planning the next sprint,
     Then the item exists on the active sprint board with an assigned owner.

3. Open every retrospective with a review of the previous action item.
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start until the team completes Step 2.
   - Increment: Reserve the first five minutes of each retrospective for an item review.
   - Blocker removed: Stops the team from generating new ideas before completing old commitments.
   - Cynefin domain: Clear, because a fixed agenda order follows established meeting standards [2].
   - Evaluative test:
     Evaluate the review agenda against this rubric:
     - Criterion: Agenda placement (weight: 50%, pass threshold: first item on agenda, evidence: meeting notes).
     - Criterion: Status resolution (weight: 50%, pass threshold: marked as done or adapted, evidence: ticket status).

4. Conduct a safe-to-fail probe on session format if action completion remains below 100%.
   - Step 4 runs only if Step 3 reveals that the item did not finish.
   - Increment: Run one retrospective focused solely on why process changes stall.
   - Capability added: Identifies hidden organizational friction without committing to major reorganizations.
   - Cynefin domain: Complex, because human team dynamics produce unpredictable interactions.
   - Stochastic test:
     Acceptance test metric: Team completion rate of the chosen action item.
     Baseline: 0% completion.
     Pass threshold: 100% completion within one sprint.
     Sample size: Two consecutive sprints.

### Follow-up

Conditions that change the recommendation:
If A1 is false, replace Step 1 with an escalation meeting with engineering management.
