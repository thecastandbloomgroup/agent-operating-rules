## Background

The team has three data warehouse proposals and a 400,000 budget over three years. The data is 14 TB today and grows about 3 TB per year [1]. The request is to recommend one vendor.

## Relevance

The vendors quote the cost of the system but not the cost of staff time or the price after year 3 [1]. A choice made on the quoted price alone hides these costs.
