## Headline

Measure the failure pattern first, then fix the largest cause of the flaky build.

- The material gives no logs, so the cause is unknown today.
- Four causes cover all flaky builds, and data from past runs separates them.

## Background

The request states that the build is flaky. That is, it passes and fails on the same code. The material has no logs, no tool name, and no failure rate.

## Relevance

Each false failure blocks a merge and costs a rerun. Without data, the team may fix the wrong cause.
