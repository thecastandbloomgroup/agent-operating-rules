## Headline

Name one decision owner now, check the facts against records, then give Legal, PR, and the board one shared brief.

- **Authority:** one owner ends the conflicting instructions.
- **Facts:** company records decide whether the statement was false, not the article.
- **Alignment:** one brief with scored options gives all three groups the same input.
- **Public line:** a short holding statement limits damage while the check runs.

## Background

A journalist published that the CEO's statement from yesterday (Saturday, October 31, 2026) was false. Legal, PR, and the board each send you separate guidance. No one owns the decision.

## Relevance

Each hour without one company line lets the article set the facts. Conflicting messages also raise the risk of a second inconsistent statement. [judgment]

## Information

**Evidence**

- Three groups give conflicting guidance. This comes from your message.
- A second inconsistent statement costs more than a delay of a few hours. [judgment]
- Work with no visible cause and effect needs stabilising first. This is the Chaotic domain in the Cynefin framework.

  [1]

- Confidence in this plan: low confidence. The basis is that the article, the statement, and the records are not available.

**Assumption log**

A1. The statement is unverified: not yet true or false. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: compare each claim to source records (Step 2). If wrong: if the statement is confirmed false, Step 3 offers only correction options.

A2. You can name an owner, or can ask the board chair to do so. Why not measured: the user has not answered. Validate by: send the board chair one message. If wrong: the board chair names the owner in Step 1.

A3. No legal disclosure deadline is tied to the statement. Why not measured: the material does not state it. Validate by: ask Legal one yes/no question. If wrong: the deadline overrides the timing in Step 3.

**Alternative considered**

The strongest alternative is an immediate public denial or retraction. It loses because the facts are unverified. A wrong second statement costs more than a delay of a few hours. [judgment]

## End

I recommend that we take these four steps, in this order:

1. **Stabilise (Chaotic: no group accepts a shared view of cause and effect).**
   - *Increment:* in the next hour, send one message to Legal, PR, the board chair, and the CEO. It names one decision owner, bans outside statements, and orders a legal hold (an order to keep all related records).
   - *Blocker removed:* conflicting instructions.
   - *Kind:* deterministic.
   - *Test:*

     [2]

     Given three groups send conflicting advice, When the owner message goes out, Then each group confirms the owner in writing and one hold notice exists.

2. **Verify (Complicated: records and experts can answer the question).**
   - *Increment:* Legal and one staff member who did not write the statement compare each claim, in the statement and in the article, to source records. Finish within 4 hours.
   - *Blocker removed:* no shared facts.
   - *Kind:* evaluative.
   - *Rubric:* record found for each claim, 40 points. Exact wording matches the record, 30. Second reviewer agrees, 20. Sources named in the article checked, 10. Pass threshold: 90 points.
   - *Evidence needed:* dated documents and the second reviewer's written sign-off.
   - Step 2 MUST NOT start until Step 1 passes its test.

3. **Align (Complicated: the options are known, and analysis picks among them).**
   - *Increment:* the owner issues a one-page brief. It scores four options (correct, clarify, stand by, no comment) in a Pugh matrix, with "stand by" as the baseline.

     [3]

   - *Blocker removed:* no shared basis for a decision.
   - *Kind:* evaluative.
   - *Rubric:* truthfulness, 40. Legal exposure, 30. Stakeholder trust, 20. Speed, 10.
   - *Pass threshold:* the chosen option beats the baseline on total score and does not score lower on truthfulness.
   - *Evidence needed:* Step 2 results, Legal's written opinion, and PR's coverage data.
   - Step 3 MUST NOT start until the test in Step 2 passes.

4. **Probe the public line (Complex: the reaction of the journalist and the public shows only afterward).**
   - *Increment:* send a 3-sentence holding statement to the journalist and to staff. It says the company is reviewing the claim and gives a time for the next update. It neither repeats nor defends any claim. Legal approves it first.
   - *Capability added:* control of the public tone while Steps 2 and 3 run.
   - *Parallel run:* start it with Step 2. It does not wait for Step 3.
   - *Kind:* stochastic.
   - *Test:*
     - Metric: the number of new outlets that repeat the claim as fact.
     - Baseline: the count when the statement goes out.
     - Pass threshold: growth of at most 50% by hour 24. [judgment]
     - Sample: all outlets in monitoring, checked at hours 6, 12, and 24 (three checks).
   - *Stop condition:* growth above 100% at hour 6. Then the owner starts Step 3 early with the facts found so far.
   - *Budget:* one hour of PR time and one draft.
   - *Leverage:* a guess, because the domain is Complex.
