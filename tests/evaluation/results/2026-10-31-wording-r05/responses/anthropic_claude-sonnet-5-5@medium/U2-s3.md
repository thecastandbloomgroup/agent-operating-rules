## Headline

Find the cause of the wrong numbers by checking the report in three separate layers, starting with a list of the figures that look wrong.

- The data layer: the source records are wrong or incomplete.
- The calculation layer: a formula or total is wrong.
- The definition layer: the period, scope, or metric definition differs from the one the reader expects.
